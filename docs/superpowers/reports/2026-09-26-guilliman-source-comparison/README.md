## September 30 verified history and terminology

Last verified: 2026-09-30. The official [June 4, 2025 Balance Dataslate announcement](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/bk58priy/the-warhammer-40000-balance-dataslate-june-2025/) states: “We have changed Guilliman’s Supreme Strategist ability to function once per battle round rather than per turn.” This confirms a deliberate frequency amendment, rather than an inference from two translations.

The [official 11th-edition English core rules](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_01-06_warhammer40k_new40k_core_rules-was6fbu1ix-hfewhmxyiy.pdf), page 28, sections 07.01–07.03, define a battle round as its start, both players' turns and its end. Each player takes one turn. The [official Simplified Chinese core rules](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/chi_01-06_warhammer40k_new40k_core_rules-gihrxgzhgo-iickazpeog.pdf) use 战斗轮次 for battle round and 玩家回合/回合 for a player's turn. The saved bilingual chapter is [core rules chapter 7](../../../../wiki/core-rules/sections/07-the-battle-round.md). The web fetch of the large English PDF exceeded the retrieval tool's size limit; the saved local chapter supplies the checked text. The PDF URL is the official source, not a claim of a successful fresh download on September 30.

Applied to Supreme Strategist's frequency alone, the previous wording could allow one activation in each player's turn, potentially two across a battle round. The amended wording permits one across that battle round, subject to the other conditions. It does not reset when the opposing player's turn starts. This practical interpretation follows the verified turn structure; it does not establish the community translator's intended use of 回合.
