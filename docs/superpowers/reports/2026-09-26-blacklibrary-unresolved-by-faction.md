## Guilliman language diagnosis

Roboute Guilliman (000000138) already has five Chinese ability entries in unit_zh_detail. The English ability table contains seven entries. web_api/entity_card.py::_abilities chooses the complete English list whenever the Chinese list is shorter. Black Library nests three selectable effects inside one Chinese entry, so entry counts do not establish semantic coverage. Guilliman has no official_rule_revisions row; that separate staleness guard is not the cause here.

The cached Chinese Supreme Strategist text says once per turn; the English canonical text says once per battle round. Its Chinese introduction also carries an older points value. The display behavior was diagnosed, not changed to bypass the guard. Remaining work: verified ability-by-ability mapping with explicit freshness and completeness checks, preserving official English where the community text disagrees.
