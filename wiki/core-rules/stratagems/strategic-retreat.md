0 CP、Shooting phase、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One unit from your army (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
