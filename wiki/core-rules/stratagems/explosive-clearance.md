1 CP、Shooting phase、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select one model in your unit that is equipped with a weapon with the [BLAST] ability. Until the end of the phase, when determining how many models are in the target unit for the purpose of that ability, include models that are not visible to the attacking model. In addition, attacks made with that weapon can be allocated to models that are not visible to the attacking model.
