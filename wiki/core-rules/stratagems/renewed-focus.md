0 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1 and re-roll a Wound roll of 1.
