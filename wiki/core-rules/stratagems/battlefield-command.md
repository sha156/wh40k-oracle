1 CP、Any phase、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start or end of any phase.

## 使用对象

One Leader unit from your army and one friendly Bodyguard unit within 6" of it that it could normally be attached to.

## 效果

Select one of your Leader unit’s Leader abilities. Until the start of your next Command phase, that Bodyguard unit is treated as being led by that Leader unit for the purposes of that Leader ability.

## 限制

Once you target a unit with this Stratagem, that unit cannot be targeted with it again until your next Command phase.
