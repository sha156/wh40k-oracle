1 CP、Any phase、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after you have made a Hit roll, a Wound roll, a Damage roll, a saving throw, an Advance roll, a Charge roll, a Desperate Escape test, a Hazardous test, or just after you have rolled the dice to determine the number of attacks made with a weapon, for an attack, model or unit from your army.

## 使用对象

That unit or model from your army.

## 效果

You re-roll that roll, test or saving throw.
