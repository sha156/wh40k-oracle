1 CP、Fight phase、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, when a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit from your army that is within Engagement Range of one or more Attached units is selected to fight.

## 使用对象

One [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model in your unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, all melee attacks made by that model have the [PRECISION] ability.
