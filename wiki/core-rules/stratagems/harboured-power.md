0 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [HAZARDOUS] ability and your choice of the [LETHAL HITS] or [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.
