1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement or Charge phase, just after an enemy unit is set up or when an enemy unit starts or ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move, or declares a charge.

## 使用对象

One unit from your army that is within 24" of that enemy unit and that would be eligible to shoot if it were your Shooting phase.

## 效果

If that enemy unit is visible to your unit, your unit can shoot that enemy unit as if it were your Shooting phase.

## 限制

You cannot target a [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] unit with this Stratagem. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 6 is required to score a hit, irrespective of the attacking weapon’s Ballistic Skill or any modifiers. You can only use this Stratagem once per turn.
