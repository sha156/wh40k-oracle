## 效果

While the bearer is leading a unit, ranged weapons equipped by models in that unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability. In addition, while the bearer’s unit is under the effects of the Devastator Doctrine, you can reroll Advance rolls made for that unit.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
