25 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/godhammer-assault-force.md|Godhammer Assault Force]] 分队。

## 效果

Add 2 to the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by the bearer. Each time a melee attack made by the bearer is allocated to an enemy model, if the bearer disembarked from a Transport this turn, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of that attack.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
