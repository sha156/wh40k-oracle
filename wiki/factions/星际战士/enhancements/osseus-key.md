15 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/black-spear-task-force.md|黑矛特遣队]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of your opponent’s Shooting phase, select one enemy VEHICLE unit (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] units) within 12" of and visible to the bearer. That unit must take a Leadership test. If that test is passed, until the end of the phase, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll; if that test is failed, that unit is not eligible to shoot this phase.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Watch Master or [[factions/星际战士/units/techmarine.md|Techmarine]] model only.
