20 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-beastslayer.md|Saga of the Beastslayer]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle, at the start of the Fight phase, if the bearer is leading a [[factions/星际战士/units/blood-claws.md|Blood Claws]] unit, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in that unit by 1.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Space Wolves model only.
