15 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/reclamation-force.md|Reclamation Force]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time the bearer’s unit declares a charge, if one or more targets of that charge are within range of an objective marker, you can re-roll the Charge roll.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
