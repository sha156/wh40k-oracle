25 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/black-spear-task-force.md|Black Spear Task Force]] 分队。

## 效果

Improve the Strength, Damage and Armour Penetration characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons by 1. At the end of the Fight phase, if one or more enemy models were destroyed as a result of a melee attack made by the bearer this phase, until the end of the battle, improve the Strength, Damage and Armour Penetration characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons by 2 instead.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
