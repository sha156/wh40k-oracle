## 效果

The first time the bearer is destroyed, at the end of the phase, roll one D6: on a 2+, set the bearer back up on the battlefield as close as possible to where it was destroyed and not within Engagement Range of any enemy units, with 3 wounds remaining.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Death Company model only.
