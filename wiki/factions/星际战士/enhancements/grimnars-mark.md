## 效果

Once per battle round, you can target this unit with the Rapid Ingress or Heroic Intervention stratagem, regardless of any other uses of that stratagem this phase. If you do:
- That use is -1 CP.
- That use does not prevent any uses of that stratagem on other units this phase.

**分数**：20 分
