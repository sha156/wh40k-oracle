30 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/black-spear-task-force.md|Black Spear Task Force]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle, after you have selected your Oath of Moment target, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, select a second enemy unit to be an Oath of Moment target.

**Designer’s Note:** *This means that each time a model with the Oath of Moment ability makes an attack that targets either of your Oath of Moment targets, you can re-roll the Hit roll.*

**分数**：30 分

## 携带限制

Watch Master or [[factions/星际战士/units/captain.md|Captain]] model only.
