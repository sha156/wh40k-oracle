## 效果

Improve the Strength and Armour Penetration characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons by 1. Each time the bearer ends a Charge move, until the end of the turn, improve the Strength and Armour Penetration characteristics of the bearers melee weapons by 2 instead.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS ASTARTES MOUNTED model only.
