## 效果

Add 2 to the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by the bearer. If the bearer’s unit has achieved one or more Boasts, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of those weapons as well.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Space Wolves model only.
