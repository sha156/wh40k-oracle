15 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/champions-of-fenris.md|Champions of Fenris]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) within Engagement Range of the bearer’s unit Falls Back, all models in that enemy unit must take Desperate Escape tests. When doing so, if that enemy unit is Battle-shocked, subtract 1 from each of those tests.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Adeptus Astartes Terminator model only.
