20 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-beastslayer.md|Saga of the Beastslayer]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, select up to three [[factions/星际战士/units/thunderwolf-cavalry.md|Thunderwolf Cavalry]], [[factions/星际战士/units/wulfen.md|Wulfen]] and/or [[factions/星际战士/units/blood-claws.md|Blood Claws]] units from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in Strategic Reserves if you wish, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
