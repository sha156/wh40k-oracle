15 分、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-bold.md|Saga of the Bold]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of the Fight phase, if there are more enemy models than friendly models wholly within 6" of the bearer, until the end of the phase, add 2 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by the bearer. If the bearer’s unit has achieved one or more Boasts, add 3 to the Attacks characteristic instead.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Space Wolves model only.
