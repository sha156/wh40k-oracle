1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/emperors-shield.md|Emperor’s Shield]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Terminator, [[factions/星际战士/units/bladeguard-veteran-squad.md|Bladeguard Veteran Squad]], [[factions/星际战士/units/sternguard-veteran-squad-2.md|Sternguard Veteran Squad]] or [[factions/星际战士/units/vanguard-veteran-squad.md|Vanguard Veteran Squad]] unit from your army within range of an objective marker you control.

## 效果

That objective marker remains under your control, even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the start or end of any phase.
