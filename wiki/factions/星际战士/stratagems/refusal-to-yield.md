1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/vindication-task-force.md|Vindication Task Force]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after an [[factions/星际战士/units/ancient.md|Ancient]] model from your army is destroyed.

## 使用对象

That ANCIENT model. You can use this Stratagem on that model even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

At the end of the phase, set your model back up on the battlefield, as close as possible to where it was destroyed and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, with its full wounds remaining.

## 限制

You cannot target the same model with this Stratagem more than once per battle.
