1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/lions-blade-task-force.md|Lion’s Blade Task Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Ravenwing unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Normal, Advance or Charge move, it can move horizontally through terrain features.
