1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/hammer-of-avernii.md|Hammer of Avernii]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Terminator, [[factions/星际战士/units/bladeguard-veteran-squad.md|Bladeguard Veteran Squad]], [[factions/星际战士/units/sternguard-veteran-squad-2.md|Sternguard Veteran Squad]] or [[factions/星际战士/units/vanguard-veteran-squad.md|Vanguard Veteran Squad]] unit from your army within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit, subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of that attack.
