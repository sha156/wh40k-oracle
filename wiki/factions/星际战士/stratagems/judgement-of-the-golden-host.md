1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/encarmine-speartip.md|Encarmine Speartip]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, when a friendly SANGUINARY GUARD unit from your army ends a charge move.

## 使用对象

That SANGUINARY GUARD unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit engaged with your unit. Roll one D6 for each model in your unit engaged with that enemy unit: for each 3+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
