1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-beastslayer.md|Saga of the Beastslayer]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, after your unit has shot, select one enemy [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]], MONSTER, or VEHICLE unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the start of your next Shooting phase, that unit is pinned. While a unit is pinned, subtract 2" from its Move characteristic and subtract 2 from Charge rolls made for it.
