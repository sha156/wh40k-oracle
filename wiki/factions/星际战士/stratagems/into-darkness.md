1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/shadowmark-talon.md|影标之爪]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two Phobos and/or [[factions/星际战士/units/scout-squad.md|Scout Squad]] units from your army, or one other Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army. You cannot target a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove those units from the battlefield and place them into Strategic Reserves.
