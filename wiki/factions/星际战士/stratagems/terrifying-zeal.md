1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/interrogation-conclave.md|Interrogation Conclave]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, when a friendly CHAPLAIN unit ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

That CHAPLAIN unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (excluding MONSTER and VEHICLE units) engaged with your unit. That enemy unit makes a Leadership roll. If that Leadership roll is failed, that enemy unit’s attacks have -1 to Hit rolls until the end of the turn.
