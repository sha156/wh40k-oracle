1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/reclamation-force.md|Reclamation Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army ends a Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back.
