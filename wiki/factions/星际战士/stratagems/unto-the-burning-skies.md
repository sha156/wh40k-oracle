1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/angelic-inheritors.md|Angelic Inheritors]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Jump Pack unit from your army. You cannot target a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, unless it is [[factions/星际战士/units/the-sanguinor.md|The Sanguinor]].

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
