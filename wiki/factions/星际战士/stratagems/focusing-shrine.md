1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/terminator-assault.md|Terminator Assault]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, at the start of the Reinforcements step.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Terminator unit from your army that is in Reserves.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, when setting up your unit on the battlefield using its Deep Strike ability, you can set it up unit anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy models (measure this distance as you would normally for the Deep Strike ability, ignoring Walls and closed Hatchways).

## 限制

Until the end of the turn, your unit is not eligible to declare a charge.
