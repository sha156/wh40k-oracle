1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-great-wolf.md|头狼之歌]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one Hunting Pack from the Master of Wolves Detachment rule. Until the start of your next Command phase, that Hunting Pack is active for your unit instead of any other Hunting Pack that is active, even if you have already selected that Hunting Pack this battle.
