1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-hunter.md|Saga of the Hunter]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

Two ADEPTUS ASTARTES units from your army within Engagement Range of that enemy unit, or one Space Wolves Beasts unit from your army within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in that enemy unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
