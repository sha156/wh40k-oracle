1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/firestorm-assault-force.md|Firestorm Assault Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES TRANSPORT unit from your army that has no models embarked within it, and one ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY unit from your army wholly within 6" of that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].

## 效果

Your [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] unit can embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].

## 限制

You cannot target an [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, that cannot normally embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]], or that disembarked from a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] this turn.
