1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/company-of-hunters.md|Company of Hunters]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Ravenwing Mounted unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets an INFANTRY CHARACTER or MOUNTED CHARACTER unit, add 1 to the Wound roll.
