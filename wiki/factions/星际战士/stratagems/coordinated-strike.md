1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-beastslayer.md|Saga of the Beastslayer]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Space Wolves unit from your army that is wholly within 9" of one or more battlefield edges and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
