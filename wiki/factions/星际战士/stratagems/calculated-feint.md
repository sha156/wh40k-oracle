1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/vanguard-spearhead.md|Vanguard Spearhead]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit declares a charge.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY unit from your army that was selected as a target of that charge.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to D6", or up to 6" instead if it is a PHOBOS or ScouT SQUAD unit.

## 限制

You cannot select a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
