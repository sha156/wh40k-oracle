1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/champions-of-fenris.md|Champions of Fenris]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Select either the [LETHAL HITS] or [SUSTAINED HITS 1] abilities. Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have that ability.
