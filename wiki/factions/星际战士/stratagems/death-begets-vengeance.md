1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/wrath-of-the-doomed.md|迷失者的怒火]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Any phase, when a friendly DEATH COMPANY unit is destroyed by an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That enemy unit.

## 效果

That enemy unit is hated until the end of the battle. While a unit is hated, friendly DEATH COMPANY units’ attacks that target that unit have +1 to wound rolls.
