1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-hunter.md|Saga of the Hunter]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

Two ADEPTUS ASTARTES units from your army (excluding Beasts) that have not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit visible to both of your units. Until the end of the phase, models in your units can only target that enemy unit (and only if it is an eligible target) and each time a model in one of your units makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1.
