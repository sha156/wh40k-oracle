1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/angelic-inheritors.md|Angelic Inheritors]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one non-[[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model in your unit. Until the start of your next Command phase, your model has the Character keyword.

**Designer’s Note:** *While in effect, your model’s unit is therefore a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit, meaning it can interact with the Legacy of the Angel Detachment rule, in addition to other rules that interact with [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] units.*
