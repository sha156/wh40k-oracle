1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-beastslayer.md|Saga of the Beastslayer]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/星际战士/units/thunderwolf-cavalry.md|Thunderwolf Cavalry]] unit from your army that has not been selected to move or declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Normal, Advance, Fall Back or Charge move, it can move through models (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] models) and sections of terrain features that are 4" or less in height. When doing so, it can move within Engagement Range of enemy models, but unless it is making a Charge move, it cannot end that move within Engagement Range of them.
