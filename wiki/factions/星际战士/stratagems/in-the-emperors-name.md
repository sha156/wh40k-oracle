1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/boarding-strike.md|Boarding Strike]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Battleline unit from your army that is within range of an objective marker and was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability.
