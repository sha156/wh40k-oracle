1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/pilum-strike-team.md|Pilum Strike Team]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army is selected to Fall Back.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit and roll one D6: on a 2-4, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound; on a 5+, that enemy unit suffers 2 mortal wounds.
