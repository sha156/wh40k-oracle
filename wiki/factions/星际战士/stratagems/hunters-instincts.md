1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/spearpoint-task-force.md|Spearpoint Task Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit or Adeptus Astartes Mounted unit from your army that is within 9" of that enemy unit. You cannot target a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to 6".
