2 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/stormlance-task-force.md|Stormlance Task Force]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES MOUNTED or ADEPTUS ASTARTES VEHICLE unit (excluding WALKERS) from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, if your unit Advances, do not make an Advance roll for it. Instead, until the end of the phase, add 6" to the Move characteristic of models in your unit, or 9" instead if your unit is [[core-rules/mounted.md|MOUNTED]].
