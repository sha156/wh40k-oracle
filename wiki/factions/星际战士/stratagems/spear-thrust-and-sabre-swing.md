1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/spearpoint-task-force.md|Spearpoint Task Force]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Select either the [LANCE] or [LETHAL HITS] ability. Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the selected ability. If it is a [[core-rules/mounted.md|MOUNTED]] unit, until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [LANCE] and [LETHAL HITS] abilities instead.
