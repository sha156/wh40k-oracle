2 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/vindication-task-force.md|Vindication Task Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that is within 6" of one or more enemy units and would be eligible to declare a charge against one or more of those enemy units if it were your Charge phase.

## 效果

Your unit now declares a charge that only targets one or more of those enemy units, and you resolve that charge.

## 限制

Note that even if this charge is successful, your unit does not receive any Charge bonus this turn.
