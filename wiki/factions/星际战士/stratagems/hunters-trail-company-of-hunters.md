1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/company-of-hunters.md|Company of Hunters]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Ravenwing Mounted unit from your army that is within range of an objective marker you control.

## 效果

That objective marker remains under your control, even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the start or end of any turn.
