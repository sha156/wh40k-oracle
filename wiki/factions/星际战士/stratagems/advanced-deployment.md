1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/armoured-speartip.md|Armoured Speartip]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Transport unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, units can disembark from your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] after it has Advanced. Units that do so count as having made a Normal move this phase, and cannot declare a charge in the same turn (unless your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] has the Assault Ramp ability), but can otherwise act normally.
