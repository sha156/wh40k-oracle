1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/hammer-of-avernii.md|艾沃尼之锤]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes [[factions/星际战士/units/dreadnought.md|Dreadnought]], Terminator, [[factions/星际战士/units/bladeguard-veteran-squad.md|Bladeguard Veteran Squad]], [[factions/星际战士/units/sternguard-veteran-squad-2.md|Sternguard Veteran Squad]] or [[factions/星际战士/units/vanguard-veteran-squad.md|Vanguard Veteran Squad]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll. If your unit is below Starting Strength, add 1 to the Wound roll as well.
