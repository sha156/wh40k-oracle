2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-bold.md|Saga of the Bold]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Space Wolves Character unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit, subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of that attack.
