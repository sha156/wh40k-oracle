1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-bold.md|Saga of the Bold]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/星际战士/units/wolf-guard.md|Wolf Guard]] Headtaker or Wolf Guard Terminator Pack Leader model from your army.

## 效果

Until the start of your next Command phase, your model has the [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] keyword.

**Designer’s Note:** *While in effect, your model’s unit is therefore a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit, meaning it can interact with the Heroes All rule, in addition to other rules that interact with [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] units.*
