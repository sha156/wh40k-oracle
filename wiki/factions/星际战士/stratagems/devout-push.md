1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/companions-of-vehemence.md|Companions of Vehemence]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Pile-in or Consolidation move, it can move up to 6" instead of up to 3".

## 限制

A unit cannot be targeted with this and the Hearts Hardened to Duty Stratagem in the same phase unless it has the [[factions/星际战士/units/chaplain.md|Chaplain]] or [[factions/星际战士/units/judiciar.md|Judiciar]] keywords.
