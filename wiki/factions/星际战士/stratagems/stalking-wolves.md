1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/champions-of-fenris.md|Champions of Fenris]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Stealth ability.
