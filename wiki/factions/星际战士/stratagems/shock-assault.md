1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/stormlance-task-force.md|Stormlance Task Force]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES MOUNTED unit from your army that has not declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, you can re-roll Charge rolls made for your unit and melee weapons equipped by models in that unit have the [LANCE] ability.
