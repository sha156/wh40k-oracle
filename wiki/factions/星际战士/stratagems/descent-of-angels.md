1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/the-angelic-host.md|The Angelic Host]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Jump Pack unit from your army that is arriving using the Deep Strike ability this phase.

## 效果

Your unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units.

## 限制

A unit targeted by this Stratagem is not eligible to declare a charge in the same turn.
