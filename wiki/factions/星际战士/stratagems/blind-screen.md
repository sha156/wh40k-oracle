1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/orbital-assault-force.md|轨道突袭部队]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit (excluding Titanic units) from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks and one friendly Adeptus Astartes Smoke Vehicle or [[factions/星际战士/units/drop-pod.md|Drop Pod]] unit within 9" of it.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your units have the Stealth ability and each time a ranged attack targets one of your units, models in that unit have the Benefit of Cover against that attack.
