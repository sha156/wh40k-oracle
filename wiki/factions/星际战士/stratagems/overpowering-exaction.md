1 CP、Command or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/lions-blade-task-force.md|Lion’s Blade Task Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Command phase or the start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit. That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test. When doing so, if your unit has the Deathwing or Ravenwing keyword, subtract 1 from the result.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle round.
