1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/the-angelic-host.md|The Angelic Host]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that was just destroyed. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Until the end of the battle, each time a friendly [[factions/星际战士/units/sanguinary-guard.md|Sanguinary Guard]] unit makes an attack that targets the enemy unit that just destroyed your unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.
