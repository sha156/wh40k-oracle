1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/the-lost-brethren.md|The Lost Brethren]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Death Company unit from your army, just before that unit Piles-in.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Pile-in move, it can move up to D3+3" instead of up to 3". If your unit is within 12" of one or more friendly [[factions/星际战士/units/chaplain.md|Chaplain]] models, or if it is below Starting Strength, it can move up to 6" instead. In either case, it can only do so provided your unit ends that Pile-in move in Unit Coherency and within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
