1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/godhammer-assault-force.md|Godhammer Assault Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/星际战士/units/land-raider.md|Land Raider]] model from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit embarked within your LAND RAIDER can disembark. When doing so, models in that unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 6" of your LAND RAIDER and can be set up within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
