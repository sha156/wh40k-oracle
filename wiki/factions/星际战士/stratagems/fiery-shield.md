1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/librarius-conclave.md|Librarius Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry or Adeptus Astartes Mounted unit from your army that is within 18" of one or more friendly Adeptus Astartes Psyker models, and that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll, and if the Pyromancy Discipline is active for your army, weapons that target your unit have the [HAZARDOUS] ability.
