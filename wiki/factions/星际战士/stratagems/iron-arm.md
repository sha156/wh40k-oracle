1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/librarius-conclave.md|Librarius Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army that is within 18" of one or more Adeptus Astartes Psyker models from your army and has not been selected to Fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit, or add 2 if the Biomancy Discipline is active for your army.
