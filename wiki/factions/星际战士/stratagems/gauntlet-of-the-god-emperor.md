1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/godhammer-assault-force.md|Godhammer Assault Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Vehicle model from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your model makes a Normal or Advance move, it can move horizontally through terrain features.
