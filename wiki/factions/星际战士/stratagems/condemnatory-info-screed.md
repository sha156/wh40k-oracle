1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/godhammer-assault-force.md|Godhammer Assault Force]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if it disembarked from a Transport this turn, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] has the [[factions/星际战士/units/land-raider.md|Land Raider]] keyword, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.
