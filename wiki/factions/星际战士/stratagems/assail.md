1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/librarius-conclave.md|Librarius Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Psyker unit from your army that is eligible to shoot.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within 18" of and visible to one or more [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] models in your unit (excluding units with the Lone Operative ability), and roll six D6, adding 1 to each result if the Telekinesis Discipline is active for your army: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
