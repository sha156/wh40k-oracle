1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/1st-company-task-force.md|1st Company Task Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR, BLADEGUARD VETERAN SQUAD, STERNGUARD VETERAN SQUAD or VANGUARD VETERAN SQUAD unit from your army within range of an objective marker you control.

## 效果

That objective marker remains under your control, even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the start or end of any turn.
