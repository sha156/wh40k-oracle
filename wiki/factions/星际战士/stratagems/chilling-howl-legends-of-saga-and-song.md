## 使用时机

Your opponent's Command phase.

## 使用对象

One friendly WOLF GUARD TERMINATORS unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within 6" of your unit. That enemy unit makes a battle-shock roll, with -1 to that battle-shock roll if that enemy unit is at or below half-strength.
