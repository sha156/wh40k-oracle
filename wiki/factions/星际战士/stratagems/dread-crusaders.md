1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/companions-of-vehemence.md|Companions of Vehemence]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit declares a charge.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army that was selected as one of the targets of that charge.

## 效果

That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from the result.
