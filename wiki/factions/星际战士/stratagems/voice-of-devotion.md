1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/wrathful-procession.md|Wrathful Procession]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry or Adeptus Astartes Mounted unit from your army.

## 效果

Select the Chorus of Relentless Hate, Rite of Perfervid Wrath or Chant of Deathless Devotion Litany. Until the end of the battle round, that Litany is active for your unit instead of any other Litany that is active for your army.
