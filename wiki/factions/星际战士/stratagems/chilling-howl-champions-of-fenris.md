1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/champions-of-fenris.md|Champions of Fenris]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Terminator unit from your army.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within 6" of your unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from that test if that unit is Below Half-strength.
