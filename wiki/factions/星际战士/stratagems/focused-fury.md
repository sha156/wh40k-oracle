1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/angelic-inheritors.md|Angelic Inheritors]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [LETHAL HITS] ability. If your unit is a Character unit, until the end of the phase, those weapons have the [LANCE] ability as well.
