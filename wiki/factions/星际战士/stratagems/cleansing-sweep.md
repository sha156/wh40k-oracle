1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/terminator-assault.md|Terminator Assault]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Movement or Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/星际战士/units/terminator-squad-2.md|Terminator Squad]] unit from your army that has not fired Overwatch this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack while firing Overwatch, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ is required to score a hit, instead of an unmodified 6.
