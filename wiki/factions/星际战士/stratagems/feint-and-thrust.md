1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/shadowmark-talon.md|影标之爪]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back. If it is a Phobos or [[factions/星际战士/units/scout-squad.md|Scout Squad]] unit, it is also eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced.
