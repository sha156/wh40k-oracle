1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/wrathful-procession.md|Wrathful Procession]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1 and you can ignore any or all modifiers to the following: that attack’s Weapon Skill characteristic; the Hit roll; the Wound roll.
