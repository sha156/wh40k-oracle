1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/shield-of-the-void.md|Shield of the Void]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select one of the following abilities: [LETHAL HITS], [IGNORES COVER] or [SUSTAINED HITS 1]. Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have that ability.
