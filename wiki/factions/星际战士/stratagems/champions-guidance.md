1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-bold.md|Saga of the Bold]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Space Wolves Character unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
