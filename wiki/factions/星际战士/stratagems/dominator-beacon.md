1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/hammer-of-avernii.md|Hammer of Avernii]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes [[factions/星际战士/units/dreadnought.md|Dreadnought]], Terminator, [[factions/星际战士/units/bladeguard-veteran-squad.md|Bladeguard Veteran Squad]], [[factions/星际战士/units/sternguard-veteran-squad-2.md|Sternguard Veteran Squad]] or [[factions/星际战士/units/vanguard-veteran-squad.md|Vanguard Veteran Squad]] unit from your army within range of an objective marker you control.

## 效果

That objective marker remains under your control, even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the end of a phase.
