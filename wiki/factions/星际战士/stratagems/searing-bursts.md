1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/dark-age-arsenal.md|黑暗时代兵器]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly HELLBLASTER SQUAD unit has shot.

## 使用对象

That HELLBLASTER SQUAD unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by your unit’s plasma ranged attacks. That enemy unit is seared until the start of your next turn. While a unit is seared, that unit has -2" Move.
