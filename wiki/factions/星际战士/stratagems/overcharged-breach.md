1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/shield-of-the-void.md|Shield of the Void]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Select one closed Hatchway within 9" of and visible to your unit. Open that Hatchway and, until the end of the battle, that Hatchway cannot be closed.

## 限制

You cannot use this Stratagem more than once.
