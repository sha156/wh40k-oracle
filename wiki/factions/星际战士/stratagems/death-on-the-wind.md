1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/company-of-hunters.md|Company of Hunters]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Ravenwing unit from your army that has just shot.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit that was hit by one or more of those attacks. That unit must take a Battle-shock test. When doing so, if one or more RAVENWING units from your army are within 6" of that enemy unit, subtract 1 from the test.
