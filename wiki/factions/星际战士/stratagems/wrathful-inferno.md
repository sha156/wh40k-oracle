1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/forgefathers-seekers.md|铸造之父的探寻者]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit from your army Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot in a turn in which it Fell Back.
