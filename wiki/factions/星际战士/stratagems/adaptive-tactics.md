1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/black-spear-task-force.md|Black Spear Task Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two Kill Team units from your army, or one other ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army.

## 效果

For each unit targeted, select Furor Tactics, Malleus Tactics or Purgatus Tactics. Until the start of your next Command phase, that Mission Tactic is active for that unit instead of any Mission Tactic that is active for your army.
