1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/bastion-task-force.md|堡垒特遣队]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an Adeptus Astartes Battleline unit from your army finished making its attacks.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS ASTARTES BATTLELINE unit.

## 效果

When an enemy unit is auspex scanned as a result of those attacks this turn, until the start of your next turn, it is suppressed. While a unit is suppressed, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
