1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/shadowmark-talon.md|Shadowmark Talon]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry or Adeptus Astartes Mounted unit from your army that is within 9" of the enemy unit that just ended that move. You cannot target a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to D6", or up to 6" instead if it is a Phobos or [[factions/星际战士/units/scout-squad.md|Scout Squad]] unit.
