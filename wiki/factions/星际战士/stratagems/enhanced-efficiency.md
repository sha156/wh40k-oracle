1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/pilum-strike-team.md|Pilum Strike Team]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit is selected to make a Normal or Advance move, it can attempt to operate one Hatchway it is within 1” of either at the start or end of that move. If it does, it cannot attempt to operate a Hatchway again this turn.
