1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/librarius-conclave.md|Librarius Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Psyker unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit that is within 18" of and visible to one [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] model in your unit. Until the start of your next turn, that enemy unit is pinned. While a unit is pinned, subtract 2 from that unit’s Move characteristic and subtract 2 from Charge rolls made for it. In addition, if the Telepathy Discipline is active for your army, that enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from the result.
