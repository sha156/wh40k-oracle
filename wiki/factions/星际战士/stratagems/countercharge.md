2 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-bold.md|Saga of the Bold]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Character unit from your army that is within 6" of one or more enemy units and would be eligible to declare a charge against one or more of those enemy units if it were your Charge phase.

## 效果

Your unit now declares a charge that targets only one or more of those enemy units, and you resolve that charge as if it were your Charge phase. Note that even if this charge is successful, your unit does not receive any Charge bonus this turn.
