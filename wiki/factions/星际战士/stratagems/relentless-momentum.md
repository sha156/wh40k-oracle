1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/wrathful-procession.md|Wrathful Procession]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase and is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, when determining which models in your unit are eligible to fight, any models in it that are within 3" of one or more enemy models are eligible to fight. When resolving those attacks, such models can target one of those enemy units that is within 3" of them and within Engagement Range of their unit.
