1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-great-wolf.md|Saga of the Great Wolf]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Mounted or Adeptus Astartes Walker unit from your army that has not been selected to move or charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Normal, Advance, Fall Back or Charge move, it can move horizontally through models (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] models) and terrain features. When doing so, it can move within Engagement Range of enemy models, but cannot end a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move within Engagement Range of them.
