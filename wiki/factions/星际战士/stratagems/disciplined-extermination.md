1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/emperors-shield.md|帝皇之盾]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Terminator, [[factions/星际战士/units/bladeguard-veteran-squad.md|Bladeguard Veteran Squad]], [[factions/星际战士/units/sternguard-veteran-squad-2.md|Sternguard Veteran Squad]] or [[factions/星际战士/units/vanguard-veteran-squad.md|Vanguard Veteran Squad]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [IGNORES COVER] ability and improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of such weapons by 1.
