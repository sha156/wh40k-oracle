1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/the-lost-brethren.md|The Lost Brethren]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Death Company unit from your army that is below Starting Strength and has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, improve the Attacks, Stength and Armour Penetration characteristics of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1 and, unless your unit is within 12" of one or more friendly [[factions/星际战士/units/chaplain.md|Chaplain]] models, until the end of the phase, those weapons have the [HAZARDOUS] ability.
