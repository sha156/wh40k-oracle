1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-beastslayer.md|Saga of the Beastslayer]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that is within 8" of that enemy unit and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to D6". If your unit has the Space Wolves Infantry or [[factions/星际战士/units/thunderwolf-cavalry.md|Thunderwolf Cavalry]] keywords, it can make a Normal move of up to 6" instead.
