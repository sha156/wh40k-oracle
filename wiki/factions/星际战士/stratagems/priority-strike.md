2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/ceramite-sentinels.md|Ceramite Sentinels]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Infantry or Adeptus Astartes Mounted unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]], MONSTER or VEHICLE unit, you can re-roll the Wound roll.
