1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/fulguris-task-force.md|Fulguris Task Force]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly SPEEDER unit is selected to shoot.

## 使用对象

That SPEEDER unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within 24" of your unit. That enemy unit has +6" detection range until your unit has shot.
