1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/vindication-task-force.md|Vindication Task Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit destroys an [[factions/星际战士/units/ancient.md|Ancient]] model from your army that has not been targeted with the Refusal to Yield Stratagem this phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army visible to that enemy unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the battle, each time an ADEPTUS ASTARTES model from your army makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, add 1 to the Hit roll.

## 限制

You cannot target that ANCIENT model with the Refusal to Yield Stratagem this phase.
