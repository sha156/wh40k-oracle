1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/terminator-assault.md|Terminator Assault]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start or end of any phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes Terminator unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of your next Command phase, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in your unit.

## 限制

Each time you use this Stratagem, you cannot use it again until after your next Command phase.
