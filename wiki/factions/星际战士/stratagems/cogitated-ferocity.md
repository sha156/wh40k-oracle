1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/hammer-of-avernii.md|Hammer of Avernii]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Astartes [[factions/星际战士/units/dreadnought.md|Dreadnought]], Terminator, [[factions/星际战士/units/bladeguard-veteran-squad.md|Bladeguard Veteran Squad]], [[factions/星际战士/units/sternguard-veteran-squad-2.md|Sternguard Veteran Squad]] or [[factions/星际战士/units/vanguard-veteran-squad.md|Vanguard Veteran Squad]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Select either the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] or [LETHAL HITS] abilities. Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the selected ability.
