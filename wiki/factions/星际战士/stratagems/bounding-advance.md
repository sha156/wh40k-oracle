1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-hunter.md|Saga of the Hunter]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Space Wolves Infantry or Space Wolves Beasts unit from your army that has not been selected to move or declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Normal, Advance, Fall Back or Charge move, it can move through models (excluding Titanic models). When doing so, it can move within Engagement Range of enemy models, but unless it is making a Charge move, it cannot end that move within Engagement Range of them.
