1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/marshals-household.md|Marshal’s Household]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, when a friendly SWORD BRETHREN SQUAD unit ends a charge move.

## 使用对象

That SWORD BRETHREN SQUAD unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit engaged with your unit. Roll one D6 for each model in your unit engaged with that enemy unit: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound (to a maximum of 6 mortal wounds).
