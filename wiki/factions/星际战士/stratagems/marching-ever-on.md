1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/reclamation-force.md|光复派遣军]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit Falls Back.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that was within Engagement Range of that enemy unit at the start of the phase.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to D6"+1.
