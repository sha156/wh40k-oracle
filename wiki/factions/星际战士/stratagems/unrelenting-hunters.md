1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星际战士/detachments/saga-of-the-great-wolf.md|Saga of the Great Wolf]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back. If your unit is a Space Wolves unit, until the end of the turn, it is eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced or Fell Back.
