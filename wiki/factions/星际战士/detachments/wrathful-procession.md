星际战士的分队，分队规则「不朽忠诚祷言」。

## 分队规则

### 不朽忠诚祷言 Chant of Deathless Devotion

Friendly CHAPLAIN units have a 5+ invulnerable save against ranged attacks.

RESTRICTIONS
Your army can include BLACK TEMPLARS units, but it cannot include any ADEPTUS ASTARTES units drawn from any other Chapter.
