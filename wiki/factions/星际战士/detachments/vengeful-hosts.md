星际战士的分队「Vengeful Hosts」，分队规则「Imperator Unleashed」。

## 分队规则

### Imperator Unleashed

In a turn a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES FLY INFANTRY unit made an ingress/charge move, that unit’s attacks can re-roll hit rolls of 1.
