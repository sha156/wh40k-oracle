星际战士的分队「Vindication Task Force」，分队规则「净化！圣化！」。

## 分队规则

### 净化！圣化！ Purge and Sanctify

- Each time an attack targets an [[factions/星际战士/units/ancient.md|Ancient]] unit from your army, if that unit is within range of one or more objective markers and the Strength characteristic of that attack is greater than the Toughness characteristic of that unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.
- Each time a friendly CRUSADER SQUAD unit makes a surge move, instead of selecting a surge target, you can select the closest objective to that unit. When that unit makes that surge move, each model in that unit must end that surge move as close as possible to that objective instead.

RESTRICTIONS
Your army can include Black Templars units, but it cannot include any ADEPTUS ASTARTES units drawn from any other Chapter.
