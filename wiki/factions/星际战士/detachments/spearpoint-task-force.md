星际战士的分队「Spearpoint Task Force」，分队规则「Storm-swift Onslaught」。

## 分队规则

> 结构库中本分队名下有 3 条分队规则，以下全部列出。

### Storm-swift Onslaught

ADEPTUS ASTARTES units from your army are eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which they Advanced or Fell Back.

### Wrath of the First Khan

At the end of the Fight phase, if a [[factions/星际战士/units/suboden-khan.md|Suboden Khan]] unit from your army destroyed one or more enemy units this phase and is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, that unit can make a Normal move of up to 6".

### Restrictions

Your army can include White Scars units, but it cannot include any ADEPTUS ASTARTES units drawn from any other Chapter.
