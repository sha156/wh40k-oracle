1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/brandfast-oathband.md|Brandfast Oathband]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/hekaton-land-fortress.md|Hekaton Land Fortress]] unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit makes a Normal or Advance move, it can move horizontally through terrain features.
