## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly EINHYR HEARTHGUARD unit is selected to shoot.

That EINHYR HEARTHGUARD unit.

Your unit’s ranged attacks have +1 S. If you spend 1YP, your unit’s ranged attacks have [SUSTAINED HITS 1] as well.
