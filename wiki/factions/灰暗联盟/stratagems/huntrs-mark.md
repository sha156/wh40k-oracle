1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/needgaârd-oathband.md|Needgaârd Oathband]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1 and re-roll a Wound roll of 1.
