1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/brandfast-oathband.md|Brandfast Oathband]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, just after a Leagues of Votann Vehicle unit from your army has shot.

## 使用对象

That LEAGUES OF VOTANN VEHICLE unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the end of the phase, each time a Leagues of Votann Infantry model from your army makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, re-roll a Hit roll of 1.
