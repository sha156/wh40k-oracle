1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/persecution-prospect.md|Persecution Prospect]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if that attack targets an assailed unit and/or if your unit has the Hernkyn keyword, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
