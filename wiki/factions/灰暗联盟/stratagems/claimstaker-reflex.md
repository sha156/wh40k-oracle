1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/persecution-prospect.md|Persecution Prospect]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army (excluding Artillery and Vehicle units) that is within 8" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Each time you use this Stratagem, you can spend 2YP. Your unit can make a Normal move of up to D6". If you spent YP during this use of this Stratagem, your unit can make a Normal move of up to 6" instead.

## 限制

A unit cannot embark within a Transport as part of this move.
