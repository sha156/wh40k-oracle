1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/persecution-prospect.md|Persecution Prospect]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Hernkyn unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, if your unit Advances, do not make an Advance roll for it. Instead, until the end of the phase, add 6" to the Move characteristic of models in your unit.
