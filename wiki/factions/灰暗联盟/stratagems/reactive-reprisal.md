2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/needgaârd-oathband.md|Needgaârd Oathband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot, if units from your army have Fortify Takeover.

## 使用对象

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Your unit can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase, can only target that enemy unit when doing so, and can only do so if that enemy unit is an eligible target.
