1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/persecution-prospect.md|Persecution Prospect]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of any phase, if units from your army have Fortify Takeover.

## 使用对象

One Leagues of Votann Character unit from your army.

## 效果

Each time you use this Stratagem, you can spend any number of YP. Once you have done so, if you have 6 or fewer YP, until the start of your next turn, units from your army have Hostile Acquisition instead.
