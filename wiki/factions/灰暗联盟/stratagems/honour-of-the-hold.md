1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/needgaârd-oathband.md|Needgaârd Oathband]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Each time you use this Stratagem, you can spend 3YP. Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a melee attack that targets that enemy unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1. If you spent YP during this use of this Stratagem, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 2 instead.
