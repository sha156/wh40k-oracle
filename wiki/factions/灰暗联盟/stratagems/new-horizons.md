1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/mercenary-oathband.md|Mercenary Oathband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Leagues of Votann Infantry unit from your army that is not within Engagement range of one or more enemy units and one friendly Transport it is able to embark within.

## 效果

If your LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit is wholly within 6" of that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]], it can embark within it.
