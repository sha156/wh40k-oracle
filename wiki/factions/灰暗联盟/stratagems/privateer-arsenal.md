1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/mercenary-oathband.md|佣兵铁誓团]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Leagues of Votann Infantry unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Each time you use this Stratagem, you can spend 3YP. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1 and re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If you spent YP during this usage of this Stratagem, you can re-roll the Hit roll and re-roll a Wound roll of 1 instead.
