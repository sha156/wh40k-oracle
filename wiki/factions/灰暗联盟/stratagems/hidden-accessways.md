1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/dêlve-assault-shift.md|Dêlve Assault Shift]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/cthonian-beserks.md|Cthonian Beserks]], [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/hearthkyn-warriors.md|Hearthkyn Warriors]] or [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/hernkyn-yaegirs.md|Hernkyn Yaegirs]] unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
