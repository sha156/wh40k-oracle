1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/hearthband.md|炉心战队]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/kâhl.md|Kâhl]], [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/ûthar-the-destined.md|Ûthar the Destined]] or [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/einhyr-hearthguard.md|Einhyr Hearthguard]] unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
