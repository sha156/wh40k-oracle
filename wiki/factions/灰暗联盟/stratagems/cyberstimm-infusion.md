1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/dêlve-assault-shift.md|Dêlve Assault Shift]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/cthonian-beserks.md|Cthonian Beserks]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Each time you use this Stratagem, you can spend 2YP. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If you spent YP during this use of this Stratagem, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.
