2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/brandfast-oathband.md|Brandfast Oathband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One Leagues of Votann Infantry unit that was hit by one or more of those attacks.

## 效果

Each time you use this Stratagem, you can spend 2YP. Select one friendly Kapricus or [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/sagitaur.md|Sagitaur]] unit within 6" of your [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] unit. If you spent YP during this use of this Stratagem, you can select one friendly [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/hekaton-land-fortress.md|Hekaton Land Fortress]] unit within 6" of your [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] unit instead. The selected unit can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase. When doing so, models in the selected unit can only target that enemy unit (and only if it is an eligible target).
