1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/brandfast-oathband.md|Brandfast Oathband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of any of your phases, if units from your army have Hostile Acquisition.

## 使用对象

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN TRANSPORT unit from your army.

## 效果

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit embarked within your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] can disembark. When doing so, models in that unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 6" of your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]]. That unit cannot declare a charge in the same turn, but can otherwise act normally in the remainder of the turn.
