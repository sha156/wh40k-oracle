1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/hearthguard-covenant.md|Hearthguard Covenant]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, when a friendly EINHYR HEARTHGUARD unit with Deep Strike is selected to make an ingress move.

## 使用对象

That EINHYR HEARTHGUARD unit.

## 效果

Your unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally from all enemy units, even if that is within your opponent’s deployment zone. Your unit is not eligible to declare a charge until the end of the turn.
