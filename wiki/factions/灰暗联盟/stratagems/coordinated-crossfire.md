## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two friendly SAGITAUR units.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit. Your units’ ranged attacks that target that enemy unit can re-roll Hit rolls of 1 and re-roll Wound rolls of 1.
