1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/armoured-trailblazers.md|装甲先驱]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly SAGITAUR unit.

## 使用对象

That SAGITAUR unit.

## 效果

Ranged attacks that target your unit with a Strength characteristic greater than your unit’s Toughness characteristic have -1 to Wound rolls.
