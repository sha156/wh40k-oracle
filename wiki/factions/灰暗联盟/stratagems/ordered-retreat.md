1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/needgaârd-oathband.md|Needgaârd Oathband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back.
