1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/mercenary-oathband.md|Mercenary Oathband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Leagues of Votann Infantry or Leagues of Votann Mounted unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [PRECISION] ability.
