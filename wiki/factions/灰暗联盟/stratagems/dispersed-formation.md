1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/persecution-prospect.md|Persecution Prospect]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Leagues of Votann Infantry or Leagues of Votann Mounted unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your unit has the Stealth ability and each time a ranged attack targets your unit, models in your unit have the Benefit of Cover against that attack.
