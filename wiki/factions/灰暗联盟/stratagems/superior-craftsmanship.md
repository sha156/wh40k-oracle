2 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/hearthband.md|Hearthband]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets a MONSTER or VEHICLE unit, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of that attack.
