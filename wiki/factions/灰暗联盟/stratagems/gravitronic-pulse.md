1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/hearthfire-strike.md|Hearthfire Strike]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, just after a LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit from your army ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

That LEAGUES OF VOTANN unit.

## 效果

For each enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit, until the end of the turn, that unit loses the Fights First ability (if it has it) and, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
