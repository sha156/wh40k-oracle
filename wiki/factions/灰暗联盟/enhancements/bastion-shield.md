25 分、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/hearthband.md|炉心战队]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time a ranged attack targets the bearer’s unit, if the attacking model is within 12" of the bearer’s unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1. If you spend 1YP, until the end of the phase, if the attacking model is within 18" of the bearer’s unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1 instead.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

LEAGUES OF VOTANN model only.
