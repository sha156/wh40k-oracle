## 效果

In your Shooting phase, after the bearer has shot, select one enemy unit (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] units) hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the start of your next turn, that enemy unit is suppressed. While a unit is suppressed, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.

**分数**：15 分
