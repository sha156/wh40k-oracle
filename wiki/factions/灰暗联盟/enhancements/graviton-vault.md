## 效果

In your Shooting phase, after the bearer has shot, and in the Fight phase, after the bearer has fought, select one enemy MONSTER or VEHICLE unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the start of your next turn, that enemy unit is suppressed. While a unit is suppressed, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.

**分数**：5 分

## 携带限制

Iron-master model only.
