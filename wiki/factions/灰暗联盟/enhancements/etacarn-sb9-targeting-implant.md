## 效果

Each time a model in the bearer’s unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1. Each time the bearer’s unit is selected to shoot or fight, you can spend 3YP. If you do, until the end of the phase, each time a model in the bearer’s unit makes an attack, that attack has the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.
