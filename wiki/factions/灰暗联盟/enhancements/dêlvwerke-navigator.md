25 分、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/dêlve-assault-shift.md|Dêlve Assault Shift]] 分队。

## 效果

In the Reinforcements step of your Movement phase, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, select one friendly [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/cthonian-beserks.md|Cthonian Beserks]] unit visible to the bearer and then spend any number of YP. Return one destroyed model to that unit and then, for every 2YP you just spent, return up to one additional destroyed model to that unit.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

LEAGUES OF VOTANN model only.
