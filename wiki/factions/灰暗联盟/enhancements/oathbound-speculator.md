## 效果

Each time a model in the bearer’s unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. Each time the bearer’s unit is selected to shoot or fight, you can spend 3YP. If you do, until the end of the phase, each time a model in the bearer’s unit makes an attack, add 1 to the Wound roll.

**分数**：30 分

## 携带限制

LEAGUES OF VOTANN model only.
