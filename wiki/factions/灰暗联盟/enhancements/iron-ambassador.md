5 分、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/needgaârd-oathband.md|Needgaârd Oathband]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle, when the bearer’s unit is selected to shoot, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, spend up to 3YP and, until the end of the phase, add the number of YP you just spent to the Damage characteristic of the bearer’s ranged weapons.

**分数**：5 分

## 携带限制

LEAGUES OF VOTANN model equipped with an Autoch-pattern combi-bolter only.
