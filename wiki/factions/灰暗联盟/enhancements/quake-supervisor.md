## 效果

While the bearer is within 3" of one or more friendly Leagues of Votann Artillery units, the bearer has the Lone Operative ability, and each time one of those ARTILLERY units makes a ranged attack that targets an enemy unit visible to the bearer, add 1 to the Hit roll.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

LEAGUES OF VOTANN model only.
