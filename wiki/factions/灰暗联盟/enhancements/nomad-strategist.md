20 分、[[factions/灰暗联盟/detachments/persecution-prospect.md|Persecution Prospect]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle, at the end of your opponent’s Fight phase, if the bearer is on the battlefield, it can use this Enhancement. If it does, you can spend up to 4YP. Select one Hernkyn unit from your army, and, for every 2YP you just spent, select up to one additional HERNKYN unit from your army. You can remove those units from the battlefield and place them into Strategic Reserves. You cannot select a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units (excluding assailed units).

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

LEAGUES OF VOTANN model only.
