## 效果

Each time the bearer’s unit is selected to fight, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, spend up to 2YP and, until the end of the phase, add the number of YP you just spent to the Damage characteristic of the bearer’s melee weapons.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

LEAGUES OF VOTANN model only.
