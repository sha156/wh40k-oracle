灰暗联盟的分队「Brandfast Oathband」，分队规则「Mobile Sensor Relays」。

## 分队规则

### Mobile Sensor Relays

Leagues of Votann Transport units from your army have the following ability:

**Firebase Control (Aura):** While a friendly Leagues of Votann Infantry unit is wholly within 6" of this [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]], ranged weapons equipped by models in that [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] unit have the [sustained hits 1] ability.
