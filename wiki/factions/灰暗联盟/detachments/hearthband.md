灰暗联盟的分队，分队规则「Methodical Annihilation」。

## 分队规则

### Methodical Annihilation

Each time a Leagues of Votann model from your army makes an attack with a weapon that targets the closest eligible target or a target that is within Engagement Range of that model’s unit:

- Re-roll a Wound roll of 1.
- If your unit is a [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/kâhl.md|Kâhl]], [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/einhyr-hearthguard.md|Einhyr Hearthguard]] or [[factions/灰暗联盟/units/ûthar-the-destined.md|Ûthar the Destined]] unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
