混沌星际战士的分队，分队规则「Fanatical Onslaught」。

## 分队规则

### Fanatical Onslaught

In your opponent’s Shooting phase, each time an enemy unit has shot, if one or more models from one or more HERETIC ASTARTES units from your army were destroyed as a result of those attacks, each such unit can make a Rush move. To do so, that unit can be moved up to 3", but must end that move as close as possible to the attacking unit. When doing so, those models can be moved within Engagement Range of enemy units. A unit cannot make a Rush move while it is Battle-shocked, and can only make one Rush move per turn.
