15 分、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/pactbound-zealots.md|Pactbound Zealots]] 分队。

## 效果

Add 1 to the Attacks and Strength characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons. Each time the bearer’s unit makes a Dark Pact and does not fail the resulting Leadership test, roll one D3: until the end of the phase, add the result to the Attacks and Strength characteristics of the bearers melee weapons instead.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Heretic Astartes Khorne model only.
