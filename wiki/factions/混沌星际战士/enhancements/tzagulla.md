25 分、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/warpstrike-champions.md|Warpstrike Champions]] 分队。

## 效果

Improve the Attacks, Strength and Armour Penetration characteristics of the bearer’s weapons by 1. In addition, each time the bearer’s unit is set up on the battlefield from Reserves, until the end of the turn, improve the Damage characteristic of the bearer’s weapons by 1.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

HERETIC ASTARTES model with the Deep Strike ability only.
