15 分、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/chaos-cult.md|Chaos Cult]] 分队。

## 效果

While the bearer’s unit is below its Starting Strength, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by DAMNED models in that unit, and while that unit is Below Half-strength, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of those weapons as well.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

DARK APOSTLE or DAMNED model only.
