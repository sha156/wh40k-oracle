20 分、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/chaos-cult.md|Chaos Cult]] 分队。

## 效果

If every other model in the bearer’s unit (excluding Dark Disciples) is DAMNED, you can re-roll Advance and Charge rolls made for the bearer’s unit.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

DARK APOSTLE or DAMNED model only.
