1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/fellhammer-siege-host.md|Fellhammer Siege-host]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES INFANTRY unit from your army (excluding DAMNED units) that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a melee attack is allocated to your unit, after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, roll one D6 (to maximum of six D6 per attacking unit): for each 4+, the attacking unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
