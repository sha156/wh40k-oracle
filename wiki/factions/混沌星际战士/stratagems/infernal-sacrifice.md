1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/chaos-cult.md|Chaos Cult]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One DAMNED unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Desperate Pact. If it does, your unit suffers D3 mortal wounds (in addition to any suffered for failing the resulting Leadership test), and until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of the melee weapons equipped by models in your unit, and if your unit did not fail the resulting Leadership test when making that Desperate Pact, until the end of the phase, improve the Strength characteristic of those weapons by 1 as well.
