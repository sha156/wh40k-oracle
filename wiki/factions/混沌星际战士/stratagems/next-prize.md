1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/infernal-reavers.md|Infernal Reavers]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase, just after a HERETIC ASTARTES unit from your army has Secured an objective marker, or your Movement phase, just after a HERETIC ASTARTES unit from your army has opened a Hatchway.

## 使用对象

That HERETIC ASTARTES unit.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to 3". If this Stratagem is used in your Movement phase, at least one model from your unit must end that move on the opposite side of the Hatchway from the one it started the phase on.
