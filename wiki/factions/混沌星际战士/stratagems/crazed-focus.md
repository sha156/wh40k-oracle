1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/chaos-cult.md|Chaos Cult]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One DAMNED unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Desperate Pact. If it does, until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1, and if your unit did not fail the resulting Leadership test when making that Desperate Pact, improve the Strength characteristic of that attack by 1 as well.
