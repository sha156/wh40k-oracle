1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/cult-of-the-arkifane.md|造物者教派]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Soul Forge unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
