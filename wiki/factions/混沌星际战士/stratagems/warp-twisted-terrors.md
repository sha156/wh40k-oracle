1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/murdertalon-raiders.md|Murdertalon Raiders]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, when a friendly WARP TALONS unit ends a move.

## 使用对象

That WARP TALONS unit.

## 效果

Select one visible enemy unit (excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE units) within 9" of your unit. That enemy unit makes a battle-shock roll, with -1 to that battle-shock roll.
