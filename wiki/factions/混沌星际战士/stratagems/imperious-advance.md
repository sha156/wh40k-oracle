1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/champions-of-chaos.md|Champions of Chaos]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One HERETIc ASTARTES TERMINATOR unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Select any number of models in your unit. Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by the selected models, and each time you select targets for attacks made by those models, they must target an enemy unit that is wholly on the opposite side of a Hatchway from them.
