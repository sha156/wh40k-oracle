1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/infernal-reavers.md|Infernal Reavers]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES unit from your army (excluding DAMNED units) that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is less than or equal to the Toughness characteristic of the target, add 1 to the Wound roll.
