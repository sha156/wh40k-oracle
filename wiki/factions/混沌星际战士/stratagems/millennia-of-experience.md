1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/veterans-of-the-long-war.md|Veterans of the Long War]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES INFANTRY or HERETIC ASTARTES MOUNTED unit from your army (excluding DAMNED units) that is within 8" of that enemy unit and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to 6".
