1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/devotees-of-destruction.md|毁灭崇拜者]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly HAVOCS/OBLITERATORS unit.

## 使用对象

That HAVOCS/OBLITERATORS unit.

## 效果

Attacks that target your unit with a S greater than your unit’s T have -1 to wound rolls.
