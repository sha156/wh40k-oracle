1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/cabal-of-chaos.md|Cabal of Chaos]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Heretic Astartes Psyker unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one visible enemy unit within 12" of your unit. Roll one D6: on a 1, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound; on a 2-4, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 5-6, that enemy unit suffers 2D3 mortal wounds.
