2 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/creations-of-bile.md|Creations of Bile]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Heretic Astartes Infantry unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a melee attack that targets a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit, you can re-roll the Wound roll. After your unit has fought, if one or more enemy [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] models were destroyed as a result of those attacks, select one enemy unit within 6" of your unit. That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test. If the enemy WARLORD was destroyed as a result of those attacks, each enemy unit within 6" of your unit must take a Battle-shock test instead.
