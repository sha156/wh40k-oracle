1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/dread-talons.md|Dread Talons]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Reinforcements step of your Movement phase, from the second battle round onwards.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES JUMP PACK unit from your army that is in Reserves.

## 效果

Set your unit up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units, but until the end of the turn, it is not eligible to declare a charge. Then select one enemy [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] or [[core-rules/mounted.md|MOUNTED]] unit within 9" of and visible to your unit: that unit makes a battle-shock roll.
