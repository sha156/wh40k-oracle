1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/warpstrike-champions.md|Warpstrike Champions]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Heretic Astartes Terminator, [[factions/混沌星际战士/units/obliterators.md|Obliterators]] or [[factions/混沌星际战士/units/mutilators.md|Mutilators]] unit from your army, within range of an objective marker you control.

## 效果

That objective marker remains under your control until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase.
