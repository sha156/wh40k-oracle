1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/underdeck-uprising.md|Underdeck Uprising]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after a DAMNED unit from your army makes a Dark Pact.

## 使用对象

That DAMNED unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] ability.
