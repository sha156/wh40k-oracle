1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/soulforged-warpack.md|Soulforged Warpack]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES DAEMON VEHICLE unit from your army (excluding Titanic units) that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, if your unit invokes its contract, each time it makes an attack that destroys an enemy model, roll one D6: on 5+, your unit regains 1 lost wound after all of its attacks have been resolved (to a maximum of 6 wound).
