1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/renegade-raiders.md|Renegade Raiders]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES unit from your army that was eligible to fight this phase.

## 效果

If your unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move of up to 6", or up to 12" if it is a MOUNTED unit. Otherwise, your unit can make a Fall Back move. It cannot embark within a TRANSPORT at the end of this move if it disembarked from a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] this turn.
