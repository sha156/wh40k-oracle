1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/underdeck-uprising.md|Underdeck Uprising]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One CULTIST MOB or DAMNED CHARAcTER unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase and is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Select one closed Hatchway within 1“ of your unit. Your unit can attempt to operate that Hatchway.

## 限制

Until the end of the turn, your unit cannot attempt to operate a Hatchway again.
