1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/chaos-cult.md|Chaos Cult]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One DAMNED unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the attacking unit has resolved all of its attacks, each time a model in your unit is destroyed, roll one D6 on a 6, the attacking unit suffers 1 mortal wound after all of its attacks have been resolved.
