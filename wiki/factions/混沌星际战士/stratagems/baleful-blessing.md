1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/cabal-of-chaos.md|Cabal of Chaos]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after a Heretic Astartes unit from your army has a mortal wound allocated to it.

## 使用对象

That HERETIC ASTARTES unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability against mortal wounds.
