1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/creations-of-bile.md|拜尔造物]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Heretic Astartes Infantry unit (excluding Damned units) from your army.

## 效果

One destroyed model (excluding Character models) is returned to your unit. If your unit is a Battleline unit, D3 destroyed models (excluding [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] models) are returned to your unit instead.
