1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/champions-of-chaos.md|Champions of Chaos]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit takes a Leadership test for the Dark Pacts ability, subtract 1 from the result and, if passed, select one objective marker within 6" of your unit. If there are no enemy units within range of that objective marker (excluding Battle-shocked units], that objective marker is Secured by your army.
