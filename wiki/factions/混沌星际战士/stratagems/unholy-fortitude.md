1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/cult-of-the-arkifane.md|Cult of the Arkifane]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Soul Forge unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Toughness characteristic of models in your unit.
