## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Heretic Astartes Terminator, [[factions/混沌星际战士/units/obliterators.md|Obliterators]] or [[factions/混沌星际战士/units/mutilators.md|Mutilators]] unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced.
