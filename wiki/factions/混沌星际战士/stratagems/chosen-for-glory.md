1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/chaos-cult.md|Chaos Cult]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One DAMNED unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Desperate Pact. If it does, until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if your unit did not fail the resulting Leadership test when making that Desperate Pact, you can re-roll the Wound roll as well.
