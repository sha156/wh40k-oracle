1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/cult-of-the-arkifane.md|造物者教派]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES unit from your army (excluding Damned units) that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, if your unit is selected to make a Dark Pact, you can select both abilities for that unit’s weapons to gain.
