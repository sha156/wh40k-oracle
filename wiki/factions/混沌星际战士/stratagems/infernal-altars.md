1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/underdeck-uprising.md|Underdeck Uprising]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One DAMNED unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit's attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, if your unit is within range of an objective marker, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability against that attack. If your unit has the CULTIST MOB keyword, models in it have the Feel No Pain 4+ ability against that attack instead.
