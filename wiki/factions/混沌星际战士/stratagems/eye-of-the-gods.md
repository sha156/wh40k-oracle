1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/pactbound-zealots.md|Pactbound Zealots]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a HERETIC ASTARTES CHARACTER unit from your army (excluding DAMNED, DAEMON and EPIC HERO units) destroys a enemy unit.

## 使用对象

One HERETIC ASTARTES CHARACTER model in that unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the battle, add 1 to the Move, Toughness and Wounds characteristics of that [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model, and add 1 to the Attacks. Strength and Damage characteristics of that [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model’s melee weapons.
