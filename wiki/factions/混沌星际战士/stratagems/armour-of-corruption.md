2 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/warpstrike-champions.md|次元突袭勇士]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Heretic Astartes Terminator, [[factions/混沌星际战士/units/obliterators.md|Obliterators]] or [[factions/混沌星际战士/units/mutilators.md|Mutilators]] unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit, subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of that attack.

## 限制

You cannot target the same unit with the Armour of Corruption and Empyric Dislocation Stratagems in the same phase.
