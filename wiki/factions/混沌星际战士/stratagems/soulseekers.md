1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌星际战士/detachments/cabal-of-chaos.md|Cabal of Chaos]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Heretic Astartes unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot that phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [IGNORES COVER] ability.
