泰伦虫族的分队，分队规则「Leader-beasts」。

## 分队规则

### Leader-beasts

Friendly Tyranid Warriors with Ranged Bio-weapons and Tyranid Warriors with Melee Bio-weapons units have the TYRANID WARRIORS and [[core-rules/battleline.md|BATTLELINE]] keywords.

TYRANID WARRIORS, [[factions/泰伦虫族/units/tyranid-prime-with-lash-whip.md|Tyranid Prime with Lash Whip]] and Winged Tyranid Prime models from your army have a 5+ invulnerable save.
