泰伦虫族的分队「Tyranid Attack」，分队规则「Xeno-Terror」。

## 分队规则

### Xeno-Terror

When you unleash the Shadow in the Warp, subtract 1 from the Battle-shock test each enemy unit on the battlefield must take as a result. For each enemy unit that fails one of those tests, that enemy unit cannot Fire Overwatch this turn.
