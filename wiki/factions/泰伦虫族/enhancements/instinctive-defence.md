## 效果

While the bearer is within 6" of one or more friendly HARVESTER units, when you target this unit with the Heroic Intervention Stratagem, that use is -1 CP. In addition, while the bearer is within 6" of one or more friendly HARVESTER units, models in the bearer’s unit have the Fights First ability.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

TYRANIDS model only.
