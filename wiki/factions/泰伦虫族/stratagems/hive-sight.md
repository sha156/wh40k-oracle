1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/tyranid-attack.md|Tyranid Attack]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One TYRANIDS SYNAPSE unit from your army and one friendly TYRANIDS unit that has not declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit that is visible to your SYNAPSE unit. Until the end of the phase, each time your other unit declares a charge, it can include that enemy unit as a target of that charge, even if it is not visible to it, and when doing so, add 1 to the Charge roll.
