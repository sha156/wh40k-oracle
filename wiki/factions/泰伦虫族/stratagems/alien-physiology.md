1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/warrior-bioform-onslaught.md|Warrior Bioform Onslaught]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly TYRANID WARRIORS unit.

## 使用对象

That TYRANID WARRIORS unit.

## 效果

Attacks that target your unit with a S greater than your unit’s T have -1 to wound rolls.
