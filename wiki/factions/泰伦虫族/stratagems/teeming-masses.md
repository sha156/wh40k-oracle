1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/unending-swarm.md|Unending Swarm]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Endless Multitude unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
