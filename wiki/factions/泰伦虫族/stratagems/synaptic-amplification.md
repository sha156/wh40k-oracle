1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/warrior-bioform-onslaught.md|Warrior Bioform Onslaught]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One TYRANIDS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

If that unit is a Tyranid Warriors unit, select up to one Endless Multitude unit from your army, that is not Battle-shocked, within 6" of it. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in those units makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1, and, if it is a TYRANID WARRIORS unit, re-roll a Hit roll of 1 as well.
