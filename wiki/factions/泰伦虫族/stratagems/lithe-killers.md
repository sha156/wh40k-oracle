1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/boarding-swarm.md|Boarding Swarm]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One TYRANIDS unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 5+ invulnerable save.
