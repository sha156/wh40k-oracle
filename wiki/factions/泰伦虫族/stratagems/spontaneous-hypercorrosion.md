1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/warrior-bioform-onslaught.md|战斗生物总攻]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One TYRANIDS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 2 to the Strength characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit and add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by Tyranid Warriors and Winged Tyranid Prime models in your unit.
