1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/boarding-swarm.md|Boarding Swarm]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One TYRANIDS unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit is destroyed, if that model has not fought this phase, roll one D6. On a 5+, do not remove the destroyed model from play; it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play.
