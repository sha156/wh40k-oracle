1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/infestation-swarm.md|Infestation Swarm]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One GREAT DEVOURER unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
