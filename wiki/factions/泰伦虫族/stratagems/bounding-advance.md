1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/unending-swarm.md|Unending Swarm]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Endless Multitude unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit Advances, do not make an Advance roll. Instead, until the end of the phase, add 6" to the Move characteristic of models in your unit.
