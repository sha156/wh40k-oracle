1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/biotide.md|Biotide]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, at the start of the Reinforcements step.

## 使用对象

One ENDLESS MULTITUDE unit from your army that is in Reserves.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Deep Strike ability and, when setting them upon the battlefield using that ability, they can be set up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units, provided they are also within Synapse Range of your army.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once and, until the end of the turn, your unit is not eligible to declare a charge.
