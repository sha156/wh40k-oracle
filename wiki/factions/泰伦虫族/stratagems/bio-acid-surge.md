## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One TYRANIDS unit from your army that is within Synapse Range of your army and has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.
