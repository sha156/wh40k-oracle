1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/boarding-swarm.md|Boarding Swarm]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit opens a Hatchway.

## 使用对象

One TYRANIDS unit from your army that is within 3" of that Hatchway and is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Pounce move. To do so, your unit can be moved up to 3", but must end that move as close as possible to that Hatchway. When doing so, those models can be moved within Engagement Range of that enemy unit, but only that enemy unit.
