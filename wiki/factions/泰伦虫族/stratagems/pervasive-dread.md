1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/infestation-swarm.md|Infestation Swarm]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after the enemy WARLORD is destroyed (and is not returned to the battlefield) by a GREAT DEVOURER unit from your army.

## 使用对象

That GREAT DEVOURER unit.

## 效果

In your opponent’s next Command phase, each enemy unit on the battlefield must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from the result. This Stratagem cannot cause a unit to take two Battle-shock tests in the same phase.
