1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/泰伦虫族/detachments/biotide.md|Biotide]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One TERMAGANTs unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit can ignore other models in their unit for the purposes of determining visibility. In addition, if your unit is within Synapse Range of your army, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
