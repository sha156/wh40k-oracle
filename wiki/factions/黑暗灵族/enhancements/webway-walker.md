15 分、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/reapers-wager.md|Reaper’s Wager]] 分队。

## 效果

Models in the bearer’s unit have the Deep Strike ability. Each time the bearer’s unit is set up on the battlefield using the Deep Strike ability, if that unit is currently losing the wager, until the end of the turn, you can re-roll Charge rolls made for that unit.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Harlequins or Drukhari model only.
