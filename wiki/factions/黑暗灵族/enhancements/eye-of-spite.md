15 分、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/realspace-raiders.md|Realspace Raiders]] 分队。

## 效果

Improve the Attacks and Armour Penetration characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons by 1. Each time the bearer’s unit is selected to fight, you can spend 1 Pain token; if you do, until the end of the phase, improve the Attacks and Armour Penetration characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons by 2 instead.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

[[factions/黑暗灵族/units/succubus.md|Succubus]] model only.
