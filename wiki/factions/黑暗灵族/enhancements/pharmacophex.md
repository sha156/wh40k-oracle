15 分、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/spectacle-of-spite.md|Spectacle of Spite]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of your Command phase, after selecting which Combat Drugs will be active for your army, roll one D6 and consult the Combat Drugs list. The result rolled applies to the bearer’s unit until the start of your next Command phase in addition to any other Combat Drugs that are active for your army. If you randomly select one that is already active for your army, it has no additional effect.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

[[factions/黑暗灵族/units/succubus.md|Succubus]] model only.
