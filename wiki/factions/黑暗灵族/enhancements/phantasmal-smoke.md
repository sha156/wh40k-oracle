15 分、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/skysplinter-assault.md|Skysplinter Assault]] 分队。

## 效果

While the bearer’s unit is wholly within 6" of a friendly Drukhari Transport:

- Models in the bearer’s unit have the Stealth ability.
- Each time a ranged attack targets the bearer’s unit, models in that unit have the Benefit of Cover against that attack.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

DRUKHARI model only.
