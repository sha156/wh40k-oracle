黑暗灵族的分队，分队规则「痛苦联盟」。

## 分队规则

### 痛苦联盟 Alliance of Agony

At the start of the battle, you gain 2 Pain tokens for each of the following combinations your army contains (these do not need to be in the same Attached unit):

- One or more [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/archon.md|Archon]] models and one or more [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/kabalite-warriors.md|Kabalite Warriors]] units.
- One or more [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/succubus.md|Succubus]] models and one or more [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/wyches.md|Wyches]] units.
- One or more [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/haemonculus.md|Haemonculus]] models and one or more [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/wracks.md|Wracks]] units.

**Designer’s Note:** *These are all cumulative, so if your army contains at least one of all of the combinations listed above, you start the battle with 6 Pain tokens.*
