黑暗灵族的分队，分队规则「缝肉憎恶」。

## 分队规则

### 缝肉憎恶 Stitchflesh Abominations

Each time an attack targets a [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/haemonculus.md|Haemonculus]] Covens unit from your army, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is greater than the Toughness characteristic of your unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.
