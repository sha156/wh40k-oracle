2 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/spectacle-of-spite.md|Spectacle of Spite]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, when an enemy unit ends a fall-back move.

## 使用对象

One friendly unengaged WYCH CULT unit that is within 6" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Declare a charge with your unit. When selecting charge targets, you can only select enemy units that made a fall-back move this phase and are within the maximum distance.
