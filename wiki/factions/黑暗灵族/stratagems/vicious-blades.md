1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/skysplinter-assault.md|Skysplinter Assault]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a Drukhari Transport from your army has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

That [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].

## 效果

After your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] has fought, select one enemy unit that was the target of one or more of those attacks and roll one D6 for each model embarked within your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]], adding 1 to the result if that embarked model is a [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/wracks.md|Wracks]] model: for each 5+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound (to a maximum of 6 mortal wounds).
