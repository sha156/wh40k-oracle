1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/realspace-raiders.md|Realspace Raiders]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Drukhari unit from your army (excluding [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/haemonculus.md|Haemonculus]] Covens units) that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
