1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/painbringers.md|Painbringers]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of any phase, when an objective marker Secured by your army would be Seized by an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

One HAEMONcULUS COvENS unit from your army within range of that objective marker.

## 效果

Until the end of your next turn, while you have one or more DRUKHARI units within range of that objective marker (excluding Battle-shocked units), that objective marker remains Secured by your army and cannot be Seized by enemy units.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle round.
