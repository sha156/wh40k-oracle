1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/skysplinter-assault.md|Skysplinter Assault]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One Drukhari Transport from your army that is within 8" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] can make a Normal move of up to 6".
