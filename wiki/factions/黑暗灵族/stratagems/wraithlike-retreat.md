1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/skysplinter-assault.md|Skysplinter Assault]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Drukhari Infantry unit from your army that fought this phase.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal or Fall Back move, but unless it is a [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/wyches.md|Wyches]] unit, it must end that move wholly within 3" horizontally and 5" vertically of a friendly Drukhari Transport and must embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] at the end of that move (otherwise, it cannot make that move). Your unit can embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] in a turn it disembarked from that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].
