2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/realspace-raiders.md|Realspace Raiders]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/kabalite-warriors.md|Kabalite Warriors]] units from your army, or one other Drukhari unit from your army (excluding ScouRGES and Aircraft).

## 效果

Each of those units can make a Normal move of up to 6".

## 限制

You cannot select units that are within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units. Until the end of the turn, those units are not eligible to declare a charge.
