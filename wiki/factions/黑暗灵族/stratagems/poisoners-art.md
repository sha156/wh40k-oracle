1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/covenite-coterie.md|Covenite Coterie]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/haemonculus.md|Haemonculus]] Covens unit from your army has fought.

## 使用对象

That HAEMONCULUS COVENS unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (excluding VEHICLES) hit by one or more of your unit’s attacks this phase. Until the end of the battle, that enemy unit is poisoned. At the start of each Command phase, roll one D6 for each poisoned unit on the battlefield: on a 4+, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.
