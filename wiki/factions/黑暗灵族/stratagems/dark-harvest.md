1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/realspace-raiders.md|Realspace Raiders]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/wracks.md|Wracks]] units from your army, or one other Drukhari unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in each of those units have the [LETHAL HITS] ability.
