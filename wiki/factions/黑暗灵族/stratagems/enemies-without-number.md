1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/kabalite-cartel.md|Kabalite Cartel]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase, just after you complete a Contract.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/archon.md|Archon]] WARLORD from your army.

## 效果

Select one new Contract (this can be one you have already completed), then select one unit from your opponent’s army that is on the battlefield and matches the ‘Contract’ description in that Contract. Until that Contract is completed, that unit is your Contract unit and the Murderous Agenda Detachment rule applies as normal.
