1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/kabalite-agonysts.md|阴谋团折磨使]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when a friendly BLADES FOR HIRE/KABAL unit targets a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit.

## 使用对象

That BLADES FOR HIRE/KABAL unit.

## 效果

Your unit’s attacks that target a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit can:

- Re-roll hit rolls of 1.
- Re-roll wound rolls of 1.
