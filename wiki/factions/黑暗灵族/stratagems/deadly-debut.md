1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/spectacle-of-spite.md|Spectacle of Spite]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One DRUKHARI unit from your army that made a Charge move this turn and has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [LETHAL HITS] ability. If your unit is a [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/wyches.md|Wyches]] unit, until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1 as well.
