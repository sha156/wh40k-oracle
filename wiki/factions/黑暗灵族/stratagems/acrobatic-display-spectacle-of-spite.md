1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/spectacle-of-spite.md|Spectacle of Spite]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Wych Cult unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 5+ invulnerable save.
