1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/kabalite-cartel.md|Kabalite Cartel]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/kabalite-warriors.md|Kabalite Warriors]] or [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/hand-of-the-archon.md|Hand of the Archon]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, improve the Ballistic Skill and Armour Penetration characteristics of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1.
