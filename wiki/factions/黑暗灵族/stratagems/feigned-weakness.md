1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/spectacle-of-spite.md|Spectacle of Spite]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a Drukhari unit from your army Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That DRUKHARI unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in turn in which it Fell Back.
