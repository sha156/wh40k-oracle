1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/ship-killer-cult.md|Ship-killer Cult]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One WYCH CULT unit from your army that opened a Hatchway this turn.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your unit can declare a charge that includes one or more targets that are not visible to it, provided each of those units that are not visible to it are wholly on the opposite side of that Hatchway from your unit.
