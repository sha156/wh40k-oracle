1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/skysplinter-assault.md|Skysplinter Assault]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Drukhari Vehicle unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
