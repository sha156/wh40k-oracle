1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/realspace-raiders.md|Realspace Raiders]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase or the start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two Drukhari Battleline units from your army, or one other Drukhari unit from your army.

## 效果

You can spend 1 Pain token. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in each of those units makes an attack that targets an enemy unit that is Below Half-strength, add 1 to the Hit roll. If you spent 1 Pain token, add 1 to the Wound roll as well.
