1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/skysplinter-assault.md|Skysplinter Assault]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Drukhari unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase and that disembarked from a Transport this turn.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability, or the [SUSTAINED HITS 2] ability if your unit is a [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/kabalite-warriors.md|Kabalite Warriors]] or [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/hand-of-the-archon.md|Hand of the Archon]] unit.
