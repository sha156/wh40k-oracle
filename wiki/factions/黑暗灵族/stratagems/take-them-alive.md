1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/kabalite-corsairs.md|Kabalite Corsairs]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, just after a KABAL unit from your army has shot.

## 使用对象

That KABAL unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of your unit’s attacks this phase (excluding those made with Pistols). That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from the result if your unit is within 6” of it.
