1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/covenite-coterie.md|Covenite Coterie]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a Drukhari unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That DRUKHARI unit.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within 9" of and visible to your unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from that test if your unit is a [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/haemonculus.md|Haemonculus]] Covens unit.
