1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/tools-of-torment.md|折磨工具]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, when a friendly HAEMONCULUS COVENS unit destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That HAEMONCULUS COVENS unit.

## 效果

Select one visible enemy unit within 12" of your unit. That enemy unit makes a battle-shock roll, with -1 to that battle-shock roll.
