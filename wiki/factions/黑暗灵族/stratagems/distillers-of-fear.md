2 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/covenite-coterie.md|Covenite Coterie]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/黑暗灵族/units/haemonculus.md|Haemonculus]] Covens unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit that is Battle-shocked, that attack has the [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] ability.
