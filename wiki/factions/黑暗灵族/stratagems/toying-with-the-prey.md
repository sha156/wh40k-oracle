1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/ship-killer-cult.md|Ship-killer Cult]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a WYcH CULT unit from your army has fought.

## 使用对象

That WYCH CULT unit.

## 效果

If your unit destroyed one or more enemy models this phase, select one of those enemy models. Until the end of your opponent’s next turn, subtract 1 from the Objective Control characteristic of models in that enemy model's unit (to a minimum of l).
