1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/黑暗灵族/detachments/kabalite-corsairs.md|Kabalite Corsairs]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One KABAL unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Select one objective marker you do not control that is visible to your unit. Your unit can make a Normal move of up to D6", but if it does, every model in that unit must end that move as close as possible to that objective marker.
