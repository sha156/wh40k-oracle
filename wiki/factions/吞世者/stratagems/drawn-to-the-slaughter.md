2 CP、Any phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/cult-of-blood.md|Cult of Blood]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/吞世者/units/jakhals.md|Jakhals]] unit from your army that was just destroyed. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Add a new unit to your army identical to your destroyed unit, in Strategic Reserves, at its Starting Strength.

## 限制

This Stratagem cannot be used to return destroyed Character units to Attached units. You can only use this Stratagem once per battle.
