1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/boarding-butchers.md|Boarding Butchers]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a WORLD EATERS unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That WORLD EATERS unit.

## 效果

Until the end of your opponent’s next Command phase, subtract 1 from Battle-shock and Leadership tests taken for all enemy units.
