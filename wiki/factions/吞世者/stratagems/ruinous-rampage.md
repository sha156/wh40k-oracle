1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/skullsworn.md|Skullsworn]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement or Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One WORLD EATERS TERMINATOR, EIGHTBOUND or EXALTED EIGHTBOUND unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase and has not declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, when making a Normal, Advance or Charge move, your unit can move through a single closed Hatchway as if it was open. When doing so, that Hatchway is opened and cannot be closed for the rest of the battle. This can allow that unit to declare a charge against enemy units that are not visible to it, but to do so those enemy units must be within 1" of that Hatchway and wholly on the opposite side of it from your unit.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per turn.
