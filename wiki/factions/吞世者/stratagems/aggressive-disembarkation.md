1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/goretrack-onslaught.md|Goretrack Onslaught]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Rhino model from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

One WORLD EATERS unit embarked within your RHINO can disembark. When doing so, models in that unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 6" of your RHINO and can be set up within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
