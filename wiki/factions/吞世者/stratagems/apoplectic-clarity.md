1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/brazen-engines.md|Brazen Engines]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when a friendly DAEMON VEHICLE unit is selected to attack.

## 使用对象

That DAEMON VEHICLE unit.

## 效果

Your unit’s attacks can ignore modifiers to:

- BS.
- WS.
- Hit rolls and wound rolls.
