1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/goretrack-onslaught.md|Goretrack Onslaught]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Vehicle model from your army that was hit by one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Your model can make a Normal move of up to 6".

## 限制

A unit cannot be targeted by this Stratagem and the Fury Unleashed Stratagem in the same phase.
