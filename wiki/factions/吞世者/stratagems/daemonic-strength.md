1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/possessed-slaughterband.md|Possessed Slaughterband]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Possessed unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack made by a model in your unit is allocated to an enemy model, if your unit has the [[factions/吞世者/units/eightbound.md|Eightbound]] keyword and that enemy model is not a MONSTER or VEHICLE, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of that attack. If your unit has the [[factions/吞世者/units/exalted-eightbound.md|Exalted Eightbound]] keyword and that enemy model is a MONSTER or VEHICLE, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of that attack instead.
