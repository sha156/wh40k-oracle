1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/khorne-daemonkin.md|Khorne Daemonkin]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, when the last model in a unit is destroyed, before removing it from play. (If that unit is a Transport, any units embarked within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] model must disembark first.)

## 使用对象

One [[factions/吞世者/units/bloodletters.md|Bloodletters]] unit from your army that is in Reserves.

## 效果

Set your unit up anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 9" of that destroyed model and more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units, then remove the destroyed model from play.

## 限制

You cannot use this Stratagem more than once per battle round.
