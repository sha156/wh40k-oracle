1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/berzerker-warband.md|Berzerker Warband]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase and that made a charge move this turn.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1.
