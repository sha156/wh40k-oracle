## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Infantry unit from your army and one friendly Transport that it is able to embark within.

## 效果

If your WORLD EATERS INFANTRY unit is wholly within 6" of that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]], it can embark within it.
