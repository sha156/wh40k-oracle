2 CP、Command phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/cult-of-blood.md|Cult of Blood]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Monster or World Eaters Titanic unit from your army.

## 效果

Select the Idol of Infinite Rage, Idol of Burning Wrath or Idol of Blessed Blood. Until the start of your next Command phase, that Idols of Khorne ability is active for your unit instead of any other Idols of Khorne ability that is active for your army, even if you have already selected that ability this battle.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle.
