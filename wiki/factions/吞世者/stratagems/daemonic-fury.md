1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/khorne-daemonkin.md|Khorne Daemonkin]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Blood Legions unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one friendly World Eaters unit within 6" of your unit. Until the end of the turn, melee weapons equipped by models in your WORLD EATERS unit have the [LANCE] ability. If the Daemonic Rage ability is active for your army, then until the end of the phase those melee weapons also have the [TWIN-LINKED] ability.
