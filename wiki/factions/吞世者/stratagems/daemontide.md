1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/khorne-daemonkin.md|Khorne Daemonkin]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one friendly Blood Legions unit within 6" of your unit. One destroyed Mounted model, up to D3 destroyed Beast models, or up to D6 destroyed Infantry models are returned to that BLOOD LEGIONS unit with their full wounds remaining.

## 限制

This Stratagem cannot be used to return destroyed Character models to Attached units.
