1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/khorne-daemonkin.md|Khorne Daemonkin]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Blood Legions unit from your army that is within 6" of a friendly World Eaters unit that was selected as the target as one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your WORLD EATERS unit have a 5+ invulnerable save. If the Boon of Blood ability is active for your army, then until the end of the phase, models in your WORLD EATERS unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
