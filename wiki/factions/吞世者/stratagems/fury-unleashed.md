1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/goretrack-onslaught.md|Goretrack Onslaught]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Rhino model from your army that has one or more wounds remaining and was hit by one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

One KHORNE BERZERKERS unit embarked within your model can make a disembark move and then make a surge move of up to D6+2".

## 限制

A unit cannot be targeted by this Stratagem and the Unrelenting Advance Stratagem in the same phase.
