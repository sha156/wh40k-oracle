1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/cult-of-blood.md|Cult of Blood]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Monster or World Eaters Titanic unit from your army that was just destroyed by an enemy unit. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Until the end of the battle, each time a model in a [[factions/吞世者/units/jakhals.md|Jakhals]] or [[factions/吞世者/units/goremongers.md|Goremongers]] unit from your army makes an attack that targets the enemy unit that just destroyed your unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
