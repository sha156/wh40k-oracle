1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/vessels-of-wrath.md|Vessels of Wrath]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Infantry, World Eaters Mounted or World Eaters Daemon Prince unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets a MONSTER or VEHICLE unit, you can re-roll the Wound roll.
