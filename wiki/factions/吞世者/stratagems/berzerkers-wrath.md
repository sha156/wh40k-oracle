1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/berzerker-warband.md|Berzerker Warband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One KHORNE BERZERKERS unit from your army that can make a surge move as a result of those attacks.

## 效果

Do not roll a D6 to determine how far models in your unit can move when they make a surge move. Instead, when making a surge move, those models can move up to 8".
