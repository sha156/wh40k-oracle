1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/berzerker-warband.md|Berzerker Warband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a KHORNE BERZERKERS unit from your army is selected to Advance.

## 使用对象

That [[factions/吞世者/units/khorne-berzerkers.md|Khorne Berzerkers]] unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced.
