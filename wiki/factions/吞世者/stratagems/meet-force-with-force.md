1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/vessels-of-wrath.md|Vessels of Wrath]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One World Eaters Infantry, World Eaters Mounted or World Eaters Daemon Prince unit from your army that lost one or more wounds as a result of those attacks.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Blood Surge move. To do so, roll one D6: models in your unit move a number of inches up to the result, but your unit must end that move as close as possible to the closest enemy unit (excluding [[core-rules/aircraft.md|AIRCRAFT]]). When doing so, those models can be moved within Engagement Range of that enemy unit. If your unit is a [[factions/吞世者/units/khorne-berzerkers.md|Khorne Berzerkers]] or VESSEL OF WRATH unit, you can re-roll the D6 to determine how far your unit can move.
