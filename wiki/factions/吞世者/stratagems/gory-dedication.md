1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/vessels-of-wrath.md|怒火容器]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One WORLD EATERS unit from your army that destroyed one or more enemy models with a melee attack this phase.

## 效果

Select one objective marker you control that your unit is within range of. That objective marker remains under your control until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase.
