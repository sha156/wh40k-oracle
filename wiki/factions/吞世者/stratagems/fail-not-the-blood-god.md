1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/cult-of-blood.md|Cult of Blood]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/吞世者/units/jakhals.md|Jakhals]] or [[factions/吞世者/units/goremongers.md|Goremongers]] unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1. If that model’s unit is within 6" of one or more friendly World Eaters Monster units, or within 9" of one or more friendly World Eaters Titanic units, you can re-roll the Hit roll instead.
