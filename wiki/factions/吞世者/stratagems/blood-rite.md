1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/skullsworn.md|Skullsworn]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a WORLD EATERS TERMINATOR, EIGHTBOUND or EXALTED EIGHTBOUND unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That unit.

## 效果

Select one objective marker that your unit or the destroyed unit was within range of at the start of the phase. That objective marker is Secured by your army.
