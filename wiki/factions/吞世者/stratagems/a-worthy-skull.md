1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/khorne-daemonkin.md|Khorne Daemonkin]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a Blood Legions or World Eaters unit from your army has fought, and one or more enemy [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] or MONSTER models were destroyed as a result of those attacks.

## 使用对象

That BLOOD LEGIONS or WORLD EATERS unit.

## 效果

You gain D3BTP and you can then spend one or more BTP you have to activate one of the Blood Tithe abilities.
