1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/skullsworn.md|Skullsworn]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement or Charge phase, just after an enemy unit is selected to fire Overwatch.

## 使用对象

One WORLD EATERS unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an enemy unit is selected to fire Overwatch, it must take a Leadership test, subtracting 1 from the result: if failed, that enemy unit cannot fire Overwatch at your unit this phase.
