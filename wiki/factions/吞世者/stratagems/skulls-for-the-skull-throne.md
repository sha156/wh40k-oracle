1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/berzerker-warband.md|Berzerker Warband]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a WORLD EATERS unit from your army destroys a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] or MONSTER model.

## 使用对象

That World Eaters unit.

## 效果

Make a Blessings of Khorne roll and use the results to activate one Blessing of Khorne. Until the end of the battle round, that Blessing of Khorne is active in addition to any other Blessings of Khorne that are currently active.
