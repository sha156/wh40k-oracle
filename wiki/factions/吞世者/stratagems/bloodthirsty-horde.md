1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/cult-of-blood.md|Cult of Blood]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/吞世者/units/jakhals.md|Jakhals]] or [[factions/吞世者/units/goremongers.md|Goremongers]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase and is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit is selected to fight, when determining which models in it are eligible to fight, any models in your unit that are within 3" of one or more enemy models are eligible to fight. When resolving those attacks, such models can target one of those enemy units that is within 3" of them and within Engagement Range of their unit.
