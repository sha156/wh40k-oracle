1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/cult-of-blood.md|Cult of Blood]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/吞世者/units/jakhals.md|Jakhals]] or [[factions/吞世者/units/goremongers.md|Goremongers]] unit from your army that was selected as the target as one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 6+ ability. If your unit is within 6" of one or more friendly World Eaters Monster units, or within 9" of one or more friendly World Eaters Titanic units, your unit has the Feel No Pain 5+ ability instead.
