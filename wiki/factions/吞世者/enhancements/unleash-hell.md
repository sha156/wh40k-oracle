10 分、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/goretrack-onslaught.md|Goretrack Onslaught]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of your Shooting phase, you can select one Vehicle model within 6" of the bearer or, if the bearer is embarked within a Transport, you can select that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] model. Until the end of the phase, after the selected model has shot, select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the start of your next turn, that enemy unit is suppressed. While a unit is suppressed, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

World Eaters model only.
