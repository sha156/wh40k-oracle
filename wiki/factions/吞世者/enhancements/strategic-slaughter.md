20 分、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/cult-of-blood.md|Cult of Blood]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, select up to three [[factions/吞世者/units/jakhals.md|Jakhals]] and/or [[factions/吞世者/units/goremongers.md|Goremongers]] units from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in Strategic Reserves, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

World Eaters model only.
