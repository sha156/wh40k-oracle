15 分、[[factions/吞世者/detachments/khorne-daemonkin.md|Khorne Daemonkin]] 分队。

## 效果

During the Declare Battle Formations step, the bearer can be attached to a [[factions/吞世者/units/bloodcrushers.md|Bloodcrushers]] or [[factions/吞世者/units/flesh-hounds.md|Flesh Hounds]] unit. While leading one of these units, the bearer has the Deep Strike ability, and it has the BLOOD LEGIONS Faction keyword instead of the WORLD EATERS Faction keyword.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

[[factions/吞世者/units/lord-on-juggernaut.md|Lord on Juggernaut]] model only.
