## 效果

Add 1 to the Attacks, Strength and Damage characteristics of the bearers melee weapons. Each time the bearer’s unit destroys an enemy unit with a melee attack, do not roll to gain a Blood Tithe point, you automatically gain 1 Blood Tithe point instead.

**分数**：30 分

## 携带限制

World Eaters model only.
