20 分、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/bringers-of-flame.md|Bringers of Flame]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of your Command phase, you can discard up to 2 Miracle dice. Then, add 1 Miracle dice to your Miracle Dice pool showing a value equal to the sum of the two Miracle dice you just discarded (to a maximum of 6).

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTA SORORITAS model only.
