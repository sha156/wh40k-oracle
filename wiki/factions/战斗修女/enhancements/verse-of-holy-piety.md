15 分、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/penitent-host.md|Penitent Host]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle, at the start of the battle round, select one Vow of Atonement. Until the start of the next battle round, that Vow of Atonement is active for the bearer’s unit in addition to any that is active for your army, even if you have already selected that Vow of Atonement this battle.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

PENITENT model only.
