30 分、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/penitent-host.md|Penitent Host]] 分队。

## 效果

While the bearer is on the battlefield, each time an enemy unit is destroyed by a penitent unit from you army, you can discard 1 Miracle dice then gain 1 Miracle dice showing a value of 6.

**分数**：30 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTA SORORITAS model only.
