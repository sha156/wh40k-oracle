战斗修女的分队「Penitents and Pilgrims」，分队规则「Bloody Redemption」。

## 分队规则

### Bloody Redemption

The first time each turn that you gain 1 Miracle dice as the result of an ARCO-FLAGELLANTS or REPENTiA SqUAD unit from your army being destroyed, do not roll a D6; that Miracle dice has a value of 6 instead.
