1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/champions-of-faith.md|Champions of Faith]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTA SORORITAS unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, improve the Strength characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1. If your unit is Righteous, until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1 as well.
