1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/penitent-host.md|Penitent Host]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One PENITENT unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a PENITENT model in your unit makes a melee attack, a successful unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a Critical Hit.
