1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/penitent-host.md|Penitent Host]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One PENITENT unit from your army that was just destroyed while it was within range of an objective marker you controlled. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

That objective marker remains under your control, even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the start or end of any turn.
