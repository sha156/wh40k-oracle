1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/chorus-of-condemnation.md|定罪合唱]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly EXORCIST unit is selected to shoot.

## 使用对象

That EXORCIST unit.

## 效果

Select one unit visible to and within 9" of a friendly ADEPTA SORORITAS INFANTRY FLY unit. Your unit’s ranged attacks that target that unit have +1 to Hit rolls.
