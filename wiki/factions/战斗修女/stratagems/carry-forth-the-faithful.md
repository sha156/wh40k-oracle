1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/bringers-of-flame.md|Bringers of Flame]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just before an ADEPTA SORORITAS TRANSPORT model from your army Advances.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTA SORORITAS TRANSPORT model.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, you can re-roll Advance rolls made for your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]], and units can disembark from your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] even though it Advanced. Units that do so count as having made a Normal move, and cannot declare a charge this turn
