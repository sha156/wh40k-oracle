1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/sacred-champions.md|Sacred Champions]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Any phase, when a friendly CELESTIAN SACRESANTS unit suffers a mortal wound.

## 使用对象

That CELESTIAN SACRESANTS unit.

## 效果

Your unit has the Feel No Pain 5+ ability against mortal wounds until the end of the phase.
