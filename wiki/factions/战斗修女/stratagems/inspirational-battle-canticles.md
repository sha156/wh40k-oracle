## 使用时机

Start of the Command phase.

## 使用对象

One friendly ADEPTA SORORITAS INFANTRY FLY unit or one friendly EXORCIST unit.

## 效果

Select one friendly battle-shocked ADEPTA SORORITAS unit within 6" of your unit. That unit is no longer battle-shocked.
