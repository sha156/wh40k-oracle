1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/pious-protectors.md|Pious Protectors]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement or Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTA SORORITAS unit from your army that has not fired Overwatch this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack while firing Overwatch, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ is required to score a hit, instead of an unmodified 6. In addition, until the end of the phase, each time you roll to determine the number of attacks made with a weapon equipped by a model in your unit, you can re-roll the result.
