1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/sacred-champions.md|神圣冠军]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, when a friendly CELESTIAN SACRESANTS unit is selected to make an Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

That CELESTIAN SACRESANTS unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit’s ranged attacks have [ASSAULT], and that move does not prevent your unit from being eligible to shoot or declare a charge.
