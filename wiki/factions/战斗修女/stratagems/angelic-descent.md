1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/army-of-faith.md|Army of Faith]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTA SORORITAS JUMP PACK unit from your army.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.

## 限制

You cannot select a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
