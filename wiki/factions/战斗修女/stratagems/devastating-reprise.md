1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/chorus-of-condemnation.md|Chorus of Condemnation]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly EXORCIST unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One friendly ADEPTA SORORITAS INFANTRY FLY unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE units) hit by those ranged attacks. Your unit’s ranged attacks that target that unit have [DEVASTATING WOUNDS].
