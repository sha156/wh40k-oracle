1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/pious-protectors.md|Pious Protectors]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTA SORORITAS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

You can discard 1 Miracle dice. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1. If you discarded a Miracle dice, you can re-roll the Hit roll instead.
