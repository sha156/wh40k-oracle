1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/pious-protectors.md|Pious Protectors]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit has declared a charge.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTA SORORITAS unit from your army (excluding ARcO-fLAGELLANTS and REPENTIA SqUAD units) that was selected as a target of that charge.

## 效果

You can discard 1 Miracle dice. Until the end of the phase, subtract 1 from Charge rolls made for that enemy unit. If you discarded a Miracle dice, subtract 2 from Charge rolls made forthat enemy unit instead.
