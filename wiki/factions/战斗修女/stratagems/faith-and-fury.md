1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/army-of-faith.md|Army of Faith]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTA SORORITAS unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [LANCE] ability. If one or more enemy models are destroyed as the result of your unit's attacks this phase, you gain 1 Miracle dice.
