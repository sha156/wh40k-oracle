1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/战斗修女/detachments/penitents-and-pilgrims.md|Penitents and Pilgrims]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit is selected to Fall Back.

## 使用对象

One ARcO-fLAGELLANTS or REPENTIA SqUAD unit from your army within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, when that enemy unit Falls Back, models in it must take Desperate Escape tests as if their unit was Battle-shocked. When doing so, if that enemy unit is also Battle-shocked by other means, subtract 1 from each of those Desperate Escape tests.
