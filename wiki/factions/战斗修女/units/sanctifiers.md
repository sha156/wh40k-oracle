## 技能
- **One Shot**：The bearer can only shoot with this weapon once per battle.
- **Ministorum Sermon**：While this unit contains a <span class="kwb">MINISTORUM</span> <span class="kwb">PRIEST</span>, melee weapons equipped by models in this unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.
- **Cherub**：Once per battle, after this unit has performed an Act of Faith, you gain 1 Miracle dice.<br><br><b>Designer’s Note:</b> <i>Place a Cherub token next to the unit, removing it once this ability has been used.</i>
- **ATTACHED UNIT**：If a Ministorum Priest from your army with the Leader ability can be attached to a [[factions/战斗修女/units/battle-sisters-squad.md|Battle Sisters Squad]], it can be attached to this unit instead. If a <span class="kwb">MINISTORUM</span> <span class="kwb">PRIEST</span> from your army is attached to this unit during the Declare Battle Formations step, that model gains the Scouts 6" ability.
- **Salvationist Medikit**：In your Command phase, if the bearer is on the battlefield, you can return up to D3 destroyed models (excluding <span class="kwb">CHARACTER</span> models) to this unit.
- **Simulacrum Imperialis**：At the end of your Command phase, for each objective marker you control that has one or more units from your army with this ability within range of it, roll one D6: on a 4+, you gain 1 Miracle dice showing a value equal to that result.
