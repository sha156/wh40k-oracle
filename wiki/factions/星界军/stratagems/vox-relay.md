1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/mechanised-assault.md|Mechanised Assault]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Infantry Officer unit from your army embarked within a Transport. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it is embarked within a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your unit can issue Orders even though it is not on the battlefield, and it can issue Orders to Astra Militarum Transport units from your army [excluding TiTANic units) regardless of the distance to and from your unit’s [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].
