1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/grizzled-company.md|Grizzled Company]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army affected by the Duty and Honour! Order.

## 效果

If your unit is within range of an objective marker you control, that objective marker remains under your control, even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the start or end of a phase.
