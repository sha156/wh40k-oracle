1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/mechanised-assault.md|Mechanised Assault]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, after an enemy unit has selected targets for its charge but before it makes a Charge move.

## 使用对象

One Astra Militarum Infantry unit from your army that was selected as a target of that charge.

## 效果

Provided your unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units and every model in your unit is within 3" of an AsTRA Militarum Transport from your army, it can embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].
