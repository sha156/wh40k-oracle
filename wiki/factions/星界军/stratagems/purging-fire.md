1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/grizzled-company.md|老兵连队]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army affected by an Order that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

If your unit is within range of an objective marker, until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [LETHAL HITS] ability.
