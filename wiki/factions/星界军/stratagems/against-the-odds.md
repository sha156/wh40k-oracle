1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/embarked-regiment.md|Embarked Regiment]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army (excluding OGRYN units) that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, on a Critical Wound, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 2.
