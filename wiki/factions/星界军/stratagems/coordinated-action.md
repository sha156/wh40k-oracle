1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/combined-arms.md|Combined Arms]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of any phase.

## 使用对象

One Regiment unit from your army and one Squadron unit from your army within 6" of and visible to that REGIMENT unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, Orders affecting one of your units affect the other, and vice versa.
