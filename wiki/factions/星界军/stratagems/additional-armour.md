1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/grizzled-company.md|老兵连队]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
