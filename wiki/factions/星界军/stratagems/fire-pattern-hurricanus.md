1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/tempestus-boarding-regiment.md|Tempestus Boarding Regiment]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, just after a MILITARUM TEMPESTUS unit from your army has shot.

## 使用对象

That MILITARUM TEMPESTUS unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the start of your next Shooting phase, subtract 2 from the Move characteristic of models in that enemy unit, and subtract 2 from Advance and Charge rolls made for it.
