1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/embarked-regiment.md|Embarked Regiment]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Movement or Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army (excluding OGRYN units) that has not fired Overwatch this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack while firing Overwatch, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ is required to score a hit, instead of an unmodified 6.
