1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/bridgehead-strike.md|Bridgehead Strike]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, if all of its models have the Deep Strike ability, or one [[factions/星界军/units/valkyrie.md|Valkyrie]] unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
