1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/abhuman-auxiliaries.md|亚人类辅助军]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly BULLGRYN SQUAD/OGRYN SQUAD unit that is within range of an objective.

## 使用对象

That BULLGRYN SQUAD/OGRYN SQUAD unit.

## 效果

Attacks that target your unit have -1 AP until that enemy unit has attacked.
