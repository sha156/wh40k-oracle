1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/combined-arms.md|Combined Arms]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Regiment unit and one Squadron unit from your army that have not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit. Until the end of the phase, each time your selected REGIMENT and SQUADRON units make an attack that targets that enemy unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
