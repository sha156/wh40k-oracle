1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/grizzled-company.md|老兵连队]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Astra Militarum Infantry unit from your army affected by the First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire! Order.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, improve the Strength characteristic of that attack by 1.
