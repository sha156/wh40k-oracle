1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/steel-hammer.md|Steel Hammer]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, just after an Astra Militarum Titanic unit from your army has shot.

## 使用对象

That ASTRA MILITARUM TITANIC unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the end of the phase, that enemy unit cannot have the Benefit of Cover.
