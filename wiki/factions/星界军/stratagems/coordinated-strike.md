1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/tempestus-boarding-regiment.md|Tempestus Boarding Regiment]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One OffIcER model from your army.

## 效果

Select one Order your model can issue. Every ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army is issued that Order (regardless of any keywords those units are normally required to have).

## 限制

Until the end of the turn, OFFICER models from your army cannot issue Orders. You can only use this Stratagem once.
