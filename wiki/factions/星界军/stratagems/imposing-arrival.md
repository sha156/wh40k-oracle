1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/steel-hammer.md|钢铁之锤]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Reinforcements step of your Movement phase, from the second battle round onwards.

## 使用对象

One Astra Militarum Titanic unit from your army that is in Reserves.

## 效果

Set up your unit on the battlefield, wholly within 8" of the battlefield edge and more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units.
