1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/bridgehead-strike.md|Bridgehead Strike]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One friendly engaged REGIMENT unit.

## 效果

Roll one D6 for each enemy unit engaged with your unit:

- On a 2+ that enemy unit suffers D6 mortal wounds.
Then, your unit suffers 3D3 mortal wounds.
