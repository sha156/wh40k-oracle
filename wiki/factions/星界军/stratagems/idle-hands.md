1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/embarked-regiment.md|Embarked Regiment]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase and is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Select one closed Hatchway within 1" of your unit. Your unit can operate that Hatchway.

## 限制

Until the end of the turn, your unit cannot operate a Hatchway again.
