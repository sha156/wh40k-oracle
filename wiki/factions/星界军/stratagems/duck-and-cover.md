1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/embarked-regiment.md|Embarked Regiment]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army (excluding OGRYN units) that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks and has one or more models on the opposite side of an open Hatchway from one or more models in the attacking unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
