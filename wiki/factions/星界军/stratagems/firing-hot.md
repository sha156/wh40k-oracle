2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/bridgehead-strike.md|Bridgehead Strike]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly MILITARUM TEMPESTUS/KASRKIN unit is selected to shoot.

## 使用对象

That MILITARUM TEMPESTUS/KASRKIN unit.

## 效果

Your unit’s Hot-shot Lascarbines, Hot-shot Lasguns, Hot-shot Laspistols, Hot-shot Marksman Rifles, Hot-shot Volley Guns and Sentry Hot-shot Volley Guns weapons that targeted an enemy unit within 12" have +1 S and AP.
