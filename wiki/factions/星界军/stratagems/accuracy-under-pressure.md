2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/steel-hammer.md|钢铁之锤]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
