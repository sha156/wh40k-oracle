1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/combined-arms.md|Combined Arms]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Astra Militarum Vehicle unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a ranged attack is allocated to an [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] model from your army, if that model is not fully visible to every model in the attacking unit because of your VEHICLE, that model has the Benefit of Cover against that attack.
