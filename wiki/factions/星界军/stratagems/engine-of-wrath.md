1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/steel-hammer.md|钢铁之锤]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Astra Militarum Titanic unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit. Until the end of the phase, add 6 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of those weapons by 2, and each time a model in your unit fights, it can only target that enemy unit.
