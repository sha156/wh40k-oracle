1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/星界军/detachments/tempestus-boarding-regiment.md|Tempestus Boarding Regiment]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One ASTRA MILITARUM unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, after your unit ends a move, you can select one open Hatchway it moved through during that move; close that Hatchway.
