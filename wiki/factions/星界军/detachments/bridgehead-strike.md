星界军的分队「Bridgehead Strike」，分队规则「Only the Best」。

## 分队规则

> 结构库中本分队名下有 2 条分队规则，以下全部列出。

### Only the Best

Each time an Astra Militarum Infantry model from your army makes a ranged attack, re‐roll a Hit roll of 1.

### Fire Zone Purge

If a friendly MILITARUM TEMPESTUS OFFICER model is your WARLORD, friendly TEMPESTUS SCIONS units have:

- [[core-rules/battleline.md|BATTLELINE]].
- +1 OC.
When a friendly MILITARUM TEMPESTUS unit is selected to shoot, if that unit was set up this turn, that unit’s ranged attacks have +1 to hit rolls.
