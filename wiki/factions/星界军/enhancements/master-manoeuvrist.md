15 分、[[factions/星界军/detachments/armoured-infantry.md|装甲步兵]] 分队。

## 效果

At the end of your opponent’s Fight phase, if the bearer’s unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units and every model in that unit is within 3" of an Astra Militarum Transport from your army, it can embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Infantry Officer model only.
