20 分、[[factions/星界军/detachments/recon-element.md|Recon Element]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time an enemy [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] or [[core-rules/mounted.md|MOUNTED]] unit ends a Normal, Advance, Charge or Fall Back move within 9" of the bearer’s unit, roll one D6: on a 4+, until the start of your next turn, that enemy unit is stunned. While a unit is stunned, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Astra Militarum Infantry model only.
