25 分、[[factions/星界军/detachments/armoured-infantry.md|Armoured Infantry]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, if the bearer’s unit (or any Transport it is embarked within) is on the battlefield, select up to two units with the Regiment or Squadron keywords from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in Strategic Reserves, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Officer model only.
