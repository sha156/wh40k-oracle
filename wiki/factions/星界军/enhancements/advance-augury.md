15 分、[[factions/星界军/detachments/bridgehead-strike.md|Bridgehead Strike]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, select up to three Regiment units from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in Strategic Reserves, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Infantry Officer model only.
