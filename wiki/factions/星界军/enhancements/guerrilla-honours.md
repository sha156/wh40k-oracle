25 分、[[factions/星界军/detachments/recon-element.md|Recon Element]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, if the bearer is on the battlefield, select up to three other Astra Militarum Infantry units from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in Strategic Reserves if you wish, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Infantry Officer model only.
