1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/grand-coven.md|Grand Coven]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Thousand Sons unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, Psychic weapons equipped by models in the attacking unit have the [hazardous] ability, and models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 4+ ability against Psychic Attacks.
