1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/chosen-cabal.md|Chosen Cabal]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One THOUSAND SONS PSYKER CHARAcTER model from your army.

## 效果

Select one objective marker Secured by your opponent’s army that is within 6" of and visible to your model, then roll one D6: on a 2+, that objective marker is no longer considered Secured by your opponent’s army.
