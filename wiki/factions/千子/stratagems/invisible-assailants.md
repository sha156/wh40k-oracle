1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/fateseekers.md|Fateseekers]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your opponent’s Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One THOUSAND SONS PSYKER model from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit visible to your model. That unit must take a Leadership test, subtracting 1 from the result: if failed, until the start of your opponent’s next Movement phase, subtract 2" from the Move characteristic of models in that unit and subtract 2 from Advance and Charge rolls made for it; if passed, subtract 1" from the Move characteristic of models in that unit and subtract 1 from Advance and Charge rolls made for it.
