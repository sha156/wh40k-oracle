1 CP、Opponents Shooting phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/sekhetar-cohort.md|Sekhetar Cohort]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly SEKHETAR ROBOTS unit within 12" of a friendly THOUSAND SONS PSYKER unit.

## 使用对象

That SEKHETAR ROBOTS unit.

## 效果

Ranged attacks that target your unit with a S greater than your unit’s T have -1 to wound rolls.
