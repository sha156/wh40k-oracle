1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/warpmeld-pact.md|Warpmeld Pact]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One Thousand Sons Character model from your army (excluding Monsters) that was just destroyed. You can use this Stratagem on that model even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

At the end of the phase, add one Tzeentch [[factions/千子/units/chaos-spawn.md|Chaos Spawn]] unit containing one model to your army, and set it up as close as possible to where your model was destroyed and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle round.
