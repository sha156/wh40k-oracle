1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/devoted-thralls.md|Devoted Thralls]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One TzAANGORS unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Bestial Surge move. To do so, roll one D6: your unit can be moved a distance in inches up to the result, but must end that move as close as possible to the attacking unit. When doing so, those models can be moved within Engagement Range of that enemy unit, but only that enemy unit.
