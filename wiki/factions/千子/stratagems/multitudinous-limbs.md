## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, when a friendly [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]]/MOUNTED THOUSAND SONS PSYKER unit is selected to make an advance/fall back move.

That [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]]/MOUNTED THOUSAND SONS PSYKER unit.

## 效果

That move does not prevent your unit from being eligible to start an action.
