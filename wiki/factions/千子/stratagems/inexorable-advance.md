1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/rubricae-phalanx.md|Rubricae Phalanx]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Rubricae unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit can ignore any or all modifiers to its Move characteristic and to Advance rolls made for it, and ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [ASSAULT] ability.
