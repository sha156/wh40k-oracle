1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/chosen-cabal.md|Chosen Cabal]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One THOUSAND SONS PSYKER CHARAcTER model from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your model has a 3+ invulnerable save. At the end of the phase, your model suffers D3 mortal wounds.
