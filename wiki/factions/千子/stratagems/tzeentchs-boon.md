1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/chosen-cabal.md|Chosen Cabal]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One THOUSAND SONS PSYKER model from your army within range of an objective marker that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your model makes a Psychic Attack, you can re-roll the Wound roll.
