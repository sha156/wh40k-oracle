1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/devoted-thralls.md|Devoted Thralls]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Command phase, at the start of the Battle-shock step.

## 使用对象

One THOUSAND SONS CHARAcTER or TzAANGORS unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit below its Starting Strength that is within Engagement Range of your unit. That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test. If that enemy unit is also Below Half-strength, subtract 1 from that test. This Enhancement cannot cause a unit to take two Battle-shock tests in the same phase.
