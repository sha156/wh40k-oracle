1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/servants-of-change.md|Servants of Change]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, when a friendly [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]]/MOUNTED MUTANT unit is selected to make an advance/fall back move.

## 使用对象

That [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]]/MOUNTED MUTANT unit.

## 效果

In a turn your unit made an advance/fall-back move, that move does not prevent your unit from being eligible to start an action.
