1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/warpmeld-pact.md|Warpmeld Pact]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/千子/units/tzaangors.md|Tzaangors]] unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
