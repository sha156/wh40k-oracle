1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/rubricae-phalanx.md|Rubricae Phalanx]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/千子/units/rubric-marines.md|Rubric Marines]] unit from your army within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.
