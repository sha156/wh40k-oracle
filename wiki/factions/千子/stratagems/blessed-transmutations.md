1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/warpmeld-pact.md|Warpmeld Pact]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Thousand Sons Psyker model from your army, and one friendly [[factions/千子/units/tzaangors.md|Tzaangors]] unit that is below its Starting Strength and within 12" of that [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] model.

## 效果

Return up to D3+1 destroyed models (excluding Characters) to your TZAANGORS unit.
