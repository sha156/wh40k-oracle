1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/chosen-cabal.md|Chosen Cabal]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One RUBRIc MARINES or ScARAB OccULT TERMINATORS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select one closed Hatchway. Until the end of the phase, models in your unit can make attacks with ranged weapons (excluding Torrent weapons) that target enemy units within 1" of that Hatchway and that are wholly on the opposite side of it from your unit, as if that Hatchway was open. Each time a model does so, that attack has an Armour Penetration characteristic of 0.
