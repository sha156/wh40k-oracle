2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/rubricae-phalanx.md|Rubricae Phalanx]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/千子/units/rubric-marines.md|Rubric Marines]] Psyker unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit (excluding [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] models), subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of that attack.
