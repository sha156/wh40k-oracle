1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/changehost-of-deceit.md|Changehost of Deceit]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two Scintillating Legions units from your army (excluding Monsters), or one Scintillating Legions Monster unit from your army, if all of those units are more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units.

## 效果

Remove those units from the battlefield and place them into Strategic Reserves.
