1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/千子/detachments/rubricae-phalanx.md|Rubricae Phalanx]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after a Thousand Sons Psyker model from your army is destroyed.

## 使用对象

One Rubricae unit from your army that was within 6" of that [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] model when it was destroyed.

## 效果

After the attacking unit has shot, your RUBRICAE unit can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase, but when resolving those attacks it can only target the enemy unit that just destroyed your [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] model (and only if it is an eligible target).
