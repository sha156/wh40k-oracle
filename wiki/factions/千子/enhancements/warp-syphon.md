## 效果

While the bearer is within 6" of one or more friendly Thousand Sons Vehicle units, each time the bearer Channels the Warp, after you roll that additional D6 but before checking for doubles or triples, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, select one of those VEHICLE units; that VEHICLE unit suffers 1 mortal wound and you can re-roll that additional D6.

**分数**：5 分

## 携带限制

THOUSAND SONS model only.
