1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/spirit-conclave.md|Spirit Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithblades.md|Wraithblades]] or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithguard.md|Wraithguard]] unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one objective marker you control that your unit is within range of. That objective marker remains under your control until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase.
