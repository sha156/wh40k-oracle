1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/eldritch-raiders.md|骇人劫掠者]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/corsair-voidscarred.md|Corsair Voidscarred]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or Fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of that attack.
