1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/star-dancer-masque.md|Star-dancer Masque]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an AELDARI unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That AELDARI unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit makes a Consolidation move, it can move up to D3+3" instead of up to 3" and, if it cannot end that move within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units that are visible to it (and in Unit Coherency), it can still make that Consolidation move, and when doing so can move in any direction.
