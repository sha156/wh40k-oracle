1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/spirit-conclave.md|Spirit Conclave]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, just after a [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithblades.md|Wraithblades]], [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithlord.md|Wraithlord]] or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithknight.md|Wraithknight]] unit from your army ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

That WRAITHBLADES, WRAITHLORD or WRAITHKNIGHT unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit and roll one D6 for each WRAITHBLADES model in your unit, or roll four D6 if your unit has the WRAITHLORD keyword, or roll six D6 if your unit has the WRAITHKNIGHT keyword: for each 3+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
