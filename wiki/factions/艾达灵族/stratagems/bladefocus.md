1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/khaines-arrow.md|Khaine’s Arrow]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One HOwLING BANSHEES or STRIKING ScORPIONS unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if it made a Charge move this turn, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
