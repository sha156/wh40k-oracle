1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/warhost.md|Warhost]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, just after an Asuryani Infantry unit from your army (excluding Aircraft, [[factions/艾达灵族/units/asurmen.md|Asurmen]] and Wraith Construct units) has shot.

## 使用对象

That ASURYANI unit.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to D6+1".

## 限制

Until the end of the turn, your unit is not eligible to declare a charge or embark within a Transport.
