1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/guardian-battlehost.md|Guardian Battlehost]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit destroys a [[factions/艾达灵族/units/dire-avengers.md|Dire Avengers]] or Guardians unit from your army.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/war-walkers.md|War Walkers]] unit from your army.

## 效果

After that enemy unit has finished making its attacks, your unit can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase, but when resolving those attacks, it can only target that enemy unit (and only if it is an eligible target).

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle round.
