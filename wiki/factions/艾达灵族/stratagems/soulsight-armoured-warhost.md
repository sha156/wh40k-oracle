1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/armoured-warhost.md|Armoured Warhost]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly AELDARI VEHICLE unit is selected to shoot.

## 使用对象

That AELDARI VEHICLE unit.

## 效果

Your unit’s attacks can re-roll:
▪ One hit roll.
▪ One wound roll.
▪ One damage roll.
