1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/eldritch-raiders.md|骇人劫掠者]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Anhrathe, [[factions/艾达灵族/units/rangers.md|Rangers]] or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/shroud-runners.md|Shroud Runners]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is less than the highest Toughness characteristic of models in the target unit, add 1 to the Wound roll.
