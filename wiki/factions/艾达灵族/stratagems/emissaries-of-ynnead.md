1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/devoted-of-ynnead.md|Devoted of Ynnead]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a Ynnari Infantry unit from your army has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

That YNNARI INFANTRY unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1. If your unit is below its Starting Strength, you can re-roll the Hit roll instead.
