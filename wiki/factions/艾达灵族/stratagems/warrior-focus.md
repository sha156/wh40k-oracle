1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/aspect-host.md|Aspect Host]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Aspect Warriors or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/avatar-of-khaine.md|Avatar of Khaine]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, you can ignore any or all modifiers to that attack’s Ballistic Skill, Weapon skill, Strength, Armour Penetration and Damage characteristics and/or any or all modifiers to the Hit roll.
