1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/guardian-battlehost.md|Guardian Battlehost]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/dire-avengers.md|Dire Avengers]] or Guardians unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

If your unit is within range of one or more objective markers, until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.
