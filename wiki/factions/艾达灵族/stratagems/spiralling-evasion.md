1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/windrider-host.md|Windrider Host]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Asuryani Mounted or VYPER unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
