1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/eldritch-raiders.md|骇人劫掠者]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Aeldari Infantry unit from your army (excluding Wraith Construct units) that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability.
