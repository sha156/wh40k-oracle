1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/aspect-host.md|Aspect Host]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Aspect Warriors or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/avatar-of-khaine.md|Avatar of Khaine]] unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Each time you use this Stratagem, you can remove one Aspect Shrine token your unit has (see datasheets). Then, until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit is destroyed, if that model has not fought this phase, roll one D6, adding 1 to the result if you removed an Aspect Shrine token during this usage of this Stratagem. On a 4+, do not remove the destroyed model from play; it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play.
