## 使用时机

When an eligible unit from your army is selected to make a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move. You can trigger this Agile Manoeuvre more than once per phase (provided a different unit performs it each time).

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 2" to the Move characteristic of models in that unit.
