1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/spirit-conclave.md|Spirit Conclave]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithblades.md|Wraithblades]], [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithlord.md|Wraithlord]] or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithknight.md|Wraithknight]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] ability.
