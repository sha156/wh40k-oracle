1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/protector-host.md|Protector Host]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit opens a Hatchway.

## 使用对象

One DIRE AvENGERS or GUARDIANS unit from your army that has not fired Overwatch this phase and is wholly on the opposite side of that Hatchway from that enemy unit.

## 效果

If that enemy unit is within 9" of and visible to your unit, roll one D6: on a 2-5, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound; on a 6, that enemy unit suffers 2 mortal wounds. If that enemy unit suffers one or more mortal wounds in this way, your unit can then make a Normal move of up to D6”.

## 限制

Until the end of the turn, your unit cannot fire Overwatch.
