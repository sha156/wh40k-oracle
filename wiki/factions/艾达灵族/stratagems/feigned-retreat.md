1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/warhost.md|Warhost]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an Asuryani unit from your army Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That ASURYANI unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back.
