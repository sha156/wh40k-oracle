1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/path-of-the-outcast.md|Path of the Outcast]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly RANGERS/SHROUD RUNNERS unit has shot.

## 使用对象

That RANGERS/SHROUD RUNNERS unit.

## 效果

▪ Your unit can make a normal move of up to D6".
▪ Your unit is not eligible to declare a charge or embark within a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] until the end of the turn.
