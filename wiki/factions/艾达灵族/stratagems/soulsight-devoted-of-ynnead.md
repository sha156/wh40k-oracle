1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/devoted-of-ynnead.md|Devoted of Ynnead]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Ynnari unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [LETHAL HITS] and [IGNORES COVER] abilities.
