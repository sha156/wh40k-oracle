1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/guardian-battlehost.md|Guardian Battlehost]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/dire-avengers.md|Dire Avengers]] or Guardians unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [PISTOL] ability.
