1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/warhost.md|Warhost]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Asuryani Infantry unit from your army that is wholly within 9" of one or more battlefield edges.

## 效果

If your unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, remove it from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
