1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/spirit-conclave.md|Spirit Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithblades.md|Wraithblades]], [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithguard.md|Wraithguard]] or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithlord.md|Wraithlord]] unit from your army and one Asuryani Psyker model from your army.

## 效果

Until the start of your next Command phase, your WRAITHBLADES, WRAITHGUARD or WRAITHLORD unit is considered to be within 12" of your [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] model for the purposes of the Psychic Guidance and Spirit Guides abilities.
