1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/corsair-coterie.md|Corsair Coterie]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/rangers.md|Rangers]] or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/shroud-runners.md|Shroud Runners]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [IGNORES COVER] and [RAPID FIRE 1] abilities, and until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of those weapons by 1.
