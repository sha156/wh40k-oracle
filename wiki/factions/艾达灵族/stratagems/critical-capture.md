1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/khaines-arrow.md|Khaine’s Arrow]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One AELDARI unit from your army (excluding CHARAcTER units).

## 效果

Until the start of your next Command phase, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in your unit. In addition, at the end of your turn, select one objective marker you control that your unit is within range of. That objective marker is Secured by your army.
