1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/eldritch-raiders.md|Eldritch Raiders]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Anhrathe unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves. If that unit is below Starting Strength, return all destroyed models (excluding [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] models) to that unit.
