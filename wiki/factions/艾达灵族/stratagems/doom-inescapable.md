Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/avatar-of-khaine.md|Avatar of Khaine]] model from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your model’s Wailing Doom ranged weapon has a Range characteristic of 18" and a Damage characteristic of 8.
