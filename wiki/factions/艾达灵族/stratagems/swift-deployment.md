1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/armoured-warhost.md|Armoured Warhost]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Aeldari Transport unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, units can disembark from your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] after it has Advanced. Units that do so count as having made a Normal move that phase, and cannot declare a charge in the same turn, but can otherwise act normally.
