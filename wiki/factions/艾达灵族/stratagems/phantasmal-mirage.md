1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/twilight-flickers.md|Twilight Flickers]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly HARLEQUINS VEHICLE unit has shot.

## 使用对象

That HARLEQUINS VEHICLE unit.

## 效果

▪ Your unit can make a normal move of up to D6".
▪ Your unit is not eligible to declare a charge until the end of the turn.
