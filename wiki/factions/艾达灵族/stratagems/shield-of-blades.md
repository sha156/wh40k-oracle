1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/protector-host.md|Protector Host]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement or Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One DIRE AvENGERS or GUARDIAN DEfENDERS unit from your army that has not fired Overwatch this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack while firing Overwatch, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ is required to score a hit, instead of an unmodified 6.
