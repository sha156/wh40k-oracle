1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/seer-council.md|Seer Council]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Asuryani Infantry unit from your army (excluding Wraith Construct units) that has not been selected to move this phase, was not set up on the battlefield this phase, and is within 8" of one or more friendly Asuryani Psyker models.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and set it up again anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 9" horizontally away from all enemy models.

## 限制

Until the end of the phase, your unit is not eligible to be selected to move.
