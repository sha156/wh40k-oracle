1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/eldritch-raiders.md|Eldritch Raiders]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your opponent’s Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/艾达灵族/units/rangers.md|Rangers]], [[factions/艾达灵族/units/shroud-runners.md|Shroud Runners]] or Starfang unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] units) visible to and within 36" of your unit. Until the end of the phase, each time that enemy unit declares a charge, subtract 2 from the Charge roll (this is not cumulative with any other negative modifiers to that Charge roll).
