1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/devoted-of-ynnead.md|Devoted of Ynnead]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One Ynnari unit from your army that was just destroyed while it was within range of one or more objective markers you controlled at the end of the previous phase. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Select one of those objective markers. That objective marker remains under your control until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase.
