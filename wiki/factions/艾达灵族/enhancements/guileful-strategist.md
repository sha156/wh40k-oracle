15 分、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/armoured-warhost.md|装甲战群]] 分队。

## 效果

If your army includes the bearer, after both players have deployed their armies, select up to three Aeldari Vehicle units from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, any of those units can be placed into Strategic Reserves, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

AELDARI model only.
