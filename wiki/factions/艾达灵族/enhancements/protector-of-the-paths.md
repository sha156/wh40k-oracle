20 分、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/guardian-battlehost.md|Guardian Battlehost]] 分队。

## 效果

While the bearer is leading a [[factions/艾达灵族/units/dire-avengers.md|Dire Avengers]] or Guardians unit, once per battle round, you can target the bearer’s unit with the Fire Overwatch Stratagem for 0CP, and while resolving that Stratagem, hits are scored on unmodified Hit rolls of 5+, or unmodified Hit rolls of 4+ instead if the bearer’s unit is within range of an objective marker you control.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Asuryani model only.
