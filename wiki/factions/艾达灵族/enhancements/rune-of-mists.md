10 分、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/spirit-conclave.md|Spirit Conclave]] 分队。

## 效果

In your Command phase, select one friendly Wraith Construct unit within 12" of the bearer. Until the start of your next Command phase, each time a ranged attack targets that unit, unless the attacking model is within 18", models in that unit have the Benefit of Cover against that attack.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

[[factions/艾达灵族/units/spiritseer.md|Spiritseer]] model only.
