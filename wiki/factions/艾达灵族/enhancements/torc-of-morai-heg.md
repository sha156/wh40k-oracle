20 分、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/seer-council.md|Seer Council]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per turn, when your opponent targets a unit from their army within 12" of the bearer with a Stratagem, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, increase the CP cost of that usage of that Stratagem by 1CP.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Asuryani Psyker model only.
