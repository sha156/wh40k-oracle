25 分、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/corsair-coterie.md|海盗劫掠团]] 分队。

## 效果

Models in this unit have the Deep Strike ability. In addition, once per battle, at the end of your opponent’s turn, if this unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can use this ability. If it does, remove this unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Anhrathe unit only.
