15 分、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/ghosts-of-the-webway.md|Ghosts of the Webway]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) within Engagement Range of the bearer’s unit Falls Back, all models in that enemy unit must take a Desperate Escape test. When doing so, if that enemy unit is Battle-shocked, subtract 1 from each of those tests.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Harlequins model only.
