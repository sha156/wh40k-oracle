35 分、[[factions/艾达灵族/detachments/warhost.md|Warhost]] 分队。

## 效果

The first time the bearer is destroyed, remove it from play, then, at the end of the phase, roll one D6: on a 2+, set the bearer back up on the battlefield as close as possible to where it was destroyed and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, with its full wounds remaining.

**分数**：35 分

## 携带限制

Asuryani model only.
