艾达灵族的分队「Spirit Conclave」，分队规则「Shepherds of the Dead」。

## 分队规则

### Shepherds of the Dead

Each time an Asuryani Psyker model from your army is destroyed by an enemy unit, that enemy unit gains a Vengeful Dead token. Each time a Wraith Construct model from your army makes an attack that targets a unit with one or more Vengeful Dead tokens, add 1 to the Hit roll and add 1 to the Wound roll.

ASURYANI PSYKER models from your army have the following ability:

**Spirit Guides (Aura):** While a [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithblades.md|Wraithblades]], [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithguard.md|Wraithguard]] or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/wraithlord.md|Wraithlord]] unit from your army is within 12" of this model, that unit has the Battle Focus ability.

KEYWORDS
WRAITHBLADES and WRAITHGUARD units from your army gain the [[core-rules/battleline.md|BATTLELINE]] keyword.
