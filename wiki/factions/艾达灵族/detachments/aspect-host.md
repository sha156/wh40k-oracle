艾达灵族的分队，分队规则「Path of the Warrior」。

## 分队规则

### Path of the Warrior

Each time an Aspect Warriors or [[factions/艾达灵族/units/avatar-of-khaine.md|Avatar of Khaine]] unit from your army is selected to shoot or fight, select one of the following abilities for it to gain until the end of the phase:

- Each time a model in this unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1.
- Each time a model in this unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1.
