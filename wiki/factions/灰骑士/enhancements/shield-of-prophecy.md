## 效果

Once per battle, at the start of the battle round, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of the battle round, add 2 to the Toughness characteristic of models in the bearer’s unit.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

GREY KNIGHTS model only.
