15 分、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/augurium-task-force.md|Augurium Task Force]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time the bearer’s unit is set up in your Reinforcements step, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, the bearer’s unit can make a Normal move of up to D6", and until the end of the turn, the bearer’s unit is not eligible to declare a Charge.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

GREY KNIGHTS model only.
