## 效果

The bearer’s melee weapons have the [PRECISION] ability, and while the bearer is wholly within your army’s Hallowed Ground, each time a melee attack made by the bearer scores a wound, the target of that attack suffers 1 mortal wound in addition to any normal damage.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Grey Knights Infantry model only.
