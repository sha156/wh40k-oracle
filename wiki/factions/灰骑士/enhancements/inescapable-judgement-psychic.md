20 分、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/hallowed-conclave.md|Hallowed Conclave]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time an enemy unit within Engagement Range of the bearer’s unit Falls Back, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, roll one D6: on a 2-5, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 6, that enemy unit suffers D3+3 mortal wounds. These mortal wounds are Psychic Attacks.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

GREY KNIGHTS model only.
