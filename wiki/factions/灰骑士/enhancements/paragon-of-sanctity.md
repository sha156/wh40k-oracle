## 效果

Once per battle, at the start of any phase, the bearer can select one friendly GREY KNIGHTS unit within 18" of and visible to it. If it does, until the end of the phase, that unit is within your army’s Hallowed Ground.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

GREY KNIGHTS model only.
