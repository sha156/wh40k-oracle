1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/void-purge-force.md|Void Purge Force]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One GREY KNIGHTS unit from your army that charged this turn.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability. If such a weapon already has that ability, until the end of the phase, each time an attack is made with that weapon, a successful unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a Critical Hit.
