1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/warpbane-task-force.md|次元灾星特遣队]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/灰骑士/units/purifier-squad.md|Purifier Squad]] unit from your army that was eligible to fight this phase.

## 效果

Roll one D6 for each enemy unit within 6" of your unit, adding 1 to the result if your unit includes [[factions/灰骑士/units/castellan-crowe.md|Castellan Crowe]]: on a 4+, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.
