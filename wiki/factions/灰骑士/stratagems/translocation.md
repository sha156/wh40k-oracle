1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/void-purge-force.md|Void Purge Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One INTERcEPTOR SqUAD or GREY KNIGHTS CHARAcTER unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

The next time your unit is selected to move this phase, models in that unit can move through Walls and closed Hatchways as if they were not there, but when doing so, no model can move more than 6".

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once.
