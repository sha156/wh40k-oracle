1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/void-purge-force.md|Void Purge Force]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One GREY KNIGHTS unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in that enemy unit have the [HAZARDOUS] ability.
