1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/fires-of-purgation.md|Fires of Purgation]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly PURGATION SQUAD unit has shot.

## 使用对象

That PURGATION SQUAD unit.

## 效果

Select one battle-shocked enemy unit hit by those attacks. That enemy unit suffers D3+1 mortal wounds.
