1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/baneslayer-strike.md|Baneslayer Strike]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One GREY KNIGHTS CHARAcTER model from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your model has the Feel No Pain 4+ ability.
