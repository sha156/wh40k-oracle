1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/灰骑士/detachments/warpbane-task-force.md|次元灾星特遣队]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One GREY KNIGHTS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase and that is wholly within your army’s Hallowed Ground.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1, or, re-roll the Wound roll instead if your unit is a [[factions/灰骑士/units/purifier-squad.md|Purifier Squad]].
