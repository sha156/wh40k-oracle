## 效果

At the start of your Shooting phase, select one other IMPERIAL KNIGHTS model from your army that is within 12" of and visible to the bearer. Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by that selected model have the [ignores cover] ability.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制
