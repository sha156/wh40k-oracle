25 分、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/valourstrike-lance.md|Valourstrike Lance]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of your Charge phase, select one other IMPERIAL KNIGHTS model from your army that is within 12" of and visible to the bearer. Until the end of the phase, you can re-roll Charge rolls made for that selected model.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

IMPERIAL KNIGHTS model only.
