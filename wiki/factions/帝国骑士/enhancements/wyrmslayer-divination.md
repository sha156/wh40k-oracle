## 效果

In your Shooting phase, when the bearer is selected to shoot, it can use this Enhancement. If it does, this Enhancement is expended, then until the end of the phase, each time it makes an attack that targets a unit that can FLY, you can re-roll the Hit roll.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制
