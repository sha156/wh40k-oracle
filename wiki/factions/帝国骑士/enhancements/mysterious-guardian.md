35 分、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/freeblade-company.md|自由之刃连队]] 分队。

## 效果

The bearer has the Deep Strike ability. Once per battle, at the end of your opponent’s turn, if the bearer is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can use this Enhancement. If it does, remove the bearer from the battlefield. In the Reinforcements step of your subsequent Movement phase, set the bearer up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 9" horizontally away from all enemy units. If the battle ends and the bearer is not on the battlefield, it is destroyed.

**分数**：35 分

## 携带限制

IMPERIAL KNIGHTS model only.
