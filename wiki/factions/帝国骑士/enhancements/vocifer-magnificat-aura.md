## 效果

While an enemy unit is within 6" of the bearer, worsen the Leadership characteristic of models in that unit by 1. While a friendly Adeptus Mechanicus unit is within 6" of the bearer, improve the Leadership characteristic of models in that ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit by 1.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

IMPERIAL KNIGHTS model only.
