20 分、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/valourstrike-lance.md|Valourstrike Lance]] 分队。

## 效果

At the end of your Movement phase, select one other IMPERIAL KNIGHTS model from your army that is within 12" of and visible to the bearer. That selected model regains up to D3 lost wounds, or up to 3 lost wounds if your army is Honoured.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

IMPERIAL KNIGHTS model only.
