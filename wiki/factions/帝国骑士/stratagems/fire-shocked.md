1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/dominus-foebreakers.md|Dominus Foebreakers]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly IMPERIAL KNIGHTS DOMINUS unit has shot.

## 使用对象

That IMPERIAL KNIGHTS DOMINUS unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by those attacks. That enemy unit makes a battle-shock roll, with -1 to that battle-shock roll.
