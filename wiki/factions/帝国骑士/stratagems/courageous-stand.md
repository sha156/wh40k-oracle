1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/questoris-companions.md|Questoris Companions]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Imperial Knights Titanic unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 6+ ability.
