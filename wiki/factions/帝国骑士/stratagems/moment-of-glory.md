1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/questoris-companions.md|Questoris Companions]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just before an Imperial Knights Titanic unit from your army Consolidates.

## 使用对象

That IMPERIAL KNIGHTS TITANIC unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit Consolidates, models in it can move an additional 3" provided your unit can end that move within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
