2 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/questoris-companions.md|Questoris Companions]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an Imperial Knights Titanic unit from your army Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That IMPERIAL KNIGHTS TITANIC unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back.
