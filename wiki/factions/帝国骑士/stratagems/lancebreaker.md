1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/gate-warden-lance.md|Gate Warden Lance]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Imperial Knights unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks and is on your defensive line.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is greaterthan the Toughness characteristic of your unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll
