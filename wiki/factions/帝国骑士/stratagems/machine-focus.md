1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/questor-forgepact.md|Questor Forgepact]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Imperial Knights unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the start of your next turn, your unit
can ignore any or all modifiers to the following: the Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill characteristics of weapons equipped by its models; any Hit roll or Wound roll made for it.
