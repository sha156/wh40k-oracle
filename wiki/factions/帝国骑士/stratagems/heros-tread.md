1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/帝国骑士/detachments/questoris-companions.md|Questoris Companions]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Imperial Knights Titanic model from your army that is within range of an objective marker you control.

## 效果

Your Level of Control over that objective marker is 5 (unless it would otherwise be higher), until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase.
