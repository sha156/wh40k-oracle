15 分、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/daemonic-incursion.md|Daemonic Incursion]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time the bearer destroys an enemy model with a melee attack, roll one D6, adding 1 to the result if the bearer is within your army’s Shadow of Chaos. On a 4+, the bearer regains 1 lost wound.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Slaanesh Legiones Daemonica model only.
