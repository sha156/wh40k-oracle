20 分、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/blood-legion.md|Blood Legion]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time the bearer is selected to fight, it can use this Enhancement. If it does, the bearer suffers D3+1 mortal wounds, and until the end of the phase, each time it makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Legiones Daemonica Khorne Monster model only.
