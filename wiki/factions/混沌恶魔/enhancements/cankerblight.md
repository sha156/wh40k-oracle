15 分、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/plague-legion.md|Plague Legion]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) that is within 6" of the bearer fails a Battle-shock test, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, one model in that unit is destroyed (chosen by its controlling player). Each time the bearer uses this Enhancement, no mortal wounds are inflicted by your army’s Daemonic Terror rule.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Legiones Daemonica Nurgle model only.
