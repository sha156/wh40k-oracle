25 分、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/daemonic-incursion.md|恶魔入侵]] 分队。

## 效果

Add 1 to the Strength characteristic of the bearer’s ranged weapons and increase the Range characteristic of such weapons by 3". While the bearer is within your army’s Shadow of Chaos, add 2 to the Strength characteristic of the bearer’s ranged weapons and increase the Range characteristic of such weapons by 6" instead.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Tzeentch Legiones Daemonica model only.
