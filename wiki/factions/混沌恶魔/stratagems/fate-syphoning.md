## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One TzEENTcH LEGIONES DAEMONIcA unit from your army that is within range of an objective marker and was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, you can re-roll saving throws of 1 made for models in your unit.
