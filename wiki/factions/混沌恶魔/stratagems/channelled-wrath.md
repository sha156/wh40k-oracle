1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/shadow-legion.md|暗影军团]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/shadow-legion.md|Shadow Legion]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [LANCE] ability. If your unit has the Khorne keyword, until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of those weapons by 1 as well.
