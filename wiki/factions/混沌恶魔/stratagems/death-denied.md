1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/shadow-legion.md|Shadow Legion]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Shadow Legion unit from your army.

## 效果

One model in your unit regains up to 3 lost wounds. In addition, If your unit has the Tzeentch keyword, return up to one destroyed model (excluding Character models) to your unit with its full wounds remaining.
