1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/daemonic-incursion.md|恶魔入侵]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One LEGIONES DAEMONICA unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of weapons equipped by models in that unit by 1. In addition, until the end of the phase, each time such a weapon targets a unit that is Battle-shocked, you can re-roll the Wound roll.
