1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/plague-legion.md|Plague Legion]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Legiones Daemonica Nurgle unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a move, it can move through terrain features.
