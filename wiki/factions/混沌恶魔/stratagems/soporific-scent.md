1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/dread-carnival.md|Dread Carnival]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One SLAANESH LEGIONES DAEMONIcA unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, Desperate Escape tests taken for models in your unit are automatically passed and your unit does not suffer mortal wounds from the Lithe Killers Detachment rule.
