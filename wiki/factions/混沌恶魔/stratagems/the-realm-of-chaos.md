1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/daemonic-incursion.md|恶魔入侵]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s turn.

## 使用对象

Up to two LEGIONES DAEMONICA units from your army that are within your army’s Shadow of Chaos, or one other LEGIONES DAEMONICA unit from your army.

## 效果

Remove the targeted units from the battlefield and place them into Strategic Reserves. They will arrive back on the battlefield in the Reinforcements step of your next Movement phase using the Deep Strike ability.

## 限制

You cannot target units that are within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units with this Stratagem.
