1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/warptide.md|Warptide]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly LEGIONES DAEMONICA BATTLELINE unit.

## 使用对象

That LEGIONES DAEMONICA BATTLELINE unit.

## 效果

Ranged attacks that target your unit with a Strength characteristic greater than your unit’s Toughness characteristic have -1 to Wound rolls.
