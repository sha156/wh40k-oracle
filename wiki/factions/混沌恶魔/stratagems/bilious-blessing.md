## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One friendly LEGIONES DAEMONICA CHARACTER NURGLE unit (excluding MONSTER units).

## 效果

Select one visible enemy unit within 8" of your unit and roll seven D6: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
