1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/plague-legion.md|瘟疫军团]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Legiones Daemonica Nurgle Infantry unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit makes a Normal move, add 5" to the Move characteristic of models in your unit.
