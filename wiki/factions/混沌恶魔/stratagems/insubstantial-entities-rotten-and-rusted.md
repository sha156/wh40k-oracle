1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/rotten-and-rusted.md|Rotten and Rusted]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One NURGLE LEGIONES DAEMONIcA unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

The next time your unit is selected to move this phase, models in that unit can move through Walls and closed Hatchways as if they were not there, but when doing so, no model can move more than 6".

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once.
