1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/blood-legion.md|Blood Legion]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Legiones Daemonica Khorne unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a Save characteristic of 3+.
