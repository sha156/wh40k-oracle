1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/混沌恶魔/detachments/plague-legion.md|瘟疫军团]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Command phase, before selecting any targets for the Melancholic Miasma Detachment rule.

## 使用对象

One Legiones Daemonica Nurgle unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, after an enemy unit takes a Battle-shock test as a result of the Melancholic Miasma Detachment rule, select one other enemy unit within 9" of your unit; that enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test.
