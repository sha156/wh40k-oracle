1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/slaaneshs-chosen.md|Slaanesh’s Chosen]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an Emperor’s Children Character unit becomes your army’s Favoured Champions.

## 使用对象

That EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within 6" of your unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from the result.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle round.
