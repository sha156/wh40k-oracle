1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/carnival-of-excess.md|Carnival of Excess]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an Emperor’s Children unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit.

## 效果

Select one friendly Legions of Excess unit within 6" of your unit. One model in that LEGIONS OF EXCESS unit recovers up to 3 lost wounds or, if it is a [[factions/帝皇之子/units/daemonettes.md|Daemonettes]] unit, return upto D3+3 destroyed models to it instead.
