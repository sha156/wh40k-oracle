1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/slaaneshs-chosen.md|Slaanesh’s Chosen]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children Character unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Your unit can make a surge move of up to D6". If your unit is not your army’s Favoured Champions, you can re-roll the dice to determine the distance of that surge move.
