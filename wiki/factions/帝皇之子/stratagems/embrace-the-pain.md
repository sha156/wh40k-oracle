1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/coterie-of-the-conceited.md|Coterie of the Conceited]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children Infantry unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an enemy model within Engagement Range of your unit selects targets, it must select your unit as the target of its attacks.
