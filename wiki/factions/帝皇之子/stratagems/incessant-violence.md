1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/peerless-bladesmen.md|Peerless Bladesmen]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just before an Emperor’s Children unit from your army Consolidates.

## 使用对象

That EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Consolidation move, it can move up to 6" instead of up to 3", provided your unit ends that Consolidation move within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
