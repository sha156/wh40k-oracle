1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/rapid-evisceration.md|Rapid Evisceration]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children Transport from your army that has one or more [[factions/帝皇之子/units/tormentors.md|Tormentors]] units embarked within it (excluding Battle-shocked units).

## 效果

Select one objective marker you control that your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] is within range of. That objective marker remains under your control until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase.
