1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/coterie-of-the-conceited.md|Coterie of the Conceited]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an Emperor’s Children unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit.

## 效果

Increase your pledge to Slaanesh by 1.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle round.
