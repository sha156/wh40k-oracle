1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/carnival-of-excess.md|Carnival of Excess]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Slaanesh unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
