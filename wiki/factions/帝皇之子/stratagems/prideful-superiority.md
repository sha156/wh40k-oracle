1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/court-of-the-phoenician.md|凤凰王庭]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.
