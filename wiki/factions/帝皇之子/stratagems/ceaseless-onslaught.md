## 使用时机

Your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army that disembarked from a Transport that made a Normal move this turn.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit is eligible to declare a charge.
