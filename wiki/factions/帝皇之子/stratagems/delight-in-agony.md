1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/elegant-brutes.md|Elegant Brutes]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly EMPEROR’S CHILDREN TERMINATOR unit.

## 使用对象

That EMPEROR’S CHILDREN TERMINATOR unit.

## 效果

Attacks that target your unit with a Strength greater than your unit’s Toughness have -1 to wound rolls.
