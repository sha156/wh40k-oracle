1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/slaaneshs-chosen.md|Slaanesh’s Chosen]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an Emperor’s Children Character unit becomes your army’s Favoured Champions, or just after your army’s Favoured Champions unit destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

Your army’s Favoured Champions unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in an EMPEROR’S CHILDREN CHARACTER unit from your army that is not your army’s Favoured Champions makes an attack, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of that attack.
