1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/slaaneshs-chosen.md|Slaanesh’s Chosen]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children Character unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit is destroyed, if that model has not fought this phase, roll one D6, adding 1 to the result if your unit is your army’s Favoured Champions. On a 4+, do not remove the destroyed model from play; it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play.
