1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/slaaneshs-chosen.md|Slaanesh’s Chosen]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One or more Emperor’s Children Character units from your army that have not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit. Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in those [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] units have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability while targeting that enemy unit.
