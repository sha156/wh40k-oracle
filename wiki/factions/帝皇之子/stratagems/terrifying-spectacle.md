1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/peerless-bladesmen.md|Peerless Bladesmen]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army that made a Charge move in the previous turn and destroyed one or more enemy units in the previous Fight phase.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within 6" of your unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from that test if they are Below Half-strength. Enemy units affected by this Stratagem do not need to take any other Battle-shock tests in the same phase.
