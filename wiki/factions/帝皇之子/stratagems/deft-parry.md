1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/peerless-bladesmen.md|Peerless Bladesmen]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
