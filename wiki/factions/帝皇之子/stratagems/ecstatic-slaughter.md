1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/carnival-of-excess.md|Carnival of Excess]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a LEGIONS OF EXCESS unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.

## 使用对象

That Legions OF Excess unit and one friendly Emperor’s Children unit within 6" of it that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Your EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit can declare a charge. If it does so and it has already been selected to fight this phase, it cannot fight again this phase.
