1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/peerless-bladesmen.md|Peerless Bladesmen]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army that made a Charge move this turn and has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1.
