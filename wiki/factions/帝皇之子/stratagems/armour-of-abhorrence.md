1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/coterie-of-the-conceited.md|Coterie of the Conceited]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, each time an attack targets your unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
