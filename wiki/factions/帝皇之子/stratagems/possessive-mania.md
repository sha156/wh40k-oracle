1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/frenzied-host.md|Frenzied Host]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly EMPEROR’S CHILDREN BATTLELINE unit within range of an objective.

## 使用对象

That EMPEROR’S CHILDREN BATTLELINE unit.

## 效果

Attacks that target your unit have -1 AP until that enemy unit has attacked.
