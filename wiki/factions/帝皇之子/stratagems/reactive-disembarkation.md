1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/rapid-evisceration.md|Rapid Evisceration]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children Transport from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

One Emperor’s Children unit embarked within your [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] can disembark. When doing so, models in that unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 6" of that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
