1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/mercurial-host.md|Mercurial Host]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army that is wholly within 9" of one or more battlefield edges and not within 3" horizontally of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
