2 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/carnival-of-excess.md|Carnival of Excess]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/帝皇之子/units/daemonettes.md|Daemonettes]] unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves. If it arrives back on the battlefield in the Reinforcements step of your next Movement phase using the Deep Strike ability, it can be set up anywhere that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units (instead of more than 9"), provided it is also set up wholly within 9" of one or more friendly Emperor’s Children units.
