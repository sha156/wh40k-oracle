2 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/peerless-bladesmen.md|Peerless Bladesmen]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit Falls Back.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children unit from your army (you can only select a Vehicle if it is a Walker) that is within 6" of that enemy unit and would be eligible to declare a charge against that enemy unit if it were your Charge phase.

## 效果

Your unit can declare a charge. When doing so, you must select that enemy unit as a target of that charge, and your unit does not receive a Charge bonus this turn.
