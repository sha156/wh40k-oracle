1 CP、Movement or Charge phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/court-of-the-phoenician.md|Court of the Phoenician]] 分队、Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Emperor’s Children Daemon unit from your army that has not been selected to move or charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit makes a Normal, Advance or Charge move, it can move horizontally through terrain features.
