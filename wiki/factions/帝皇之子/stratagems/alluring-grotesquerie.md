1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/sublime-strike.md|Sublime Strike]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit from your army.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit must take a Battle-shock test. Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
