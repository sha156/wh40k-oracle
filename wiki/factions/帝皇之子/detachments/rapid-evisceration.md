帝皇之子的分队，分队规则「机械化屠戮」。

## 分队规则

### 机械化屠戮 Mechanised Murder

Each time an Emperor’s Children model from your army makes an attack, if it is a Transport model or disembarked from a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] this turn, re-roll a Hit roll of 1 and re-roll a Wound roll of 1.
