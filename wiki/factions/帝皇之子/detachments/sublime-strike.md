帝皇之子的分队，分队规则「Dark Radiance」。

## 分队规则

### Dark Radiance

Each time an EMPEROR’S CHILDREN unit from your army makes a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move, models in that unit can move through friendly and enemy models.
