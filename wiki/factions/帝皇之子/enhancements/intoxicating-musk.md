20 分、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/mercurial-host.md|Mercurial Host]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time a melee attack targets the bearer’s unit, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is greater than the Toughness characteristic of that unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children model only.
