## 效果

Once per battle, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of the phase, the bearer has a 3+ invulnerable save.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children Infantry model only.
