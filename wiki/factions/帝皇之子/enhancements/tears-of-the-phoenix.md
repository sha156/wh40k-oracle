## 效果

Each time a model in the bearer’s unit makes a melee attack, you can ignore any or all modifiers to that attack’s Weapon Skill characteristic and any or all modifiers to the Hit roll and Wound roll.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

EMPEROR’S CHILDREN model only.
