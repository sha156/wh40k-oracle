30 分、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/coterie-of-the-conceited.md|Coterie of the Conceited]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per turn, just after making a Hit roll, a Wound roll or a saving throw for a model in the bearer’s unit, if the bearer is not Battle-shocked, it can use this Enhancement. If it does, treat the result as an unmodified roll of 6 instead.

**分数**：30 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children model only.
