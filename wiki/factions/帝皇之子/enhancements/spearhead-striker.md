20 分、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/rapid-evisceration.md|Rapid Evisceration]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time the bearer disembarks from a Transport, until the end of the turn, you can re-roll Charge rolls made for the bearer’s unit and enemy units cannot use the Fire Overwatch Stratagem to shoot at the bearer’s unit.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children Infantry model only.
