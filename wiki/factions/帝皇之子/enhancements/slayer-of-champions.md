## 效果

The bearer’s melee weapons have the [PRECISION] ability, and each time the bearer makes a melee attack that targets a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit, improve the Strength and Armour Penetration characteristics of that attack by 1.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children model only.
