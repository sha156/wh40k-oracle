25 分、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/slaaneshs-chosen.md|Slaanesh’s Chosen]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) within Engagement Range of the bearer’s unit Falls Back, models in that enemy unit must take Desperate Escape tests. When doing so, if the bearer’s unit is your army’s Favoured Champions, subtract 1 from each of those Desperate Escape tests.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children model only.
