15 分、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/mercurial-host.md|Mercurial Host]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, select up to two EMPEROR’S CHILDREN units from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in Strategic Reserves if you wish, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children model only.
