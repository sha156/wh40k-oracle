## 效果

You can re-roll Battle-shock and Leadership tests taken for the bearer’s unit. While the bearer’s unit is your army’s Favoured Champions, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in that unit.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children model only.
