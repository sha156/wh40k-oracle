35 分、[[factions/帝皇之子/detachments/rapid-evisceration.md|Rapid Evisceration]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle round, when a saving throw is failed for a friendly Emperor’s Children Vehicle model within 6" of the bearer, you can change the Damage characteristic of that attack to 0.

**分数**：35 分

## 携带限制

Emperor’s Children Infantry model only.
