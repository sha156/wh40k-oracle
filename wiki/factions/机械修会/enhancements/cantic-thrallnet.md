25 分、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/skitarii-hunter-cohort.md|Skitarii Hunter Cohort]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of the battle round, you can select one friendly SKITARII unit within 12" of the bearer. Until the start of the next battle round, the Protector Imperative and the Conqueror Imperative are both active for that unit.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

SKITARII MARSHAL model only.
