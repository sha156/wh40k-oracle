10 分、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/skitarii-hunter-cohort.md|Skitarii Hunter Cohort]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, you can select up to three SKITARII INFANTRY units from your army and redeploy all of those units. When doing so, any of those units can be placed into Strategic Reserves, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

SKITARII MARSHAL model only.
