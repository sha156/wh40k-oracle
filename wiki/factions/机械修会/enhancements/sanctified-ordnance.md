## 效果

Add 6" to the range characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in the bearer’s unit and, each time a Hazardous test is taken for that unit, you can re-roll the result.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS MECHANICUS model only.
