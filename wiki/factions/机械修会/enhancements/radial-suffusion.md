25 分、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/rad-zone-corps.md|Rad-Zone Corps]] 分队。

## 效果

From the second battle round onwards, when resolving the Fallout effect of the Rad-bombardment Detachment rule, if the bearer is on the battlefield, roll one D6 for each enemy unit within 6" of your opponent’s deployment zone, in addition to those that are within your opponent’s deployment zone.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

ADEPTUS MECHANICUS model only.
