20 分、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/data-psalm-conclave.md|Data-Psalm Conclave]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle, at the start of your Command phase, you can select the Benediction of the Omnissiah you did not select at the start of the first battle round. Until the start of your next Command phase, that Benediction of the Omnissiah is active for the bearer’s unit in addition to the one that is currently active for your army.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

TECH-PRIEST model only.
