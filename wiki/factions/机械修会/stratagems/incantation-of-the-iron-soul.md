1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/data-psalm-conclave.md|Data-Psalm Conclave]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after you allocate a mortal wound to a CULT MECHANICUS model from your army.

## 使用对象

That CULT MECHANICUS model’s unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, CULT MECHANICUS models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 4+ ability against mortal wounds.
