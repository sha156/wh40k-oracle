1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/data-psalm-conclave.md|Data-Psalm Conclave]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One CULT MECHANICUS unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, CULT MECHANICUS models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
