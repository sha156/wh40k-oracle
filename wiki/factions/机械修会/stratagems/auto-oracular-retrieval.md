2 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/explorator-maniple.md|Explorator Maniple]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit from your army that disembarked from a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] this turn.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack that targets an enemy unit within range of your Acquisition objective marker, add 1 to the Wound roll.
