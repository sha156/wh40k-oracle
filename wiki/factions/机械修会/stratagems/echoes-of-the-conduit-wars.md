1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/luminen-auto-choir.md|启明自动合唱团]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when a friendly ELECTRO-PRIESTS unit is selected to attack.

## 使用对象

That ELECTRO-PRIESTS unit.

## 效果

Your unit’s attacks that target a unit within range of an objective can:

- Re-roll hit rolls of 1.
- Re-roll wound rolls of 1.
