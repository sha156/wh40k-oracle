1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/skitarii-hunter-cohort.md|Skitarii Hunter Cohort]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase orthe Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One SICARIAN, PTERAXII or SYDONIAN unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability.
