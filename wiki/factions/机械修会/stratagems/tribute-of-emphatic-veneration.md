1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/data-psalm-conclave.md|Data-Psalm Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One CULT MECHANICUS unit from your army and one enemy unit within 18" of it.

## 效果

That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test. If that test is failed, until the start of your next Command phase, each time a model in that enemy unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
