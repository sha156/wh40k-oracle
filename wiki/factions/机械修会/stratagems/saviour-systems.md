1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/electromartyrs.md|Electromartyrs]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit from your army (excluding KATAPHRON units) that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.
