1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/eradication-cohort.md|根除大队]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit from your army Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot in a turn in which it Fell Back. If your unit has the Skitarii keyword, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back instead.
