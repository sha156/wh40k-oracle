1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/explorator-maniple.md|Explorator Maniple]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just before an ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit from your army Consolidates.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Consolidation move, it can move up to 6" instead of up to 3", provided your unit ends that Consolidation move within range of your Acquisition objective marker.

## 限制

You cannot target a unit with this Stratagem if it is within 3" of one or more enemy units.
