## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One CULT MECHANICUS unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a CULT MECHANICUS model in your unit makes a melee attack, add 1 to the Wound roll.
