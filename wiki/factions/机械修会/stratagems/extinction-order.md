1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/rad-zone-corps.md|Rad-Zone Corps]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Tech-Priest model from your army and one objective marker within 24" of that model.

## 效果

Roll one D6 for each enemy unit within range of that objective marker. On a 4+, that unit suffers 1 mortal wound and it must take a Battle-shock test.
