1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/response-clade.md|Response Clade]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One SKITARII unit from your army that is within range of an objective marker and was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
