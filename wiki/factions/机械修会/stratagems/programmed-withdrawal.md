1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/skitarii-hunter-cohort.md|Skitarii Hunter Cohort]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two SICARIAN units from your army, or one SKITARII INFANTRY or SKITARII MOUNTED unit from your army.

## 效果

Remove those units from the battlefield and place them into Strategic Reserves.

## 限制

Each unit targeted with this Stratagem must be more than 3" away from all enemy units.
