1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/explorator-maniple.md|Explorator Maniple]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit has declared a charge.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS MECHANICUS INFANTRY
unit from your army within range of your Acquisition objective marker that was selected as a target of that charge, and one friendly ADEPTUS MECHANICUS TRANSPORT.

## 效果

Your unit can embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]].

## 限制

Every model in your unit must be within 3" of that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] and there must be sufficient transport capacity to embark the entire unit.
