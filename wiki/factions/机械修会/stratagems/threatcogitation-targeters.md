1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/eradication-cohort.md|Eradication Cohort]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Skitarii Vehicle unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a ranged attack made by a model in your unit is allocated to a MONSTER or VEHICLE model, you can re-roll the Damage roll.
