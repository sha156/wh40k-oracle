1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/machine-cult.md|Machine Cult]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One ELEcTRO-PRIESTS unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, if the attacking model is within 6” of your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
