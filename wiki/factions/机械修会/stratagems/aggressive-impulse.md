1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/haloscreed-battle-clade.md|Haloscreed Battle Clade]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/机械修会/units/skorpius-dunerider.md|Skorpius Dunerider]] model from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, each time an ADEPTUS MECHANICUS unit disembarks from that model after it has made a Normal move, that unit is still eligible to declare a charge this turn.
