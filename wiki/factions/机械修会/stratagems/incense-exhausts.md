1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/机械修会/detachments/explorator-maniple.md|Explorator Maniple]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS MECHANICUS INFANTRY unit
from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks, and one friendly ADEPTUS MECHANICUS SMOKE unit within 6" of it.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, both of those units have the Stealth ability and the Benefit of Cover.
