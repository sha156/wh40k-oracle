1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/ramship-raiders.md|Ramship Raiders]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One BOYz unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced and each time your unit Advances, do not make an Advance roll; instead, until the end of the phase, add 6" to the Move characteristic of models in your unit.
