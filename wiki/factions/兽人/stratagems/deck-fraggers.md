1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/freebooter-krew.md|海盗船团]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ORKS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit targets an [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] unit with a ranged weapon, that weapon has the [BLAST] ability.
