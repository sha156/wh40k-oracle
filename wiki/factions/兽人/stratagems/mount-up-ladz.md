1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/blitz-brigade.md|Blitz Brigade]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Orks Infantry unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, and one friendly Transport it is able to embark within.

## 效果

If your ORKS INFANTRY unit is wholly within 6" of that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]], it can embark within it.
