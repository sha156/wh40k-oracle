1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/blitz-brigade.md|闪击旅]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/battlewagon.md|Battlewagon]], [[factions/兽人/units/kill-rig.md|Kill Rig]] or [[factions/兽人/units/hunta-rig.md|Hunta Rig]] unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is greater than the Toughness characteristic of your unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.
