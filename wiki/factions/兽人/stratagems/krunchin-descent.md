1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/taktikal-brigade.md|Taktikal Brigade]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, just after a STORMBOYZ unit from your army ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

That [[factions/兽人/units/stormboyz.md|Stormboyz]] unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit, then roll one D6 for each model in your unit that is within Engagement Range of that enemy unit: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound (to a maximum of 6 mortal wounds).
