1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/bully-boyz.md|Bully Boyz]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit is selected to Fall Back.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/nobz.md|Nobz]] or [[factions/兽人/units/meganobz.md|Meganobz]] unit from your army within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.

## 效果

When that enemy unit Falls Back, all models in that enemy unit must take a Desperate Escape test. When doing so, if a Waaagh! is active for your unit, subtract 1 from each of those tests.
