1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/bully-boyz.md|Bully Boyz]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/nobz.md|Nobz]] or [[factions/兽人/units/meganobz.md|Meganobz]] unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, each time an attack targets your unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
