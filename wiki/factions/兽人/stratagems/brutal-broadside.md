1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/rollin-deff.md|Rollin' Deff]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly BATTLEWAGON unit is selected to shoot.

## 使用对象

That BATTLEWAGON unit.

## 效果

Your unit’s ranged attacks (excluding attacks made by weapons selected with Firing Deck) have [RAPID FIRE X], where X is that attack’s A.
