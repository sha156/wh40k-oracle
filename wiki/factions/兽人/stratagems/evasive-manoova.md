1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/speedwaaagh.md|Speedwaaagh!]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Speed Freeks or [[factions/兽人/units/trukk.md|Trukk]] unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
