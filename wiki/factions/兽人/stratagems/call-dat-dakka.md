1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/more-dakka.md|More Dakka!]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One ORKS unit from your army that had one or more of its models destroyed as a result of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Your unit can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase, but must target only that enemy unit when doing so, and can only do so if that enemy unit is an eligible target.
