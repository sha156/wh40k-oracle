1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/freebooter-krew.md|海盗船团]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/flash-gitz.md|Flash Gitz]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [ANTI-VEHICLE 4+] ability.
