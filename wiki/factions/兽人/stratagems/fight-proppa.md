1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/taktikal-brigade.md|Taktikal Brigade]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Orks Infantry or Orks Mounted unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Select the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] or [LETHAL HITS] ability. Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have the selected ability.
