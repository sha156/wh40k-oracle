1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/bully-boyz.md|Bully Boyz]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/nobz.md|Nobz]] or [[factions/兽人/units/meganobz.md|Meganobz]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1. If a Waaagh! is active for your unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll instead.
