1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/more-dakka.md|More Dakka!]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ORKS unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If that enemy unit is within range of an objective marker, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.
