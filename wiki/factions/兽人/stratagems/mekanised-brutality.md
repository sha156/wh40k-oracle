1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/blitz-brigade.md|闪击旅]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/battlewagon.md|Battlewagon]], [[factions/兽人/units/kill-rig.md|Kill Rig]] or [[factions/兽人/units/hunta-rig.md|Hunta Rig]] unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, each time a unit disembarks from your unit after your unit makes a Normal move, that disembarked unit is still eligible to declare a charge this turn.
