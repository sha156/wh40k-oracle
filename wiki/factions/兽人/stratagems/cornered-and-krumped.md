1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/kaptin-killers.md|Kaptin Killers]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit is selected to Fall Back.

## 使用对象

One ORKS unit from your army that is within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Your unit can fight as if it were the Fight phase, but when doing so, models in your unit can only target that enemy unit (and only if it is an eligible target) and each time a model in your unit makes an attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 6 is required to score a hit.
