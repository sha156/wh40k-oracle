1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/taktikal-brigade.md|Taktikal Brigade]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/kommandos.md|Kommandos]] or [[factions/兽人/units/stormboyz.md|Stormboyz]] unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
