1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/rollin-deff.md|死亡摇滚]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, when a friendly WAGON unit ends a charge move.

## 使用对象

That WAGON unit.

## 效果

Each enemy unit engaged with your unit makes a battle-shock roll, with -1 to that battle-shock roll.
