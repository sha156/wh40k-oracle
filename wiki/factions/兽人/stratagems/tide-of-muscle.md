1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/green-tide.md|Green Tide]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/boyz.md|Boyz]] unit from your army that has not declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit declares a charge, add 1 to the Charge roll and, if your unit contains 10 or more models, you can re-roll the Charge roll.’
