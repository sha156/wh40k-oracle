1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/bully-boyz.md|Bully Boyz]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit is destroyed.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/nobz.md|Nobz]] or [[factions/兽人/units/meganobz.md|Meganobz]] unit from your army that destroyed that enemy unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit makes a Consolidation move, it can move up to D3+3" instead of up to 3". If a Waaagh! is active for your unit, it can move up to 6" instead.
