1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/dread-mob.md|Dread Mob]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/gretchin.md|Gretchin]] unit from your army that is within 9" of that enemy unit and not within Engagement Range of any enemy units.

## 效果

Roll one D6: on a 4+, that enemy unit suffers D3+1 mortal wounds. Your unit can then make a Normal move.
