1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/blitz-brigade.md|Blitz Brigade]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/battlewagon.md|Battlewagon]], [[factions/兽人/units/hunta-rig.md|Hunta Rig]] or [[factions/兽人/units/kill-rig.md|Kill Rig]] model from your army that was hit by one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

One Orks Infantry unit embarked within your model can disembark and make a Surge move. To do so, roll one D6: models in that unit move a number of inches up to this result, but that unit must end that move as close as possible to the closest enemy unit (excluding [[core-rules/aircraft.md|AIRCRAFT]]). When doing so, those models can be moved within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.
