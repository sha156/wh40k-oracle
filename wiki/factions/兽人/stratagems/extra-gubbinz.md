1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/dread-mob.md|Dread Mob]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Orks Walker or Grots Vehicle unit from your army (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] units) that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit, subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of that attack.
