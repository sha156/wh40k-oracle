1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/kaptin-killers.md|Kaptin Killers]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an ORKS unit from your army opens a Hatchway.

## 使用对象

That ORKS unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the battle, that Hatchway cannot be closed. Until the end of the turn, each time an attack targets your unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
