1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/da-big-hunt.md|Da Big Hunt]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Beast Snagga Infantry or Beast Snagga Mounted unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a ranged attack targets your unit, models in your unit have the Benefit of Cover against that attack. In addition, if your unit has the [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] keyword, until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Stealth ability.
