1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/兽人/detachments/green-tide.md|Green Tide]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/兽人/units/boyz.md|Boyz]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If your unit contains 10 or more models, re-roll the Wound roll instead.
