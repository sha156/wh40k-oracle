## 分队规则

### 群体心理 Mob Mentality

Each time an attack targets a [[factions/兽人/units/boyz.md|Boyz]] unit from your army, models in that unit have a 6+ invulnerable save against that attack. Each time an attack targets a BOYZ unit from your army that contains 10 or more models, models in that unit have a 5+ invulnerable save against that attack.’
