兽人的分队「Bully [[factions/兽人/units/boyz.md|Boyz]]」，分队规则「老大在看」。

## 分队规则

### 老大在看 Da Boss Is Watchin’

At the start of your Command phase, in a turn in which you have not called a Waaagh!, if you have one or more [[factions/兽人/units/warboss.md|Warboss]] models on the battlefield (or embarked within a Transport that is on the battlefield), you can call a Waaagh! for a second time this battle. When doing so, that second Waaagh! only counts as having been called for WARBOSS, [[factions/兽人/units/nobz.md|Nobz]] and [[factions/兽人/units/meganobz.md|Meganobz]] units from your army.
