兽人的分队，分队规则「冲过来」。

## 分队规则

### 冲过来 Comin’ Through

In your Movement phase, Desperate Escape tests taken for ORKS models from your army are automatically passed and each time an ORKS unit from your army makes a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move, it can move through other models as if they were not there, provided those other models have a lower Toughness characteristic than it.
