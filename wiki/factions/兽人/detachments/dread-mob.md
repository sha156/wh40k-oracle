## 分队规则

> 结构库中本分队名下有 2 条分队规则，以下全部列出。

### 试试这按钮 Try Dat Button!

Each time a [[factions/兽人/units/mek.md|Mek]], Orks Walker or Grots Vehicle unit from your army is selected to shoot or fight, roll one D6. Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in that unit have the corresponding ability shown in the table below.

| | |
|---|---|
| **D6** | **BUTTON EFFECT** |
| 1 2 | **[SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.** |
| 3 4 | **[LETHAL HITS] ability.** |
| 5 6 | **Each time an attack is made with this weapon, on a Critical Wound, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 2.** |

Alternatively, when such a unit is selected to shoot or fight, you can select one of the abilities above instead of rolling the D6. If you do, until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in that unit have the [HAZARDOUS] ability as well.

If a weapon equipped by a model from you army has the [HAZARDOUS] ability from multiple sources, each time you take a Hazardous test for that weapon, it is failed on a roll of 1-3.
