15 分、[[factions/兽人/detachments/taktikal-brigade.md|Taktikal Brigade]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, select up to three ORKS units from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in Strategic Reserves, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制
