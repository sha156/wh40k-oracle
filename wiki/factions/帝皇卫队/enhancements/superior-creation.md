25 分、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/lions-of-the-emperor.md|帝皇之狮]] 分队。

## 效果

The first time the bearer is destroyed, roll one D6 at the end of the phase. On a 2+, set the bearer back up on the battlefield, as close as possible to where it was destroyed and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, with its full wounds remaining.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Adeptus Custodes Infantry model only.
