20 分、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/shield-host.md|Shield Host]] 分队。

## 效果

After both players have deployed their armies, you can select up to two Adeptus Custodes units from your army (excluding Anathema Psykana units) and redeploy all of those units. When doing so, any of those units can be placed into Strategic Reserves, regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

Shield-Captain model only.
