25 分、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/auric-champions.md|Auric Champions]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time the bearer’s unit ends a Charge move, select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of the bearer and roll one D6: on a 4+, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. Once per battle, after the bearer s unit ends a Charge move, all enemy units within 6" of the bearer must take a Battle-shock test.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Adeptus Custodes model only.
