25 分、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队。

## 效果

While the bearer is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll. If that attack targeted an enemy [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] unit, add 1 to the Wound roll as well.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

Anathema Psykana model only.
