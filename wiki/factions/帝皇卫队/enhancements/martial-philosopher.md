30 分、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/auric-champions.md|Auric Champions]] 分队。

## 效果

The bearer’s unit is eligible to shoot and/or declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back. Once per battle, in your opponent’s Movement phase, when an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move within 8" of the bearer, if the bearer’s unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move of up to 6".

**分数**：30 分

## 携带限制

Adeptus Custodes model only.
