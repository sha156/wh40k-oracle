5 分、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队。

## 效果

Add 1 to the Attacks, Strength and Damage characteristics of the bearers melee weapons. While the bearer is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] units that are Battle-shocked, add 2 to the Attacks, Strength and Damage characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons instead.

**分数**：5 分

## 携带限制

Anathema Psykana model only.
