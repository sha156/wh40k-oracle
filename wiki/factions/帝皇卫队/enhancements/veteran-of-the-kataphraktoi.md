10 分、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/solar-spearhead.md|Solar Spearhead]] 分队。

## 效果

In your Command phase, select one Adeptus Custodes Vehicle or ADEPTUS CUSTODES MOUNTED unit within 6" of the bearer. Until the start of your next Command phase, that unit is eligible to shoot in a turn in which it Fell Back.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

Adeptus Custodes Infantry or Adeptus Custodes Mounted model only.
