1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/shield-host.md|Shield Host]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS CUSTODES unit from your army (excluding Anathema Psykana units) that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select either the [LETHAL HITS] or [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability. Until the end of the phase ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the selected ability.
