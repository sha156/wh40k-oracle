1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/voyagers-in-darkness.md|Voyagers in Darkness]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, just after an ALLARUS CUSTODIANS unit from your army ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

That ALLARUS CUSTODIANS unit.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from the result.
