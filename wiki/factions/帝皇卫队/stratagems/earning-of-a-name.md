1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/auric-champions.md|Auric Champions]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two Adeptus Custodes Character units from your army that have not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model in either of your units makes an attack that targets a MONSTER or VEHICLE unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.
