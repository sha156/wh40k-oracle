1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/shield-host.md|Shield Host]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS CUSTODES unit from your army (excluding Anathema Psykana units) that is below its Starting Strength.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in that unit. If your unit is Below Half-strength, until the end of the phase, add 2 to the Attacks characteristic of those melee weapons instead.
