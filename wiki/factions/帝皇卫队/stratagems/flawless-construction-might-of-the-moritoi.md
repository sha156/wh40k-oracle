1 CP、Opponent's Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/might-of-the-moritoi.md|亡者之势]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly ADEPTUS CUSTODES WALKER unit.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS CUSTODES WALKER unit.

## 效果

Attacks that target your unit with a S greater than your unit’s T have -1 to wound rolls.
