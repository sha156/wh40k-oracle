1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/shield-host.md|Shield Host]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after a mortal wound has been allocated to an Adeptus Custodes model from your army(excluding Anathema Psykana models).

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS CUSTODES model’s unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 4+ ability against mortal wounds.
