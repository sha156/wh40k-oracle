1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/帝皇卫队/units/prosecutors.md|Prosecutors]] unit from your army that has just shot.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the start of your next turn, while your unit is on the battlefield, that enemy unit is prosecuted. While a unit is prosecuted, each time an Anathema Psykana model makes an attack against that unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1. While a [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] or Battle-shocked unit is prosecuted, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
