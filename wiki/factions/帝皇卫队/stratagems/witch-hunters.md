1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Anathema Psykana unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Select either the [LETHAL HITS] or [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability. Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have the selected ability, but models in your unit can only target [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] units with their attacks.
