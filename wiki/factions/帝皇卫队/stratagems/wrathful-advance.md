1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/solar-spearhead.md|Solar Spearhead]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just before an ADEPTUS CUSTODES unit from your army Piles In.

## 使用对象

That ADEPTUS CUSTODES unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Pile-in move, it can move up to D3+3" instead of up to 3".
