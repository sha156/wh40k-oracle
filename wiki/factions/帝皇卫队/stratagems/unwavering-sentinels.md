1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/shield-host.md|Shield Host]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Custodes Infantry unit from your army (excluding Anathema Psykana units) that is within range of an objective marker you control and that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a melee attack targets your unit, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
