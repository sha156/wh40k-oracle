1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/black-ship-guardians.md|Black Ship Guardians]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One PROSEcUTORS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1. In addition, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack, on a Critical Wound, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by an additional 1.
