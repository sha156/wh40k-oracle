1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/帝皇卫队/units/witchseekers.md|Witchseekers]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of Torrent weapons equipped by models in your unit. If such a weapon targets a [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] or Battle-shocked unit this phase, add 2 to its Attacks characteristic instead.
