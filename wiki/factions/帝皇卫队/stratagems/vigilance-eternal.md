1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/shield-host.md|Shield Host]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Custodes Battleline unit from your army (excluding Anathema Psykana units) within range of an objective marker you control.

## 效果

That objective marker remains under your control even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the start or end of any turn.
