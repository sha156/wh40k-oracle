1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Anathema Psykana Infantry unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your unit has the Stealth ability, and Battle-shocked and [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] models can only select your unit as a target of a ranged attack if they are within 12".
