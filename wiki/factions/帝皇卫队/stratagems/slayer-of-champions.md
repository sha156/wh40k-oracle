1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/auric-champions.md|Auric Champions]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One Adeptus Custodes Character unit from your army that has just destroyed the unit you selected at the start of your Command phase as the target of your Assemblage of Might ability.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit on the battlefield Until the start of your next Command phase, each time an ADEPTUS CUSTODES CHARACTER model from your army makes an attack that target that enemy unit, add 1 to the Wound roll In addition, if the destroyed unit was a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit, gain 1CP.
