1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/帝皇卫队/units/vigilators.md|Vigilators]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a melee attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll If the target of that attack is Battle-shocked or a [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]], you can re-roll the Wound roll as well.
