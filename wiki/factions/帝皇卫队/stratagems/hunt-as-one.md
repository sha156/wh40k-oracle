1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/talons-of-the-emperor.md|Talons Of The Emperor]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

Up to two ADEPTUS CUSTODES units from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your units are eilgible to shoot and/or declare a charge in a turn in which they Fell Back.

## 限制

You can only select two units if one (and only one) of them is an ANATHEMA PSYKANA unit and both are within 6" of each other.
