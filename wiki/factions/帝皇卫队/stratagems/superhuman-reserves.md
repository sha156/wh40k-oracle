2 CP、Any phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/auric-champions.md|Auric Champions]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after an Adeptus Custodes Warlord model from your army has used an ability on its datasheet or from an Enhancement that says it can only be used Once per battle.

## 使用对象

That Adeptus Custodes Warlord model.

## 效果

Your model can use its Once per battle’ ability one additional time during this battle (but not in the same phase).

## 限制

You cannot use this Stratagem more than once per battle.
