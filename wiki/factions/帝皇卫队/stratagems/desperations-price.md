1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/帝皇卫队/detachments/null-maiden-vigil.md|Null Maiden Vigil]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after an enemy [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] unit has either finished using a Psychic ability that targets a unit, or finished making Psychic Attacks.

## 使用对象

One Anathema Psykana unit from your army within 18" of that enemy [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] unit.

## 效果

That enemy [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] unit must take a Leadership test If the test is passed, that [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] unit is Battle-shocked; if the test is failed that [[core-rules/psyker.md|PSYKER]] unit suffers 3 mortal wounds and is Battle-shocked.
