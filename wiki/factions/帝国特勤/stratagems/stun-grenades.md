1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/ordo-hereticus-purgation-force.md|Ordo Hereticus Purgation Force]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of any phase (excluding the Command phase).

## 使用对象

One ADEpTUS ARbITES, INQUISITORIAl AGENTS or ORDO HERETICUS GRENADES unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS or VEHICLES) within 8" of and visible to your unit. That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test and, until the end of the phase, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
