1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/veiled-blade-elimination-force.md|Veiled Blade Elimination Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Agents of the Imperium Infantry unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability. In addition, if it is a [[factions/帝国特勤/units/culexus-assassin.md|Culexus Assassin]] unit, until the end of the phase, change the Damage characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit to 3.
