1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/imperialis-fleet.md|Imperialis Fleet]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One AGENTS OF THE IMpERIUM Attached unit that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack with the [PRECISION] ability is allocated to a [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model in your unit, if there are one or more Bodyguard models in your unit, roll one D6: on a 2+, that attack is allocated to a Bodyguard model of your choice in your unit instead.
