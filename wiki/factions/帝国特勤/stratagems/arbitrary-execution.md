1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/interdiction-team.md|Interdiction Team]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One ADEPTUS ARBITES unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, Arbites shotpistols equipped by models in your unit have an Attacks characteristic of 2 and the [LETHAL HITS] ability, and models in your unit can target enemy units that are not within Engagement Range of them (provided they are otherwise eligible targets).
