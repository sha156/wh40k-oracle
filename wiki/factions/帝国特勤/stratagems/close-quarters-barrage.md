1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/imperialis-fleet.md|Imperialis Fleet]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One VOIDFARERS unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack that targets a unit within 12", improve the Strength and Armour Penetration characteristics of that attack by 1.
