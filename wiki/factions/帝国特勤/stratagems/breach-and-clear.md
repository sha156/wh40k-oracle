1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/voidships-company.md|Voidship’s Company]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an IMPERIAL NAvY BREAcHERS unit from yourarmy opens a Hatchway.

## 使用对象

That IMPERIAL NAVY BREACHERS unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if that model is on the opposite side of the Hatchway from the side it started the turn on, add 1 to the Hit roll and add 1 to the Wound roll.
