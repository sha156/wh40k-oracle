1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/veiled-blade-elimination-force.md|Veiled Blade Elimination Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Agents of the Imperium Infantry unit from your army that is within 6" of one or more enemy units and would be eligible to declare a charge against one or more of those enemy units.

## 效果

Your unit can declare a charge. When doing so, you must select one or more of those enemy units as the targets of that charge, and your unit does not receive a Charge bonus this turn. In addition, if it is a [[factions/帝国特勤/units/callidus-assassin.md|Callidus Assassin]] unit and it makes a Charge move as a result of this Stratagem, until the end of the turn, each time a model in your unit makes a melee attack, add 1 to the Wound roll.
