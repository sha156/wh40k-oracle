1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/ordo-hereticus-purgation-force.md|Ordo Hereticus Purgation Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One ADEpTUS ARbITES, INQUISITORIAl AGENTS or ORDO HERETICUS INFANTRy unit from your army within range of an objective marker and that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability.
