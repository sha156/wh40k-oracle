1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/ordo-xenos-alien-hunters.md|Ordo Xenos Alien Hunters]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One DEATHwATCH unit from your army.

## 效果

Select the Furor Tactics, Malleus Tactics or Purgatus Tactics. Until the start of your next Command phase, that Deathwatch Mission Tactic is active for your unit instead of any other Deathwatch Mission Tactic that is active for your army, even if you have already selected that Deathwatch Mission Tactic this battle.
