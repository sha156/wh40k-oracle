1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/ordo-hereticus-purgation-force.md|Ordo Hereticus Purgation Force]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an AGENTS OF THE IMpERIUM unit from your army is destroyed as the result of an enemy unit's attacks.

## 使用对象

One ADEpTUS ARbITES, INQUISITORIAl AGENTS or ORDO HERETICUS unit from your army that was within 6" of the destroyed unit (you cannot target the destroyed unit with the Stratagem).

## 效果

After the attacking unit has shot, your unit can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase, but when resolving those attacks it can only target that enemy unit (and only if it is an eligible target).
