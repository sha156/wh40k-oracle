1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/imperialis-fleet.md|Imperialis Fleet]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One AGENTS OF THE IMpERIUM CHARACTER unit from your army (excluding OFFICIO ASSASSINORUM units) that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit's attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save and, after the attacking unit has resolved its attacks, unless your unit is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move of up to 6" (when doing so, models in this unit move as if they have the Fly keyword).
