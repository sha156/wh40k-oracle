2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/imperialis-fleet.md|Imperialis Fleet]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One AGENTS OF THE IMpERIUM unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit within range of an objective marker, that attack has the [SUSTAINED HITS 1], [LANCE] and [IGNORES COVER] abilities.
