1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/veiled-blade-elimination-force.md|隐藏利刃歼灭部队]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit has declared a charge.

## 使用对象

One Agents of the Imperium Grenades or [[factions/帝国特勤/units/vindicare-assassin.md|Vindicare Assassin]] unit from your army that was selected as one of the targets of that charge and is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, subtract 1 from Charge rolls made for that enemy unit, or, if your unit is a VINDICARE ASSASSIN, subtract 2 from Charge rolls made for that enemy unit instead.
