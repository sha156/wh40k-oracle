2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/veiled-blade-elimination-force.md|隐藏利刃歼灭部队]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Agents of the Imperium Character unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Toughness characteristic of models in your unit. In addition, if it is an [[factions/帝国特勤/units/eversor-assassin.md|Eversor Assassin]] unit, until the end of the phase, it has the Feel No Pain 4+ ability.
