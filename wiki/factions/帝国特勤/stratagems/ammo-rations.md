1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/voidships-company.md|Voidship’s Company]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One or more VOIDfARERS units from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in one of your units makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.
