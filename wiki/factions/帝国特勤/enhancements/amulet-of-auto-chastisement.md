25 分、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/ordo-xenos-alien-hunters.md|Ordo Xenos Alien Hunters]] 分队。

## 效果

At the start of your opponents Shooting phase, select one enemy VEHICLE unit (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] units) within 12" of and visible to the bearer. That unit must take a Leadership test. If that test is passed, until the end of the phase, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll; if that test is failed, that unit is not eligible to shoot this phase.

**分数**：25 分
