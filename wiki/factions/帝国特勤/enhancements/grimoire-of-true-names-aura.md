10 分、[[factions/帝国特勤/detachments/ordo-malleus-daemon-hunters.md|Ordo Malleus Daemon Hunters]] 分队。

## 效果

While an enemy unit is within 9" of the bearer, worsen the Leadership characteristic of models in that unit by 1. In addition, while an enemy DAEMON unit is within 9" of the bearer, each time a model in that DAEMON unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll and subtract 1 from the Wound roll.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

INqUISITOR model only.
