## 分队规则

### 无尽尸群 Numberless Horde

In your Command phase in each of the following battle rounds, depending on your chosen battle size, add a new [[factions/死亡守卫/units/poxwalkers.md|Poxwalkers]] unit with a Starting Strength of 10 to your army, in Strategic Reserves.

| | |
|---|---|
| **BATTLE SIZE** | **BATTLE ROUNDS** |
| Incursion | **2,3** |
| Strike Force | **2, 3, 4** |
| Onslaught | **2, 3, 4, 5** |

KEYWORDS
POXWALKERS units from your army gain the [[core-rules/battleline.md|BATTLELINE]] keyword.
