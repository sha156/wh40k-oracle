1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/mortarions-hammer.md|Mortarion’s Hammer]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets a visible enemy unit (excluding [[core-rules/aircraft.md|AIRCRAFT]]) within your opponent’s deployment zone, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
