1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/contagion-engines.md|传瘟机械]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, when an enemy unit is selected to make a fall-back move, if that enemy unit is engaged with a friendly CONTAGION ENGINE unit.

## 使用对象

That CONTAGION ENGINE unit.

## 效果

When an enemy unit engaged with your unit is selected to make a fall-back move, roll one D6:

- On a 1, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
- On a 2-5, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds.
- On a 6, that enemy unit suffers 3 mortal wounds.
