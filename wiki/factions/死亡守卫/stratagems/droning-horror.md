1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/flyblown-host.md|Flyblown Host]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly PLAGUE MARINES unit is selected to shoot.

## 使用对象

That PLAGUE MARINES unit.

## 效果

Your unit’s ranged attacks:

- Can re-roll hit rolls of 1.
- That target a unit within half range, can re-roll wound rolls of 1.
