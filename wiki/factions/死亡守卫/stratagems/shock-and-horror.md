1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/shamblerot-vectorium.md|Shamblerot Vectorium]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, just after a Death Guard unit from your army ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

That DEATH GUARD unit.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from that test.
