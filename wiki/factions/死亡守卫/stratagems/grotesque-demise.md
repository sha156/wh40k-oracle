1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/vectors-of-decay.md|Vectors of Decay]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after a DEATH GUARD unit from your army (excluding CULTISTS and POXwALKERS units) has shot or fought.

## 使用对象

That DEATH GUARD unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit that had one or more models destroyed as a result of those attacks. That enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test.
