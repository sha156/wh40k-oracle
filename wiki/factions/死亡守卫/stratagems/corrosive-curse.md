1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/arch-contaminators.md|Arch-Contaminators]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One DEATH GUARD TERMINATOR unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Select one objective marker within Contagion Range of and visible to your unit. That objective marker is Secured by your army.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once and, until the end of the turn, your unit is not eligible to shoot or declare a charge.
