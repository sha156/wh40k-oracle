1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/champions-of-contagion.md|Champions of Contagion]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, before the Reinforcements step.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard Character unit from your army that is not leading a unit.

## 效果

Select one other friendly Death Guard unit (excluding Battle-shocked units and Attached units that already have two Leader units or one of your [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] units leading it] within 2" horizontally and 5" vertically of your unit that your unit can lead (as described in the Leader section of its datasheet]. Your unit attaches to that unit as a Leader. Change that unit’s Starting Strength accordingly.
