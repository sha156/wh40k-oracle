## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Terminator unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [ASSAULT] and [HEAVY] abilities.
