1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/champions-of-contagion.md|Champions of Contagion]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard Attached unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 2 to the Toughness characteristic of models in your unit.
