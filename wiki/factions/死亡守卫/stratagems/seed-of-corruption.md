## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One DEATH GUARD TERMINATOR unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

If your unit was set up on the battlefield this turn, it can perform a Tactical Manoeuvre this phase as if it was not set up on the battlefield this turn.
