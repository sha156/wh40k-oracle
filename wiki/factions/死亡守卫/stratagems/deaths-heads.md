1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/champions-of-contagion.md|Champions of Contagion]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/死亡守卫/units/biologus-putrifier.md|Biologus Putrifier]] unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units and has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (excluding VEHICLES) that is within 8" of and visible to your unit. Until the start of your next turn, that unit has the effect of all Plagues (see Nurgle’s Gift).
