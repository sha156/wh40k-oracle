1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/virulent-vectorium.md|Virulent Vectorium]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard Vehicle or Death Guard Monster model from your army with the Deadly Demise ability that was just destroyed. You can use this Stratagem on that model even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Do not roll one D6 to determine whether mortal wounds are inflicted by your model’s Deadly Demise ability. Instead, mortal wounds are automatically inflicted. In addition, any enemy units that suffer mortal wounds as a result of this Stratagem are Afflicted until the start of your next turn.
