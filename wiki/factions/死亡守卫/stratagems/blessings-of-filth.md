1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/champions-of-contagion.md|Champions of Contagion]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard Attached unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a Critical Hit.
