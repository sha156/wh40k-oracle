1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/shamblerot-vectorium.md|Shamblerot Vectorium]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/死亡守卫/units/poxwalkers.md|Poxwalkers]] unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, add 1 to the Move characteristic of models in your unit, and add 1 to the Attacks and Strength characteristics of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit.
