1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/death-lords-chosen.md|Death Lord’s Chosen]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Terminator unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) you can re-roll the Hit roll.
