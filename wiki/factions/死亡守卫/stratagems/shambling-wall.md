1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/shamblerot-vectorium.md|Shamblerot Vectorium]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks, and one friendly [[factions/死亡守卫/units/poxwalkers.md|Poxwalkers]] unit within 3" of your unit and visible to both your unit and the attacking unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time you would allocate an attack to a model in your DEATH GUARD unit, if your POXWALKERS unit is visible to the attacking model and is an eligible target for that attack, no saving throw is made for that attack; instead a number of POXWALKERS from your POXWALKERS unit equal to the Damage characteristic of that attack are destroyed.
