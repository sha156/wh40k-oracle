1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/shamblerot-vectorium.md|Shamblerot Vectorium]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/死亡守卫/units/poxwalkers.md|Poxwalkers]] unit from your army that was just destroyed. You can target that unit with this Stratagem even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit that made one or more attacks that targeted your unit this phase. Until the end of the battle, that enemy unit is Afflicted.
