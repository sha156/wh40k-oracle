1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/virulent-vectorium.md|Virulent Vectorium]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard Infantry unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack that targets an Afflicted unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.
