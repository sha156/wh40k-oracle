1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/death-lords-chosen.md|Death Lord’s Chosen]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, just after a Terminator unit from your army ends a Charge move.

## 使用对象

That TERMINATOR unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit, then roll one D6 for each model in your unit that is within Engagement Range of that enemy unit: for each 2+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound (to a maximum of 6 mortal wounds).
