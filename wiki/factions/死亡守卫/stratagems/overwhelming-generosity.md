1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/virulent-vectorium.md|Virulent Vectorium]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Death Guard Character unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit visible to your unit. Until the end of the phase, each time a DEATH GUARD unit from your army selects that enemy unit as the target of any ranged attacks, you can re-roll the dice to determine how many attacks a weapon equipped by a model in that unit makes.
