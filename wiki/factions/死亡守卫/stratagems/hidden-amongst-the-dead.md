## 使用时机

The Reinforcements step of your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/死亡守卫/units/poxwalkers.md|Poxwalkers]] unit from your army that is in Strategic Reserves and that is not an Attached unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in that unit have the Deep Strike ability.
