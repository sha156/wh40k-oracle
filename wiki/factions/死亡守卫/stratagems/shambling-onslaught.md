1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/unclean-uprising.md|Unclean Uprising]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Movement phase, just after an enemy unit is selected to Fall Back.

## 使用对象

One POXwALKERS unit from your army within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.

## 效果

After that enemy unit ends that Fall Back move, if your unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, your unit can make a Normal move.
