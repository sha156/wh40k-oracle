10 分、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/shamblerot-vectorium.md|Shamblerot Vectorium]] 分队。

## 效果

While the bearer is leading a [[factions/死亡守卫/units/poxwalkers.md|Poxwalkers]] unit, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of the bearer’s Plague Wind weapon. Each time the bearer makes one or more attacks with a Plague Wind weapon, after the bearer’s unit has resolved its attacks, D3 Bodyguard models from the bearer’s unit are destroyed.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制

[[factions/死亡守卫/units/malignant-plaguecaster.md|Malignant Plaguecaster]] only.
