20 分、[[factions/死亡守卫/detachments/tallyband-summoners.md|Tallyband Summoners]] 分队。

## 效果

Each time a Plague Legions unit within 12" of the bearer takes a Battle-shock test, add 1 to that test and, if that test is passed, one model in that unit regains up to D3 lost wounds (if that unit is a Battleline unit and that test is passed, up to D3 destroyed models can be returned to that unit instead).

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

[[factions/死亡守卫/units/malignant-plaguecaster.md|Malignant Plaguecaster]] only.
