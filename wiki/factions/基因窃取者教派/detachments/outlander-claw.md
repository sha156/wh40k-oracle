基因窃取者教派的分队「Outlander Claw」，分队规则「Rapid Takeover」。

## 分队规则

### Rapid Takeover

While a Genestealer Cults Mounted or Genestealer Cults Vehicle model from your army is not Battle-shocked, add 1 to its Objective Control characteristic. In addition, at the end of your Command phase, if one or more [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/atalan-jackals.md|Atalan Jackals]] units from your army are within range of an objective marker you control, that objective marker remains under your control until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase.
