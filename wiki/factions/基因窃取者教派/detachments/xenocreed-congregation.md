基因窃取者教派的分队「Xenocreed Congregation」，分队规则「Unquestioning Fanaticism」。

## 分队规则

### Unquestioning Fanaticism

For each ACOLyTE HybRIDS, HybRID METAmORPHS and NEOPHyTE HybRIDS unit from your army, while one or more CHARACTER models are leading that unit, you can re-roll Advance and Charge rolls made for it. If that [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model is a MAgUS, PRImUS or ACOLyTE ICONwARD, that model has the Feel No Pain 3+ ability while leading that unit.
