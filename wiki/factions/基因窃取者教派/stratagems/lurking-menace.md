1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/cult-unveiled.md|Cult Unveiled]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One GENESTEALER CULTS unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit's attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, if the attacking model is not within 3", subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
