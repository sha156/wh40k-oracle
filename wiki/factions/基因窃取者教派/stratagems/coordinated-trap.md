2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/host-of-ascension.md|Host of Ascension]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

The start of your Shooting phase or the start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Two GENESTEALER CULTS units from your army that have not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (if this Stratagem is used in the Fight phase, that enemy unit must be within Engagement Range of both of your units). Until the end of the phase, each time a model in either of your units makes an attack, it can only target that enemy unit (and only if it is an eligible target for that attack), and when resolving that attack, add 1 to the Wound roll.
