1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/brood-brother-auxilia.md|Brood Brother Auxilia]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Reinforcements step of your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit is set up on the battlefield from Reserves.

## 使用对象

One Astra Militarum or GENESTEALER CULTS unit from your army (excluding Monsters and Vehicles) within 12" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to 6".
