1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/outlander-claw.md|Outlander Claw]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent's Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Genestealer Cults Mounted or Genestealer Cults Vehicle unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units and is wholly within 9" of one or more battlefield edges.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
