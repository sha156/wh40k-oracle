1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/host-of-ascension.md|Host of Ascension]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Genestealer Cults Battleline unit from your army in Cult Ambush.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, when setting your unit up using a Cult Ambush marker, set your unit up anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 6" of that Cult Ambush marker and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
