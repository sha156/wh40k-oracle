1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/biosanctic-broodsurge.md|Biosanctic Broodsurge]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit destroys a Genestealer Cults Character model from your army.

## 使用对象

That destroyed [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model. You can use this Stratagem on that model even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Roll one D6: on a 2+, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. If that Character model is an [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/abominant.md|Abominant]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/patriarch.md|Patriarch]], that enemy unit suffers 2D3 mortal wounds instead.
