2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/host-of-ascension.md|Host of Ascension]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One GENESTEALER CULTS units from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, a Critical Hit is scored on an unmodified Hit roll of 5+, instead of only a 6.
