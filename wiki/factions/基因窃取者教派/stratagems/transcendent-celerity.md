1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/xenocreed-congregation.md|Xenocreed Congregation]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Acolyte Hybrids, [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/hybrid-metamorphs.md|Hybrid Metamorphs]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/neophyte-hybrids.md|Neophyte Hybrids]] unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [ASSAULT] ability.
