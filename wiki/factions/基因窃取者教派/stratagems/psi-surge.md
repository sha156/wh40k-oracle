1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/final-day.md|最终之日]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of any phase.

## 使用对象

One Tyranids unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the start of your next Command phase, increase the range of your unit’s Catalyst ability by 3".

## 限制

Each time you use this Stratagem, until the end of your next Command phase, you cannot use this Stratagem again.
