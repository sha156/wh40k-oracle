1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/infestation-swarm.md|Infestation Swarm]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a GREAT DEVOURER unit from your army opens a Hatchway.

## 使用对象

That GREAT DEVOURER unit.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to 3", provided at least one of its models ends that move on the opposite side of that Hatchway from when your unit opened it.
