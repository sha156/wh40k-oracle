1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/final-day.md|最终之日]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Genestealer Cults unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If one or more friendly Tyranids units are within 6" of that enemy unit, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.
