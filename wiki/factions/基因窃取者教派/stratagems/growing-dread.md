1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/genespawn-onslaught.md|Genespawn Onslaught]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit is selected to perform a Tactical Manoeuvre.

## 使用对象

One GENESTEALER CULTS unit from your army that is within 9" of and visible to that enemy unit.

## 效果

That enemy unit must take a Leadership test, subtracting 1 from the result: if failed, that enemy unit does not perform that Tactical Manoeuvre and it cannot perform anotherTactical Manoeuvre this turn (but can otherwise act normally).
