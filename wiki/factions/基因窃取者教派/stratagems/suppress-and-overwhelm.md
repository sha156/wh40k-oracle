1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/brood-brother-auxilia.md|Brood Brother Auxilia]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, just after an Astra Militarum unit from your army has shot.

## 使用对象

That ASTRA MILITARUM unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the end of the turn, that enemy unit cannot be targeted with the Fire Overwatch Stratagem and each time a GENESTEALER CULTS unit from your army selects that enemy unit as a target of a charge, you can re-roll the Charge roll.
