1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/xenocreed-congregation.md|Xenocreed Congregation]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit destroys a Genestealer Cults Character model from your army.

## 使用对象

One other GENESTEALER CULTS CHARACTER model from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the battle, each time a friendly Acolyte Hybrids, [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/hybrid-metamorphs.md|Hybrid Metamorphs]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/neophyte-hybrids.md|Neophyte Hybrids]] model makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, re-roll a Hit roll of 1. If the destroyed model was a [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/magus.md|Magus]], [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/primus.md|Primus]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/acolyte-iconward.md|Acolyte Iconward]], you can re-roll the Hit roll instead.
