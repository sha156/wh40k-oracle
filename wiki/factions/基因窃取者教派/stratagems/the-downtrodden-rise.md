2 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/xenocreed-congregation.md|Xenocreed Congregation]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Acolyte Hybrids, [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/hybrid-metamorphs.md|Hybrid Metamorphs]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/neophyte-hybrids.md|Neophyte Hybrids]] unit from your army in Cult Ambush.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, you can set up your unit on the battlefield without using a Cult Ambush marker. When doing so, set up your unit anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units.
