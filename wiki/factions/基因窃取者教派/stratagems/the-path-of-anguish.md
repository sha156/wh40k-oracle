1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/xenocreed-congregation.md|Xenocreed Congregation]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One Acolyte Hybrids or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/neophyte-hybrids.md|Neophyte Hybrids]] unit from your army that had one or more of its models destroyed as a result of the attacking unit's attacks.

## 效果

Your unit can make a move of up to D6", but it unit must end that move as close as possible to the closest enemy unit (excluding [[core-rules/aircraft.md|AIRCRAFT]]), When doing so, models in your unit can be moved within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.
