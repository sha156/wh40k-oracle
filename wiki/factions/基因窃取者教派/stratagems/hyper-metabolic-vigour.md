1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/biosanctic-broodsurge.md|Biosanctic Broodsurge]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/aberrants.md|Aberrants]], [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/biophagus.md|Biophagus]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/purestrain-genestealers.md|Purestrain Genestealers]] unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Pile-in or Consolidation move, it can move up to 6" instead of up to 3". In addition, it does not need to end that move closer to the closest enemy model, provided it ends it as close as possible to the closest enemy unit.
