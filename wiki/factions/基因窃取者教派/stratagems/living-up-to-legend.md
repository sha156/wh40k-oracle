1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/heroes-of-the-uprising.md|叛逆英雄]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when a friendly KILLER unit has attacked, if those attacks destroyed an enemy unit or an enemy [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] model.

## 使用对象

That KILLER unit.

## 效果

Each visible friendly Battle-shocked GENESTEALER CULTS unit within 12" of your unit is no longer Battle-shocked.
