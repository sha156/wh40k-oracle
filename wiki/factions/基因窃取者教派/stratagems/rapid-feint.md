1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/outlander-claw.md|Outlander Claw]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/achilles-ridgerunners.md|Achilles Ridgerunners]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/atalan-jackals.md|Atalan Jackals]] unit from your army that is within 9" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to 6".
