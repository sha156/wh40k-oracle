1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/brood-brother-auxilia.md|Brood Brother Auxilia]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an Astra Militarum Infantry Regiment unit from your army (excluding Artillery and Character units) is destroyed.

## 使用对象

That [[core-rules/infantry.md|INFANTRY]] unit. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Roll one D6: on a 3+, place one Cult Ambush marker anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 8" horizontally away from all enemy units (if this is not possible, no marker is placed) and add a new unit to your army identical to your destroyed unit, in Cult Ambush, at its Starting Strength, with all of its wounds remaining and any [ONE SHOT] weapons those models are equipped with considered as not having been shot.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle.
