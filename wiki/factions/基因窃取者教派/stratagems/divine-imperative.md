1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/final-day.md|最终之日]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase, when a friendly Genestealer Cults unit within 12" of a friendly engaged Tyranids unit declares a charge.

## 使用对象

That GENESTEALER CULTS unit.

## 效果

- Your unit has +1 to Charge rolls.
- You can use this part of this Stratagem. If you do:

- Your unit can re-roll Charge rolls.
- Your unit must end that charge move engaged with an enemy unit engaged with that friendly TYRANIDS unit.
