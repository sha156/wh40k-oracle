1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/cult-unveiled.md|Cult Unveiled]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One GENESTEALER CULTS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of one or more friendly GENESTEALER CULTS units. Until the end of the phase, your unit can make ranged attacks that target that enemy unit. If it does, after your unit has finished making those attacks, roll one D6 for each of those friendly units: on a 2-4, that friendly unit suffers 1 mortal wound; on a 5+, that friendly unit suffers 2 mortal wounds.
