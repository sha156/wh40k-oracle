1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/outlander-claw.md|Outlander Claw]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/achilles-ridgerunners.md|Achilles Ridgerunners]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/atalan-jackals.md|Atalan Jackals]] unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
