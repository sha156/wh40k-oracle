1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/xenocreed-congregation.md|Xenocreed Congregation]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Acolyte Hybrids, [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/hybrid-metamorphs.md|Hybrid Metamorphs]] or [[factions/基因窃取者教派/units/neophyte-hybrids.md|Neophyte Hybrids]] unit from your army that has not declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

- If your unit made an Advance or Fall Back move this turn, that Advance or Fall Back move does not prevent your unit from being eligible to declare a charge.
- When your unit declares a charge, you can use this part of this Stratagem. If you do:

- Your unit can re-roll Charge rolls.
- Your unit must end that charge move engaged with an enemy unit engaged with a friendly [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] unit.
