1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/xenocult-masses.md|异形教众]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, when a friendly NEOPHYTE HYBRIDS unit is selected to shoot.

## 使用对象

That NEOPHYTE HYBRIDS unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit. Your unit’s ranged attacks that target that enemy unit can re-roll Hit rolls.
