1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/genespawn-onslaught.md|Genespawn Onslaught]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase, just after a HYBRID METAMORPHS unit from your army is selected to perform the Set Overwatch Tactical Manoeuvre.

## 使用对象

That HYBRID METAMORPHS unit.

## 效果

Select one objective marker you control that your unit is within range of. That objective marker is Secured by your army.
