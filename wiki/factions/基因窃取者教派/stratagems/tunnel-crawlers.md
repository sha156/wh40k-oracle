1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/host-of-ascension.md|Host of Ascension]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One GENESTEALER CULTS unit from your army that is arriving using the Deep Strike ability this phase.

## 效果

Your unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units.

## 限制

A unit targeted by this Stratagem is not eligible to declare a charge in the same turn.
