1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/final-day.md|Final Day]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Genestealer Cults or Tyranids unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
