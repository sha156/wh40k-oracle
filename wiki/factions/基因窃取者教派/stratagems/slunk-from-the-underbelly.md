1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/xenocult-masses.md|Xenocult Masses]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly NEOPHYTE HYBRIDS unit with every model within a terrain area.

## 使用对象

That NEOPHYTE HYBRIDS unit.

## 效果

Ranged attacks that target your unit have -1 AP until that unit has attacked.
