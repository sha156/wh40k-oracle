1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/host-of-ascension.md|Host of Ascension]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit declares a charge.

## 使用对象

One Genestealer Cults Infantry unit from your army that was selected as a target of that charge.

## 效果

Roll one D6: on a 2-4, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 5+, that enemy unit suffers 3 mortal wounds.
