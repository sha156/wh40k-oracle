1 CP、Shooting or Charge phase、[[factions/基因窃取者教派/detachments/final-day.md|最终之日]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Tyranids unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back.
