25 分、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/annihilation-legion.md|Annihilation Legion]] 分队。

## 效果

In the Fight phase, each time a model in the bearer’s unit is destroyed, if that model has not fought this phase, roll one D6: on a 4+, do not remove the destroyed model from play; it can fight after the attacking models unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

NECRONS model only.
