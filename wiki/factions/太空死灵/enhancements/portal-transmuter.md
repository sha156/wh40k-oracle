## 效果

Once per turn, when the bearer ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move within 3" of an open Hatchway or when the bearer opens a Hatchway, it can, before any units decide if they wish to fire Overwatch, use this ability. If it does, remove the bearer from the battlefield and set it back up on the battlefield wholly within 3" of another open Hatchway and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units. When doing so, if the bearer cannot be set back up on the battlefield, it is destroyed.
