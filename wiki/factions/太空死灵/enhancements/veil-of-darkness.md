20 分、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/awakened-dynasty.md|Awakened Dynasty]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle, at the end of your opponent’s turn, if the bearer’s unit is not within Engagement Range of any enemy units, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, remove that unit from the battlefield. Then, in the Reinforcements step of your next Movement phase, set up that unit anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 9" horizontally away from all enemy models.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

NECRONS model only.
