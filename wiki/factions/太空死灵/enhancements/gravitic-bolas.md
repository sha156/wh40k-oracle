15 分、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cryptek-conclave.md|Cryptek Conclave]] 分队。

## 效果

In your Shooting phase, after the bearer has shot, select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks (excluding [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]] units); until the start of your next turn, that enemy unit is pinned. While a unit is pinned, subtract 2 from that unit’s Move characteristic and subtract 2 from Charge rolls made for that unit.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Cryptek model only.
