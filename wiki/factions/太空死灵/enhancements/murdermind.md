15 分、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cursed-legion.md|Cursed Legion]] 分队。

## 效果

The bearer has the Destroyer Cult keyword and during the Declare Battle Formations step, the bearer can be attached to a DESTROYER CULT unit (excluding [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] units). If you do, the bearer’s unit cannot contain any models without the DESTROYER CULT keyword. Add 3" to the Move characteristic of the bearer.

**分数**：15 分

## 携带限制

Cryptek model only.
