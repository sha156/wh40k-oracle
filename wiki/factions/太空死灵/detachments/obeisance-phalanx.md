太空死灵的分队「Obeisance Phalanx」，分队规则「劲敌」。

## 分队规则

### 劲敌 Worthy Foes

In your Command phase, select one enemy unit. Until the start of your next Command phase, each time a Noble, [[factions/太空死灵/units/lychguard.md|Lychguard]] or Triarch unit from your army makes an attack that targets that unit, add 1 to the Wound roll.
