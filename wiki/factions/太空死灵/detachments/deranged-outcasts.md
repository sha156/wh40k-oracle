太空死灵的分队，分队规则「恐怖冲锋」。

## 分队规则

### 恐怖冲锋 Terrifying Charge

Each time a NECRONS unit from your army declares a charge, each enemy unit that is a target of that charge must take a Leadership test: if failed, until the end of the turn, each time a model in that enemy unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
