太空死灵的分队「Canoptek Harvesters」，分队规则「报应协议」。

## 分队规则

### 报应协议 Retribution Protocols

Each time an enemy unit destroys a CRYPTEK model from your army, until the end of the battle, each time a NECRONS model from your army makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
