1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/canoptek-harvesters.md|Canoptek Harvesters]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One CRYPTEK model from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, your model has a 4+ invulnerable save.
