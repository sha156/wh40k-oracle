1 CP、Opponent's Shooting phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/hand-of-the-dynasty.md|王朝之手]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit that targeted a friendly IMMORTALS/NECRON WARRIORS unit has shot.

## 使用对象

That IMMORTALS/NECRON WARRIORS unit.

## 效果

Your unit shoots using snap shooting, but while doing so your unit can only target that enemy unit.
