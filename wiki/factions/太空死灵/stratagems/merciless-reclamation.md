2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/starshatter-arsenal.md|Starshatter Arsenal]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One NECRONS unit (excluding Monster and Titanic units) from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if the target of that attack is within range of one or more objective markers, add 1 to the Wound roll.
