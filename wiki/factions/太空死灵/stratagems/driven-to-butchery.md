1 CP、Shooting or Charge phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cursed-legion.md|诅咒军团]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One Destroyer Cult unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per turn.
