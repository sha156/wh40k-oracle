1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/tomb-ship-complement.md|Tomb Ship Complement]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One NEcRON WARRIORS or IMMORTALS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
