1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cursed-legion.md|Cursed Legion]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One NECRONS unit (excluding Monsters and Vehicles) from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.
