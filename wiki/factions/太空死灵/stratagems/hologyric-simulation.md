1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/harbinger-cabal.md|Harbinger Cabal]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One NECRONS unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, if your unit is within 6" of a friendly Cryptek unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll. If your unit has the CRYPTEK keyword, you can re-roll the Wound roll as well.
