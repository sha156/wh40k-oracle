1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/canoptek-harvesters.md|Canoptek Harvesters]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One CRYPTEK model from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your model makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.
