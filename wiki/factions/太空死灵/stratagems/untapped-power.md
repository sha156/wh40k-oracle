1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cryptek-conclave.md|Cryptek Conclave]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One Cryptek unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit is selected to shoot, when selecting an ability for the Technosorcerous Augmentations Detachment Rule, you can select one additional ability from those available.
