1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cursed-legion.md|Cursed Legion]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after the first time a Destroyer Cult unit from your army destroys an enemy unit this turn.

## 使用对象

One friendly NECRONS unit (excluding Monsters and Vehicles) within 9" of that DESTROYER CULT unit.

## 效果

The friendly unit’s Reanimation Protocols activate.
