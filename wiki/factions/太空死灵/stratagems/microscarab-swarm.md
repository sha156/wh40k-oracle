1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cryptek-conclave.md|墓穴技师密会]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Cryptek Infantry unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

If your unit has the [[factions/太空死灵/units/necron-warriors.md|Necron Warriors]] keyword, until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 5+ invulnerable save. If your unit has the [[factions/太空死灵/units/immortals.md|Immortals]] keyword, until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
