1 CP、Opponent's Shooting phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/skyshroud-spearhead.md|Skyshroud Spearhead]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly NECRONS MOUNTED unit.

## 使用对象

That NECRONS MOUNTED unit.

## 效果

Ranged attacks that target your unit with a S greater than your unit’s T have -1 to wound rolls.
