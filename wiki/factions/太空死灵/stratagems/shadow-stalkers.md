1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/deranged-outcasts.md|Deranged Outcasts]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, at the start of the Reinforcements step.

## 使用对象

One FLAYED ONES unit from your army that is in Reserves.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have the Deep Strike ability.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once.
