1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/canoptek-court.md|Canoptek Court]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has shot or fought.

## 使用对象

One CRYPTEK model from your army that was destroyed by one of the attacking unit’s attacks.
You can use this Stratagem on that model even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Until the end of the battle, each time a friendly CANOPTEK model makes an attack that targets the attacking unit, add 1 to the Hit roll and add 1 to the Wound roll.
