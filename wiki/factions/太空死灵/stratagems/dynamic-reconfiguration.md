1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/harbinger-cabal.md|Harbinger Cabal]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of any phase.

## 使用对象

One Cryptek unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one of the abilities from the Arcanoscientific Expertise Detachment rule. Until the start of your next Command phase, your unit has that ability instead of any others from the Detachment Rule.
