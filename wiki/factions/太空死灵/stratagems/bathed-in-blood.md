1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/deranged-outcasts.md|Deranged Outcasts]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit is destroyed by a DESTROYER CULT unit from your army

## 使用对象

That DESTROYER CULT unit.

## 效果

Select one objective marker that your unit or the destroyed unit was within range of at the start of the phase. That objective marker is Secured by your army.
