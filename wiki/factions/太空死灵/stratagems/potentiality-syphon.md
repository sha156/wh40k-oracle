1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cryptek-conclave.md|Cryptek Conclave]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Command phase.

## 使用对象

One NECRONS unit from your army within range of one or more objective markers.

## 效果

Your unit’s Reanimation Protocols activate. If it is a Cryptek unit, it reanimates an additional 1 wound.
