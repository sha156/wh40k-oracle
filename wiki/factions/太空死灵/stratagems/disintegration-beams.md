1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/tomb-ship-complement.md|Tomb Ship Complement]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after an enemy unit attempts to prevent a NECRONS unit from you army from operating a closed Hatchway.

## 使用对象

That NECRONS unit.

## 效果

Roll one D6: on a 2-4, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound; on a 5+, that enemy unit suffers 2 mortal wounds.
