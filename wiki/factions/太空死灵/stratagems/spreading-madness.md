1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/cursed-legion.md|Cursed Legion]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Charge phase.

## 使用对象

One NECRONS unit (excluding Monsters and Vehicles) from your army that has not declared a charge this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit declares a charge, if one or more targets of that charge are within Engagement Range of one or more friendly units, add 2 to the Charge roll.
