1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/太空死灵/detachments/obeisance-phalanx.md|Obeisance Phalanx]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One [[factions/太空死灵/units/lychguard.md|Lychguard]] or Triarch unit from your army.

## 效果

Until the start of your next Command phase, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in your unit. If your unit has the VEHICLE keyword, add 3 to the Objective Control characteristic instead.
