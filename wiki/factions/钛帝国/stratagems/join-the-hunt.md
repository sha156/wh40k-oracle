2 CP、Any phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kroot-hunting-pack.md|Kroot Hunting Pack]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One Kroot Infantry or [[factions/钛帝国/units/kroot-hounds.md|Kroot Hounds]] unit from your army that was just destroyed. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Add a new unit to your army identical to your destroyed unit, in Strategic Reserves, at its Starting Strength.

## 限制

This Stratagem cannot be used to return destroyed Character units to Attached units. You can only use this Stratagem once per battle.
