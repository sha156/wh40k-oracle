1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/starfire-cadre.md|Starfire Cadre]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a FIRE WARRIOR or PATHfINDER TEAM unit from your army opens a Hatchway.

## 使用对象

That FIRE WARRIOR or PATHFINDER TEAM unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack that targets an enemy unit wholly on the opposite side of that Hatchway from them, a Critical Hit is scored on an unmodified Hit roll of 5+, instead of only a 6.
