1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kroot-raiding-party.md|Kroot Raiding Party]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One KROOT unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Pile-in or Consolidation move, it can move up to 6" instead of up to 3". In addition, when making a Consolidation move, that model can end that move within Engagement Range of the closest enemy unit that was not visible to its unit when it started that move.
