1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kroot-raiding-party.md|Kroot Raiding Party]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One KROOT unit from your army.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within 9" of and visible to your unit and roll one D6: on a 2+, until the start of your next Shooting phase, halve the Move characteristic of models in that enemy unit and halve Advance and Charge rolls made for it.
