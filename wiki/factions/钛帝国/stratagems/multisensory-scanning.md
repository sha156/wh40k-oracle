1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/auxiliary-cadre.md|Auxiliary Cadre]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If it is a Kroot or [[factions/钛帝国/units/vespid-stingwings.md|Vespid Stingwings]] unit, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.
