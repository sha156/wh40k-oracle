1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kauyon.md|Kauyon]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army that has just been selected as an Observer unit (see For the Greater Good).

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets their Spotted unit, improve the Ballistic Skill characteristic of that attack by 1 and, if your unit has the Markerlight keyword, that attack has the [IGNORES COVER] ability.
