1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/auxiliary-cadre.md|Auxiliary Cadre]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase.

## 使用对象

One Kroot or [[factions/钛帝国/units/vespid-stingwings.md|Vespid Stingwings]] unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit Advances, do not make an Advance roll for it. Instead, until the end of the phase, add 6" to the Move characteristic of models in your unit.
