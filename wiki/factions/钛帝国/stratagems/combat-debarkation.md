1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/montka.md|Mont’ka]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One T’au Empire Infantry unit from your army that disembarked from a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] this turn.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets the closest enemy unit, you can re-roll the Wound roll.
