1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kauyon.md|Kauyon]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Stealth, Ghostkeel or [[factions/钛帝国/units/commander-shadowsun.md|Commander Shadowsun]] unit from your army.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.

## 限制

You cannot target a unit that is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
