1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/auxiliary-cadre.md|Auxiliary Cadre]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army that was eligible to fight this phase.

## 效果

If your unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move of up to 6". Otherwise, your unit can make a Fall Back move. It cannot embark within a Transport at the end of this move if it disembarked from a [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] this turn.
