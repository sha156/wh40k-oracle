1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/experimental-prototype-cadre.md|Experimental Prototype Cadre]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time you roll one or more dice to determine the number of attacks made with a weapon equipped by a model in your unit, you can re-roll the result.
