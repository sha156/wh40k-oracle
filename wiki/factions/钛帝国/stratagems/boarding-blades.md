1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kroot-raiding-party.md|Kroot Raiding Party]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One KROOT unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit by 1. In addition, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, on a Critical Wound, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by an additional 1.
