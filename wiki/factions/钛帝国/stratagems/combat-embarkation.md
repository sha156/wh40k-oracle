1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kauyon.md|Kauyon]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Charge phase, just after an enemy unit has declared a charge.

## 使用对象

One T’au Empire Infantry unit from your army that was selected as one of the targets of that charge, and one friendly Transport.

## 效果

Your unit can embark within that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]]. If it does, your opponent can select new targets for that charge.

## 限制

Every model in your T’AU EMPIRE INFANTRY unit must be within 3" of that [[core-rules/transport.md|TRANSPORT]] and there must be sufficient transport capacity to embark the entire unit.
