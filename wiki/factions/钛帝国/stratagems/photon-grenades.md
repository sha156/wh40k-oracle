1 CP、Charge phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kauyon.md|Kauyon]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s charge phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its charge target.

## 使用对象

One T’au Empire Grenades unit from your army that was selected as one of the targets of that charge.

## 效果

That enemy unit must immediately take a Battle-shock test, and until the end of the phase, subtract 2 from Charge rolls made for that enemy unit.
