1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kauyon.md|Kauyon]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a ranged attack that targets an enemy unit within 9", improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.

## 限制

You cannot use this Stratagem during the first or second battle rounds.
