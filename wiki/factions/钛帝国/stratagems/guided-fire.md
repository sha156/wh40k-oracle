1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/auxiliary-cadre.md|Auxiliary Cadre]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army (excluding Kroot and [[factions/钛帝国/units/vespid-stingwings.md|Vespid Stingwings]] units) that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit. If your unit is wholly within 9" of one or more KROOT or VESPID STINGWINGS units from your army, add 2 to the Strength characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit instead.
