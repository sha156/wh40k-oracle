1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/starfire-cadre.md|Starfire Cadre]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after an enemy unit destroys a T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army.

## 使用对象

One FIRE WARRIOR or PATHfINDER TEAM unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, has not fired Overwatch this turn and is within 9" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Your unit can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase, but when doing so, models in your unit can only target that enemy unit (and only if it is an eligible target) and, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ is required to score a hit.
