1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/experimental-prototype-cadre.md|Experimental Prototype Cadre]] 分队、Wargear Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase.

## 使用对象

One T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

## 效果

Select the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] or [LETHAL HITS] ability. Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the selected ability. You can instead select the [SUSTAINED HITS 1], [LETHAL HITS] and [HAZARDOUS] abilities to apply to those weapons until the end of the phase.

## 限制

You cannot target the same unit with the Experimental Ammunition and Threat Assessment Analyser Stratagems in the same phase.
