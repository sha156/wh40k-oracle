钛帝国的分队「Starfire Cadre」，分队规则「标记光精准」。

## 分队规则

### 标记光精准 Markerlight Precision

Each time a T’AU EMPIRE unit from your army (excluding KROOT units) is selected to shoot, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have their Ballistic Skill characteristic improved by 1 and have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability while targeting an enemy unit that is visible to one or more friendly MARKERLiGHT units that are not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy models.
