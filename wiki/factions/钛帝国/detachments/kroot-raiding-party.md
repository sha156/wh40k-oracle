钛帝国的分队，分队规则「游击伏兵」。

## 分队规则

### 游击伏兵 Guerrilla Ambushers

Each time an enemy unit is selected to fire Overwatch, roll one D6: on a 4+, until the end of the turn, that unit cannot make Overwatch attacks that target KROOT units from your army.
