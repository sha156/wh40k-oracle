10 分、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/kroot-hunting-pack.md|Kroot Hunting Pack]] 分队。

## 效果

Ranged weapons equipped by models in the bearer’s unit have the [IGNORES COVER] ability, and enemy units that are set up on the battlefield as Reinforcements cannot be set up within 12" horizontally of the bearer.

**分数**：10 分

## 携带限制
