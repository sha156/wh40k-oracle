20 分、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/retaliation-cadre.md|Retaliation Cadre]] 分队。

## 效果

At the end of your opponent’s turn, if the bearer’s unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, you can remove that unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

T’au Empire Battlesuit model only.
