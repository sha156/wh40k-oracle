25 分、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/auxiliary-cadre.md|Auxiliary Cadre]] 分队。

## 效果

The bearer’s unit can be set up using the Deep Strike ability in the Reinforcements step of your first, second or third Movement phase, regardless of any mission rules. During your first Movement phase, when you set up the bearer’s unit, it must be set up within 12" of one or more friendly Kroot or [[factions/钛帝国/units/vespid-stingwings.md|Vespid Stingwings]] units and not within 9" horizontally of one or more enemy units.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

T’au Empire Walker model with the Deep Strike ability only.
