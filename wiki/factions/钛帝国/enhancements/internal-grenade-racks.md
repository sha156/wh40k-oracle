20 分、[[factions/钛帝国/detachments/retaliation-cadre.md|Retaliation Cadre]] 分队。

## 效果

The bearer has the Grenades keyword, and each time the bearer ends a Normal move, you can select one enemy unit that it moved over during that move. If you do, roll six D6: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

T’au Empire Battlesuit model only.
