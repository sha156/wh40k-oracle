1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/houndpack-lance.md|Houndpack Lance]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

End of your opponent’s Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One War Dog unit from your army that is wholly within 12" of one or more battlefield edges and not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.

## 效果

Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.
