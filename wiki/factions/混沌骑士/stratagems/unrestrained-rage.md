1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/iconoclast-fiefdom.md|Iconoclast Fiefdom]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a CHAOS KNIGHTS unit from your army Advances or Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That Chaos Knights unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced or Fell Back.
