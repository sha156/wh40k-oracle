1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/iconoclast-fiefdom.md|Iconoclast Fiefdom]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One Chaos Knights unit from your army that is within 9" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Select one Damned unit from your army that is not Battle-shocked, not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units and is within 6" of your unit. That DAMNED unit can make a Normal move of up to 6", but it must end this move closer to that enemy unit than when it started the move.
