2 CP、Any phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/iconoclast-fiefdom.md|Iconoclast Fiefdom]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase.

## 使用对象

One Damned unit (excluding Accursed Cultists) from your army that was just destroyed. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Add a new unit to your army identical to your destroyed unit, in Strategic Reserves, at its Starting Strength and with all of its wounds remaining.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per battle. This Stratagem cannot be used to return destroyed [[core-rules/character.md|CHARACTER]] units to Attached units.
