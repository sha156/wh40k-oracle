1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/iconoclast-fiefdom.md|Iconoclast Fiefdom]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One friendly DAMNED unit (excluding ACCURSED CULTISTS units) within 9" of a friendly CHAOS KNIGHTS model.

## 效果

Select one CHAOS KNIGHTS model within 9" of your unit and roll D3+3:
- Your DAMNED unit suffers that number of mortal wounds.
- That CHAOS KNIGHTS model heals that number of wounds.
