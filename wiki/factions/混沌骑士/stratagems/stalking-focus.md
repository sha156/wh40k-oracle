1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/hunting-warpack.md|Hunting Warpack]] 分队。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly WAR DOG unit within range of an objective marker.

## 使用对象

That WAR DOG unit.

## 效果

Ranged attacks that target your unit have -1 AP until that enemy unit has attacked.
