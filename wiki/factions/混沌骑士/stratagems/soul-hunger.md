1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/iconoclast-fiefdom.md|叛道封地]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just after a CHAOS KNIGHTS unit from your army has fought.

## 使用对象

That Chaos Knights unit.

## 效果

If one or more models were destroyed as a result of those attacks, your unit regains up to D3 lost wounds. If one or more models from Battle-shocked units were destroyed as a result of those attacks, your unit regains up to D3+2 lost wounds instead.
