1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/houndpack-lance.md|Houndpack Lance]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after a War Dog unit from your army that has not yet fought is destroyed.

## 使用对象

That WAR DOG unit. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

Before resolving your unit’s Deadly Demise ability, it can either shoot or fight. When doing so, it must target only the enemy unit that just destroyed it, and can only do so if that enemy unit is an eligible target. After it has done so and after any Consolidation moves have been made, resolve your unit’s Deadly Demise ability as normal.
