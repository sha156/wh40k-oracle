1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/houndpack-lance.md|Houndpack Lance]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a War Dog unit from your army Falls Back.

## 使用对象

That WAR DOG unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the turn, your unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back.
