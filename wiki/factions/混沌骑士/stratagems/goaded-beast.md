1 CP、Shooting phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/helhunt-lance.md|地狱猎手骑兵队]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has shot.

## 使用对象

One CHAOS KNIGHTS unit from your army that lost one or more wounds as a result of those attacks.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Surge move. To do so, roll one D6: models in your unit move a number of inches up to the result, but your unit must end that move as close as possible to the closest enemy unit (excluding [[core-rules/aircraft.md|AIRCRAFT]]). When doing so, those models can be moved within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.
