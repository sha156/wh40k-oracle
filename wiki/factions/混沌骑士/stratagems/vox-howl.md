2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/houndpack-lance.md|Houndpack Lance]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of the Shooting phase or the start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One War Dog Character unit from your army.

## 效果

Each enemy unit within 6" of your unit must take a Battle-shock test. Each friendly WAR DOG unit that is within 6" of your unit and is Battle-shocked must take a Leadership test; if that test is passed, that unit is no longer Battle-shocked.

## 限制

You can only use this Stratagem once per turn.
