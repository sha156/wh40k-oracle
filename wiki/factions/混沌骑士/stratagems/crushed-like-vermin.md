1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/lords-of-dread.md|Lords of Dread]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Movement phase, just after a Chaos Knights Character unit from your army ends a Normal move.

## 使用对象

That CHAOS KNIGHTS CHARACTER unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) that your unit moved over during that move and roll six D6: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound. If one or more models are destroyed as a result of this Stratagem, that enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test.
