1 CP、Movement phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/houndpack-lance.md|Houndpack Lance]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Movement phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move.

## 使用对象

One War Dog unit from your army that is within 9" of that enemy unit.

## 效果

Your unit can make a Normal move of up to 6".
