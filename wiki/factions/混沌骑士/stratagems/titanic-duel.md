1 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/lords-of-dread.md|Lords of Dread]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

One Chaos Knights Character unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

## 效果

Select one enemy MONSTER or VEHICLE unit. Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, re-roll a Hit roll of 1 and re-roll a Wound roll of 1. Each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, if that enemy unit is [[core-rules/titanic.md|TITANIC]], you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.
