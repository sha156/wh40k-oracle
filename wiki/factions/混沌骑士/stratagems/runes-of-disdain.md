2 CP、Shooting or Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/lords-of-dread.md|Lords of Dread]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

## 使用对象

One Chaos Knights Character unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit, subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of that attack.
