1 CP、Command phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/traitoris-lance.md|Traitoris Lance]] 分队、Strategic Ploy Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Your Command phase.

## 使用对象

One Abhorrent unit from your army and up to two friendly War Dog units.

## 效果

Until the start of your next Command phase, those WAR DOG units are treated as being within range of any Aura abilities your ABHORRENT unit has.
