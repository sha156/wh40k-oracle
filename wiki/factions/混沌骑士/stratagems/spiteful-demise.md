1 CP、Any phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/lords-of-dread.md|Lords of Dread]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Any phase, just after a Chaos Knights Character unit from your army is destroyed.

## 使用对象

That CHAOS KNIGHTS CHARACTER unit. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

## 效果

When rolling to determine whether mortal wounds are inflicted by your unit’s Deadly Demise ability, that model’s Deadly Demise ability inflicts mortal wounds on a D6 roll of a 4+ instead of on a 6.
