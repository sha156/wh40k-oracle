1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/houndpack-lance.md|Houndpack Lance]] 分队、Battle Tactic Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Start of the Fight phase.

## 使用对象

Two or more War Dog units from your army within Engagement Range of the same enemy unit.

## 效果

Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of those WAR DOG units. Until the end of the phase, models in those WAR DOG units can only target that enemy unit, but each time a model in one of those WAR DOG units makes an attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a Critical Hit.
