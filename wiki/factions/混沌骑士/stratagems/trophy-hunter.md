1 CP、Fight phase、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/lords-of-dread.md|Lords of Dread]] 分队、Epic Deed Stratagem。

## 使用时机

Fight phase, just before a Chaos Knights Character unit from your army consolidates.

## 使用对象

That CHAOS KNIGHTS CHARACTER unit.

## 效果

Until the end of the phase, each time your unit Consolidates, models in it can move an additional 3" as long as your unit can end that move within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units.
