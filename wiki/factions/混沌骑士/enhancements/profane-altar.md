## 效果

Each time the bearer makes a Dark Sacrifice, the maximum number of models are destroyed in the selected Damned unit (i.e. 3 if the unit’s Leadership test is passed, or 6 if it is failed) but, until the end of the phase, the bearer’s weapons have both [LETHAL HITS] and [SUSTAINED HITS 1].

**分数**：20 分

## 携带限制

CHAOS KNIGHTS model only.
