30 分、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/lords-of-dread.md|Lords of Dread]] 分队。

## 效果

Once per battle round, you can target the bearer’s unit with the Command Re-roll Stratagem for 0CP, and can do so even if another unit from your army has already been targeted by that Stratagem this phase. In addition, you gain Lord of Deceit ability:

**Lord of Deceit (Aura):** Each time your opponent targets a unit from their army with a Stratagem, if that unit is within 12" of this model, increase the cost of that use of that Stratagem by 1CP.

**分数**：30 分

## 携带限制

CHAOS KNIGHTS model only.
