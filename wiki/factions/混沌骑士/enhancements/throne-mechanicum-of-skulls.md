25 分、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/lords-of-dread.md|Lords of Dread]] 分队。

## 效果

You can re-roll Charge rolls made for the bearer’s unit. In addition, once per battle, in your Charge phase, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of the phase, the bearer’s unit is eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

CHAOS KNIGHTS model only.
