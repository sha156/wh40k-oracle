25 分、[[factions/混沌骑士/detachments/traitoris-lance.md|Traitoris Lance]] 分队。

## 效果

At the end of your Command phase, select one objective marker the bearer is within range of and that you control. That objective marker remains under your control until your opponent’s Level of Control over that objective marker is greater than yours at the end of a phase. In addition, until you lose control of that objective marker, it has the Deathly Terror ability as if it were a CHAOS KNIGHTS model from your army.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制

CHAOS KNIGHTS model only.
