## 效果

In your Command phase, select one other Chaos Knights Character model within 9" of the bearer. If the bearer has one or more Aura abilities that affect friendly War Dog units, until the start of your next Command phase, the selected CHAOS KNIGHTS CHARACTER model is affected by those Aura abilities as well.

**分数**：25 分

## 携带限制
